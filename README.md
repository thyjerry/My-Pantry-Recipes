My Pantry Recipes helps you cook with what you already have. Add your ingredients and the app shows which of its 2,600+ original recipes you can make now, what you're missing, and which ingredient to buy next to unlock the most meals. 

It has 15 diet filters, plus general guides for renal, cardiac and diabetic eating. It also has country-based cuisines, metric units, EU allergen labels, and grocery lists with estimated costs. 

Everything runs on your device — no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. It ships as a web app and as two Android builds (a lightweight one for older Android devices and a modern one for newer phones).
