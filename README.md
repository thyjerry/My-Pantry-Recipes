# My Pantry Recipes

**Cook with what you already have.**

My Pantry Recipes is a free, open-source, offline-first recipe app for Android and the web. Add the ingredients in your pantry and it shows you which of its **3,700+ original recipes** you can make right now, what’s missing, and which single ingredient to buy next to unlock the most new meals.

Everything runs on your device — no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no Google Play Services required.

## Key Features

- **Pantry matching** — Instantly see what you can cook now and what’s one or two items away.
- **“Buy these next”** — Smart suggestions that prioritize the ingredient which unlocks the most recipes, with estimated cost and one-tap add to shopping list.
- **Shopping lists** with your own prices or regional averages.
- **3,700+ original recipes** covering mains, breakfast, sides, soups, baking, snacks, desserts, drinks, salads, and sauces. Includes carnivore, vegetarian, vegan, Instant Pot, air fryer, slow cooker, and 30-minute options.
- **15 diet filters** plus general guides for renal, cardiac, and diabetic eating.
- **Ingredient substitutions** with scaled quantities, diet/allergen conflict warnings, and pantry-aware matching.
- **Country-based cuisines** with support for both metric and imperial units, plus EU allergen labels.
- **Holiday & fasting calendar** (dozens of holidays) with optional reminders.
- **Baking tools** (baker’s percentages, pan converter, sourdough tracker, and more).
- **150+ preset color themes** plus the ability to customize colors to your liking.
- **Optional online features** (TheMealDB photos and recipes) that can be fully disabled. Personal data never leaves the device.
- **Barcode lookup** via Open Food Facts (manual entry or external scanner today; built-in camera scanner planned).

## Platforms

| Platform | File | Best for |
|----------|------|----------|
| **Android (Legacy)** | `MyPantryRecipes-v2.3-legacy.apk` | Older devices & Fire tablets (Android 5.0+) — lightweight build |
| **Android (Modern)** | `MyPantryRecipes-v2.3-modern.apk` | Newer phones & tablets (Android 8.0+) — themed icon & shortcuts |
| **Web / PWA** | Hosted or single-file HTML | Any browser — works offline after first load |

Both Android builds install over previous versions of the same build and keep all your pantry data, recipes, and settings. Legacy and Modern are separate apps (different package names).

## Privacy

- Fully offline by default.
- No accounts, analytics, trackers, or ads.
- Optional network calls only to TheMealDB (photos/recipes) and Open Food Facts (barcode lookup) when you explicitly use those features.
- Your pantry, favorites, and settings never leave the device.

## Getting Started

1. **Android**: Download the matching APK from the [Releases](https://github.com/thyjerry/My-Pantry-Recipes/releases) page and install it (enable “Install unknown apps” if prompted).
2. **Web**: Open the single-file HTML or host the web zip / `docs` folder with GitHub Pages.
3. Add ingredients to your Pantry and start cooking.
