# Garlic Ginger Bok Choy
Side | Chinese | 5 | 6 | 4 | Dinner, Side dish
- 500 g | bok choy, halved lengthways
- 1 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 3 cloves | garlic, sliced
- 1 tbsp | ginger, grated
- 1 tbsp | soy sauce
- 1 tsp | sesame oil
- 1 tsp | sesame seeds | opt
> Rinse the bok choy well, shaking off most of the water.
> Heat the oil in a wok or large pan over high heat. Add the garlic and ginger and stir for 30 seconds.
> Add the bok choy cut side down and cook for 2 minutes, then turn and add 2 tablespoons of water. Cover for 2 minutes until the stems are just tender.
> Toss with the soy sauce and sesame oil, scatter with sesame seeds and serve.

# Crispy Tempeh Rice Bowls
Main | International | 15 | 20 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 400 g | tempeh, cut into thin slices
- 3 tbsp | soy sauce
- 2 tbsp | maple syrup
- 1 tbsp | rice vinegar
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 300 g | rice
- 2 | carrots, cut into matchsticks
- 1 | cucumber, sliced
- 2 | green onions, sliced
- 1 tbsp | sriracha | opt
> Cook the rice following the packet instructions.
> Mix the soy sauce, maple syrup and rice vinegar in a small bowl.
> Fry the tempeh in the oil over medium-high heat for 4 minutes per side until golden. Pour in the sauce and let it bubble and coat the tempeh, about 1 minute.
> Fill bowls with rice and top with the tempeh, carrot, cucumber and green onions. Add sriracha if you like heat.

# Halloumi and Chickpea Traybake
Main | International | 10 | 30 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 250 g | halloumi, cut into cubes
- 400 g | chickpeas, drained
- 2 | bell peppers, cut into chunks
- 1 | red onion, cut into wedges
- 1 | zucchini, cut into chunks
- 3 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 tsp | smoked paprika
- 1 tsp | cumin
- 1 | lemon
- | parsley, chopped | opt
> Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan).
> Toss the chickpeas, peppers, onion and zucchini with the olive oil, paprika, cumin and a pinch of salt on a large tray. Roast for 20 minutes.
> Add the halloumi, stir and roast for 10 minutes more until the cheese is golden at the edges.
> Squeeze over the lemon and scatter with parsley.

# Gochujang Pork Lettuce Wraps
Main | Korean | 10 | 12 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 500 g | ground pork
- 2 tbsp | gochujang
- 1 tbsp | soy sauce
- 1 tbsp | honey
- 2 cloves | garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 1 | lettuce, leaves separated
- 2 | green onions, sliced
- 1 | cucumber, cut into sticks | opt
> Mix the gochujang, soy sauce and honey together.
> Heat the oil in a large pan over high heat. Add the pork and cook, breaking it up, for 6-8 minutes until browned.
> Add the garlic for 1 minute, then stir in the sauce and cook for 2 minutes until sticky.
> Spoon into lettuce leaves and top with green onions and cucumber.

# Black Bean Quesadillas
Main | Mexican | 10 | 10 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 4 | flour tortillas
- 400 g | black beans, drained and lightly mashed
- 150 g | monterey jack, grated
- 100 g | sweetcorn
- 1 | jalapeno, finely chopped
- 1 tsp | cumin
- 1 tbsp | vegetable oil
- | salsa, to serve | opt
- | sour cream, to serve | opt
> Mix the beans, corn, jalapeno and cumin with a pinch of salt.
> Spread the bean mix over half of each tortilla, top with cheese and fold over.
> Cook in a lightly oiled pan over medium heat for 2-3 minutes per side until crisp and the cheese melts.
> Cut into wedges and serve with salsa and sour cream.

# Miso Mushroom Udon
Main | Japanese | 10 | 12 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 400 g | udon noodles
- 250 g | shiitake mushrooms, sliced
- 2 tbsp | miso
- 1 tbsp | soy sauce
- 1 tbsp | mirin
- 1 l | vegetable stock
- 200 g | spinach
- 2 | green onions, sliced
- 1 tbsp | vegetable oil
> Fry the mushrooms in the oil over medium-high heat for 5 minutes until browned.
> Add the stock and bring to a simmer. Whisk the miso with a ladle of the hot stock and stir it back in with the soy sauce and mirin.
> Add the noodles and spinach and simmer for 2-3 minutes until hot and wilted.
> Serve in bowls topped with green onions.

# Coconut Chana Masala
Main | Indian | 10 | 25 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 800 g | chickpeas, drained
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 3 cloves | garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp | ginger, grated
- 400 g | canned tomatoes
- 200 ml | coconut milk
- 2 tsp | garam masala
- 1 tsp | turmeric
- 1 tsp | cumin
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- | cilantro, chopped | opt
- | naan, to serve | opt
> Fry the onion in the oil over medium heat for 8 minutes until soft and golden.
> Add the garlic, ginger and spices and stir for 1 minute.
> Add the tomatoes, chickpeas and coconut milk. Simmer for 15 minutes, stirring now and then, until thick.
> Season, scatter with cilantro and serve with rice or naan.

# Buttermilk Biscuits
Baking | American | 15 | 15 | 8 | Breakfast, Brunch, Dinner, Side dish, Baked goods
- 300 g | flour
- 1 tbsp | baking powder
- 0.5 tsp | baking soda
- 1 tsp | salt
- 1 tbsp | sugar
- 115 g | butter, cold and cubed
- 240 ml | buttermilk
> Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan) and line a tray with baking paper.
> Mix the flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt and sugar. Rub in the butter until it looks like coarse crumbs with a few bigger pieces.
> Stir in the buttermilk until just combined. Pat the dough out 2 cm thick, fold it over twice, and cut into 8 squares.
> Bake for 12-15 minutes until tall and golden.
