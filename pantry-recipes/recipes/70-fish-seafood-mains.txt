# Fish and Chips
Main | British | 20 | 30 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 4 | white fish fillets, such as cod or haddock
- 150 g | flour
- 1 tsp | baking powder
- 250 ml | sparkling water, ice-cold
- 1 kg | potatoes, cut into chips
- 1 l | vegetable oil, for frying
- 1 | lemon
- | salt
> Par-cook the chips in oil at 150 °C for 6 minutes and drain.
> Whisk the flour, baking powder, salt and sparkling water to a batter, dip the floured fish and fry at 180 °C for 6 to 7 minutes until golden; drain.
> Fry the chips again at 180 °C until crisp, salt and serve with lemon; never leave hot oil unattended.

- 3 tbsp | butter
- 3 tbsp | flour

- 2 | eggs, hard-boiled
- 1 kg | potatoes
- 50 g | cheddar, grated
- | salt

# Mussels in White Wine (Moules Marinieres)
Main | French | 15 | 10 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 2 kg | mussels, scrubbed
- 2 | shallots, chopped
- 3 cloves | garlic
- 200 ml | dry white wine
- 3 tbsp | butter
- 4 tbsp | heavy cream
- 1 handful | parsley
- 1 | baguette
> Discard any mussels that stay open when tapped.
> Cook the shallots and garlic in the butter for 3 minutes, add the wine and bring to the boil.
> Add the mussels, cover and steam 4 to 5 minutes, shaking, until opened (discard any that stay shut); stir in the cream and parsley and serve with bread.

# Seafood Paella
Main | Spanish | 20 | 40 | 6 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 350 g | paella rice
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 1 | red bell pepper, diced
- 3 cloves | garlic
- 1 tsp | smoked paprika
- 1 pinch | saffron
- 2 | tomatoes, grated
- 1 l | fish stock
- 300 g | shrimp, shell-on
- 500 g | mussels, scrubbed
- 200 g | squid rings
- 150 g | peas
- 4 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | lemon
- | salt
> Cook the onion and pepper in the oil in a paella pan for 6 minutes, add the garlic, paprika and tomato for 3 minutes.
> Stir in the rice, then the hot stock and saffron; spread out and simmer without stirring for 10 minutes.
> Arrange the seafood and peas on top and cook 8 to 10 minutes until the rice is done and the mussels open.
> Rest 5 minutes and serve with lemon.

# Bouillabaisse-Style Fish Stew
Main | French | 25 | 45 | 6 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 1 | leek, sliced
- 1 | fennel bulb, sliced
- 4 cloves | garlic
- 2 | tomatoes, chopped
- 1 pinch | saffron
- 1 | orange, zested
- 1.5 l | fish stock
- 800 g | mixed white fish fillets, chunked
- 300 g | mussels
- 4 tbsp | olive oil
- 6 slices | baguette, toasted
- 4 tbsp | garlic aioli
> Cook the onion, leek and fennel in the oil for 10 minutes, add the garlic, tomatoes, saffron and orange zest.
> Add the stock and simmer 20 minutes.
> Add the fish and mussels and simmer 6 to 8 minutes; serve with toasts spread with aioli.
