# Air Fryer Chicken Wings
Snack | American | 15 | 27 | 4 | Appetizer, Snack
- 1 kg | chicken wings
- 1 tbsp | baking powder
- 1 tsp | garlic powder
- 1 tsp | smoked paprika
- 4 tbsp | hot sauce
- 2 tbsp | butter, melted
- | salt
> Preheat the air fryer to 400 °F (205 °C) for 3 minutes.
> Pat the wings very dry and toss with the baking powder, garlic powder, paprika and salt.
> Arrange in a single layer in the basket without crowding (cook in batches if needed) and air fry for 24 minutes, turning them halfway. Smaller air fryers may need a few extra minutes.
> Toss in the hot sauce mixed with butter.

# Air Fryer French Fries
Side | American | 15 | 23 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Side dish
- 4 | potatoes, cut in thin sticks
- 1 tbsp | vegetable oil
- | salt
> Preheat the air fryer to 380 °F (195 °C) for 3 minutes.
> Soak the potato sticks in cold water for 30 minutes, then dry very well and toss with the oil.
> Arrange in a single layer in the basket without crowding (cook in batches if needed) and air fry for 20 minutes, shaking the basket every 5 minutes. Smaller air fryers may need a few extra minutes.
> Season with salt straight away.

# Air Fryer Chicken Thighs
Main | American | 15 | 25 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 8 | chicken thighs, bone-in, skin-on
- 1 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 tsp | garlic powder
- 1 tsp | paprika
- 1 tsp | dried thyme
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Preheat the air fryer to 380 °F (195 °C) for 3 minutes.
> Pat the chicken dry and rub with oil, spices and seasoning.
> Arrange in a single layer in the basket without crowding (cook in batches if needed) and air fry for 22 minutes, skin-side down for 10 minutes, then skin-side up. Smaller air fryers may need a few extra minutes.
> Check they read 165 °F (74 °C) at the bone and rest 5 minutes.

# Air Fryer Quesadillas
Main | Mexican | 15 | 9 | 2 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 2 | flour tortillas
- 100 g | cheddar, grated
- 100 g | chicken, cooked and shredded
- 2 tbsp | salsa
- | cooking spray
> Preheat the air fryer to 375 °F (190 °C) for 3 minutes.
> Fill half of each tortilla with cheese, chicken and salsa, fold, and spray the outside.
> Arrange in a single layer in the basket without crowding (cook in batches if needed) and air fry for 6 minutes, turning halfway; hold the top down with a toothpick so it doesn't fly up. Smaller air fryers may need a few extra minutes.
> Cut into wedges.
