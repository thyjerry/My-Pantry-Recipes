# Mediterranean Orzo Salad
Salad | Greek | 15 | 9 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Salad
- 250 g | orzo
- 1 | cucumber, diced
- 200 g | cherry tomatoes, halved
- 80 g | olives, halved
- 100 g | feta, crumbled
- 0.5 | red onion, finely chopped
- 4 tbsp | olive oil
- 2 tbsp | red wine vinegar
- 1 tsp | dried oregano
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Cook the orzo in salted boiling water for about 9 minutes until tender, then drain and rinse under cold water.
> Whisk together the olive oil, vinegar and oregano with salt and pepper.
> Toss the cooled orzo with everything else and the dressing.
> Taste for seasoning. It keeps well in the fridge for 2 to 3 days, so it is good for packed lunches.
