# Apple Pie Overnight Oats
Breakfast | International | 5 | 0 | 2 | Breakfast
- 100 g | rolled oats
- 250 ml | milk, or plant milk
- 2 tbsp | yogurt
- 1 tbsp | chia seeds
- 1 tbsp | maple syrup
- 1 | apple, grated
- 1 tsp | cinnamon
- 2 tbsp | walnuts
> Stir the oats, milk, yogurt, chia and maple syrup together in two jars.
> Mix in the apple, cinnamon and walnuts, keeping a little fruit for the top.
> Cover and refrigerate overnight, or for at least 4 hours.
> Stir, loosen with a splash of milk and eat cold or warmed.

# Spinach Feta Frittata
Breakfast | American | 10 | 15 | 4 | Breakfast, Brunch
- 8 | eggs
- 150 g | spinach
- 100 g | feta
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Heat the oven grill. Cook the onion in the oil in an ovenproof pan for 5 minutes, add the spinach until wilted.
> Beat the eggs with salt and pepper, pour in and scatter with feta.
> Cook over medium-low heat for 6 minutes until the edges set.
> Grill for 3 to 4 minutes until puffed and set.

# Denver Omelette
Breakfast | American | 10 | 15 | 2 | Breakfast, Brunch
- 3 | eggs
- 50 g | ham, diced
- 0.5 | green bell pepper, diced
- 0.25 | onion, diced
- 40 g | cheddar, grated
- 1 tbsp | butter
- | salt
> Cook the ham, pepper and onion in the butter for 4 minutes and set aside.

> Add the filling and cheese to one half, fold and slide onto a plate.

# Eggs Benedict
Breakfast | American | 10 | 15 | 4 | Breakfast, Brunch
- 7 | eggs, 4 to poach and 3 yolks for the sauce
- 2 | english muffins, split and toasted
- 4 slices | canadian bacon
- 120 g | butter, melted
- 1 | lemon
- 1 tbsp | white vinegar
- | salt
> Whisk the yolks with 1 tbsp lemon juice over barely simmering water until thick, then slowly whisk in the melted butter for hollandaise; season.
> Warm the bacon.
> Poach the eggs in simmering water with the vinegar for 3 minutes.
> Stack muffin, bacon and egg and spoon over the hollandaise.

# Sweet Potato Breakfast Hash
Breakfast | American | 10 | 15 | 4 | Breakfast, Brunch
- 2 | sweet potatoes, diced
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 1 | red bell pepper, diced
- 1 tsp | smoked paprika
- 4 | eggs
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 handful | cilantro
- | salt
> Fry the sweet potato in the oil, covered, for 10 minutes.
> Add the onion, pepper and paprika and cook uncovered for 8 minutes until browned.
> Make four wells, crack in the eggs, cover and cook 5 minutes.
> Season and scatter with cilantro.

# Japanese Tamagoyaki
Breakfast | Japanese | 10 | 15 | 4 | Breakfast, Brunch
- 4 | eggs
- 1 tbsp | sugar
- 1 tsp | soy sauce
- 1 tbsp | mirin
- 1 tbsp | vegetable oil
> Beat the eggs with the sugar, soy and mirin.
> Oil a small pan and pour in a thin layer; when nearly set roll it to one end.
> Add another layer, lifting the roll so egg flows under, and roll again; repeat.
> Rest the roll for 2 minutes and slice.

# Full English Breakfast
Breakfast | British | 10 | 15 | 4 | Breakfast, Brunch
- 4 | pork sausages
- 4 slices | bacon
- 2 | tomatoes, halved
- 200 g | mushrooms
- 1 can | baked beans
- 4 | eggs
- 4 slices | bread
- 1 tbsp | vegetable oil
> Fry the sausages for 12 to 15 minutes, adding the bacon for the last 6.
> Fry the tomatoes and mushrooms in the fat.
> Warm the beans and fry the eggs.
> Toast or fry the bread and serve everything together.
