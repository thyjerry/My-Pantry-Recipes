# Parmesan Roasted Zucchini
Side | Italian | 10 | 20 | 4 | Side dish
- 4 | zucchini, in thick batons
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 tsp | garlic powder
- 1 tsp | italian seasoning
- | salt
- | black pepper
- 30 g | parmesan, grated
> Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan, 425°F).
> Toss the vegetables with the oil, garlic powder and Italian seasoning, salt and pepper and spread out on a baking tray in one layer, with space between the pieces.
> Roast for about 20 minutes, turning halfway, until tender and browned at the edges.
> To finish, sprinkle with parmesan for the last 5 minutes.
