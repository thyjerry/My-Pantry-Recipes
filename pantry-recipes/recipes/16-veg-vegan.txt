# Chana Masala
Main | Indian | 10 | 25 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 800 g | chickpeas, drained
- 1 | onion, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp | ginger garlic paste
- 1 | chili, chopped
- 400 g | canned tomatoes
- 2 tsp | garam masala
- 1 tsp | cumin
- 1 tsp | coriander
- 0.5 tsp | turmeric
- 3 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 1 | lemon
- 3 tbsp | cilantro, chopped
> Fry the onion in the oil for 8 minutes until golden. Add the paste, chili and spices for 1 minute.
> Add the tomatoes and cook 5 minutes, then the chickpeas and 200 ml water. Simmer 15 minutes, mashing a few chickpeas to thicken.
> Finish with lemon juice and cilantro.

# Palak Paneer
Main | Indian | 10 | 20 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 400 g | paneer, cubed
- 500 g | spinach
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 2 tbsp | ginger garlic paste
- 1 | chili
- 1 tsp | garam masala
- 1 tsp | cumin
- 2 tbsp | ghee
- 100 ml | heavy cream
> Wilt the spinach in a splash of water, then blend with the chili until smooth.
> Fry the paneer in half the ghee until golden and set aside.
> Fry the onion in the rest of the ghee for 6 minutes, add the paste and spices for 1 minute, then the spinach and a pinch of salt. Simmer 5 minutes.
> Stir in the cream and paneer and warm through.
