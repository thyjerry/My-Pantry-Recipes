# Shrimp Po' Boy
Main | Cajun | 20 | 10 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 500 g | shrimp, peeled
- 100 g | cornmeal
- 50 g | flour
- 2 tsp | cajun seasoning
- 120 ml | buttermilk
- 4 | bread rolls, long sub
- 1 | lettuce, shredded
- 2 | tomatoes, sliced
- 120 g | remoulade sauce
- 500 ml | vegetable oil, for frying
> Soak the shrimp in the buttermilk for 10 minutes.
> Mix the cornmeal, flour and seasoning and toss the shrimp in it.
> Shallow-fry in hot oil for 2 minutes until golden and drain.
> Spread the rolls with remoulade and fill with lettuce, tomato and shrimp.

# Chicken Gyros with Tzatziki
Main | Greek | 20 | 15 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 600 g | chicken thighs, boneless
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | lemon
- 2 cloves | garlic
- 1 tsp | oregano
- 1 tsp | paprika
- 4 | pita breads
- 200 g | tzatziki
- 1 | tomato, sliced
- 0.5 | red onion, sliced
- | salt
> Marinate the chicken in the oil, lemon, garlic, oregano, paprika and salt for 30 minutes.
> Cook in a hot frying pan for 5 to 6 minutes per side and slice.
> Warm the pitas and fill with chicken, tomato, onion and tzatziki.

# Muffuletta Sandwich
Main | Cajun | 20 | 0 | 6 | Lunch, Main dish
- 1 | bread loaf, round, about 23 cm
- 200 g | olives, chopped with pickled vegetables as olive salad
- 100 g | salami, sliced
- 100 g | ham, sliced
- 100 g | mortadella, sliced
- 100 g | provolone, sliced
> Split the loaf and pull out a little of the soft crumb.
> Spread both halves with the olive salad and its oil.
> Layer the meats and cheese, close, wrap tightly and press under a weight for 1 hour before cutting into wedges.

# Cheese and Black Bean Arepas
Main | Venezuelan | 20 | 20 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 250 g | masarepa, precooked cornmeal
- 350 ml | water, warm
- 1 tsp | salt
- 1 can | black beans
- 120 g | mozzarella, grated
- 1 | avocado
- 1 tbsp | vegetable oil
> Mix the masarepa, water and salt to a soft dough and rest 5 minutes.
> Shape 8 discs 1.5 cm thick and cook in an oiled frying pan over medium heat for 5 to 6 minutes per side.
> Split each pocket and fill with warm beans, cheese and avocado.

# Pupusas de Queso
Main | Salvadoran | 30 | 20 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 300 g | masa harina
- 400 ml | water, warm
- 200 g | mozzarella, grated
- 1 tsp | salt
- 300 g | cabbage, shredded and quickly pickled as curtido
- 120 ml | salsa roja
- 1 tbsp | vegetable oil
> Knead the masa, water and salt to a smooth dough.
> Shape 8 balls, press a hollow, fill with cheese, seal and flatten to 1 cm discs.
> Cook on a lightly oiled griddle over medium heat for 4 minutes per side.
> Serve with curtido and salsa.

# Pork Katsu Sando
Main | Japanese | 15 | 10 | 2 | Lunch, Main dish
- 2 | pork loin cutlets
- 1 | egg
- 50 g | panko
- 2 tbsp | flour
- 4 slices | bread, soft milk bread
- 3 tbsp | tonkatsu sauce
- 100 g | cabbage, finely shredded
- 1 tbsp | mayonnaise
- 4 tbsp | vegetable oil
> Pound the cutlets thin, dust with flour, dip in egg and coat in panko.
> Fry in the oil for 3 to 4 minutes per side until cooked through.
> Spread the bread with mayonnaise, add cabbage, the katsu and sauce, close and trim the crusts.

# Monte Cristo Sandwich
Main | American | 10 | 10 | 2 | Brunch, Lunch, Main dish
- 4 slices | bread
- 100 g | ham, sliced
- 100 g | turkey, sliced
- 60 g | swiss cheese, sliced
- 2 | eggs
- 60 ml | milk
- 1 tbsp | butter
- 2 tbsp | raspberry jam
- 1 tsp | powdered sugar
> Make two sandwiches with the ham, turkey and cheese.
> Dip them in the egg beaten with the milk.
> Fry in the butter over medium heat for 3 minutes per side until golden and the cheese melts.
> Dust with powdered sugar and serve with jam.

# Smashed Burger Sliders
Main | American | 15 | 10 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 500 g | ground beef
- 8 | slider buns
- 8 slices | american cheese
- 1 | onion, very thinly sliced
- 3 tbsp | mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp | ketchup
- 8 slices | dill pickles
- | salt
> Roll the beef into 8 balls.
> Heat a heavy frying pan very hot, add a ball with a little onion and smash flat with a spatula; season.

# Philly-Style Mushroom Cheesesteak
Main | American | 10 | 15 | 2 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 400 g | mushrooms, sliced
- 1 | onion, sliced
- 1 | green bell pepper, sliced
- 4 slices | provolone
- 2 | bread rolls, long sub
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 1 tsp | worcestershire sauce
- | salt
> Cook the onion and pepper in the oil over medium-high heat for 6 minutes.
> Add the mushrooms, worcestershire and salt and cook until browned.
> Pile into two portions, lay the cheese on top to melt and scoop into the toasted rolls; use a vegetarian worcestershire if you need it meat-free.
