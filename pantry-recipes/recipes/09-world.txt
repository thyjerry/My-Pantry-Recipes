# Easy Pad Thai
Main | Thai | 15 | 10 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 250 g | rice noodles
- 3 tbsp | fish sauce
- 2 tbsp | brown sugar
- 1 tbsp | tomato paste
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 300 g | shrimp
- 2 | eggs
- 2 clove | garlic
- 150 g | bean sprouts | opt
- 4 | green onions
- 3 tbsp | peanuts
- 2 | limes, one juiced, one in wedges
> Soak the noodles in hot water until just soft, then drain.
> Mix the fish sauce, sugar, the juice of one lime and the tomato paste.
> Stir-fry the garlic and shrimp in the oil for 2 minutes. Push aside, scramble the eggs in the space.
> Add the noodles and sauce and toss for 2 minutes. Add the bean sprouts and green onions.
> Serve topped with chopped peanuts and lime wedges.

# Hearty Beef Stew
Main | British | 20 | 150 | 6 | Dinner, Main dish
- 1 kg | stewing beef, cubed
- 3 tbsp | flour
- 3 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 2 | onions
- 3 | carrots
- 2 stalk | celery
- 2 tbsp | tomato paste
- 1 l | beef stock
- 2 | bay leaves
- 1 tsp | thyme
- 500 g | potatoes
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Toss the beef in seasoned flour and brown in batches in the oil. Set aside.
> Cook the chopped onion, carrot and celery for 8 minutes. Stir in the tomato paste.
> Return the beef, add the stock, bay leaves and thyme. Cover and simmer gently for 1 hour 30 minutes.
> Add the potatoes in chunks and cook 45 minutes more, until the beef is tender. Season.

# Coconut Fish Curry
Main | Indian | 10 | 20 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 600 g | white fish, in chunks
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 1 | onion
- 2 clove | garlic
- 1 tbsp | ginger
- 2 tsp | curry powder
- 0.5 tsp | turmeric
- 400 ml | coconut milk
- 200 g | cherry tomatoes
- 1 | lime
- 1 handful | cilantro
- | salt
> Cook the sliced onion in the oil for 6 minutes. Add the garlic, ginger and spices for 1 minute.
> Pour in the coconut milk and tomatoes and simmer for 8 minutes.
> Add the fish and simmer gently for 5 to 6 minutes until it flakes.
> Season, squeeze over the lime and scatter with cilantro. Serve with rice.

# Lemon Chicken Orzo
Main | Greek | 10 | 25 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 500 g | chicken breast, diced
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | onion
- 2 clove | garlic
- 300 g | orzo
- 900 ml | chicken stock
- 1 | lemon
- 100 g | spinach
- 50 g | feta | opt
- 1 tsp | oregano
- | salt
> Brown the chicken in the oil, season and remove.
> Cook the chopped onion for 5 minutes, add the garlic and oregano, then the orzo for 1 minute.
> Add the stock, return the chicken and simmer for 10 to 12 minutes, stirring, until the orzo is tender.
> Stir in the spinach, lemon zest and juice. Crumble over the feta.

# Korean-Style Beef Rice Bowls
Main | Korean | 10 | 10 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 500 g | ground beef
- 3 tbsp | soy sauce
- 2 tbsp | brown sugar
- 1 tbsp | sesame oil
- 2 clove | garlic
- 1 tbsp | ginger
- 0.5 tsp | chili flakes
- 600 g | cooked rice
- 1 | cucumber
- 3 | green onions
- 1 tsp | sesame seeds | opt
> Brown the beef in a hot pan, breaking it up.
> Add the garlic and ginger for 1 minute, then the soy sauce, sugar, sesame oil and chili. Cook 2 minutes until glossy.
> Serve over rice with sliced cucumber, green onions and sesame seeds.

# Baked Potatoes with Chili Beans
Main | British | 10 | 60 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 4 | potatoes, large
- 1 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | onion
- 400 g | kidney beans
- 400 g | canned tomatoes
- 1 tsp | chili powder
- 1 tsp | cumin
- 80 g | cheddar | opt
- | salt
> Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Prick the potatoes, rub with a little oil and salt and bake for 1 hour until crisp outside and soft inside.
> Meanwhile cook the chopped onion in the rest of the oil for 6 minutes. Add the spices, beans and tomatoes and simmer for 15 minutes.
> Split the potatoes, spoon in the beans and top with cheese.

# Egg Fried Noodles
Main | Chinese | 5 | 10 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 300 g | noodles
- 3 | eggs
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 4 | green onions
- 150 g | cabbage, shredded
- 3 tbsp | soy sauce
- 1 tsp | sesame oil
> Cook the noodles, drain and toss with the sesame oil.
> Scramble the eggs in half the oil and remove.
> Stir-fry the cabbage and green onions in the rest of the oil for 2 minutes. Add the noodles and soy sauce and toss.
> Fold the eggs back through and serve.

# Mediterranean Baked Cod
Main | International | 10 | 20 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 4 | cod fillets
- 300 g | cherry tomatoes
- 80 g | olives
- 2 clove | garlic
- 3 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | lemon
- 1 handful | parsley
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F).
> Put the tomatoes, olives and sliced garlic in a baking dish with the oil, toss and roast for 8 minutes.
> Nestle in the fish, season, add lemon slices and bake 12 minutes more until the fish flakes.
> Scatter with parsley.
