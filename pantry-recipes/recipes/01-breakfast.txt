# Fluffy Pancakes
Breakfast | American | 10 | 15 | 4 | Breakfast, Brunch
- 200 g | flour
- 2 tsp | baking powder
- 1 tbsp | sugar
- 1 pinch | salt
- 300 ml | milk
- 2 | eggs
- 2 tbsp | butter, melted, plus extra for the pan
- | maple syrup, to serve | opt
> Whisk the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt in a large bowl.
> In a jug, beat the milk, eggs and melted butter together. Pour into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined; a few lumps are fine.
> Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat and wipe with a little butter.
> Pour in about 3 tablespoons of batter per pancake. Cook for 2 minutes until bubbles appear, flip and cook 1 minute more.
> Keep warm in a low oven while you cook the rest. Serve with maple syrup or fruit.

# Classic French Toast
Breakfast | French | 5 | 10 | 4 | Breakfast, Brunch
- 3 | eggs
- 150 ml | milk
- 1 tsp | cinnamon
- 1 tsp | vanilla extract
- 8 slice | bread
- 2 tbsp | butter
- | maple syrup, to serve | opt
> Whisk the eggs, milk, cinnamon and vanilla in a shallow dish.
> Melt a little butter in a frying pan over medium heat.
> Dip each slice of bread in the egg mixture for a few seconds per side.
> Fry for 2 to 3 minutes per side until golden. Repeat with the remaining bread, adding butter as needed.
> Serve hot with maple syrup or fresh fruit.

# Cheesy Scrambled Eggs on Toast
Breakfast | British | 5 | 5 | 4 | Breakfast, Brunch
- 8 | eggs
- 4 tbsp | milk
- 1 tbsp | butter
- 60 g | cheddar, grated
- 4 slice | bread
- | salt
- | black pepper
- 1 tbsp | chives, snipped | opt
> Beat the eggs with the milk and a pinch of salt and pepper.
> Toast the bread.
> Melt the butter in a non-stick pan over low heat. Add the eggs and stir slowly with a spatula.
> When the eggs are almost set but still glossy, take off the heat and fold in the cheese.
> Spoon onto the toast and scatter with chives.

# Vegetable Omelette
Breakfast | French | 10 | 10 | 2 | Breakfast, Brunch, Lunch
- 4 | eggs
- 1 tbsp | butter
- 1 | bell pepper, diced
- 2 | green onions, sliced
- 4 | mushrooms, sliced
- 40 g | cheddar, grated | opt
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Beat the eggs with a pinch of salt and pepper.
> Melt half the butter in a frying pan and cook the pepper, green onions and mushrooms for 4 minutes until soft. Tip onto a plate.
> Add the rest of the butter, pour in the eggs and swirl. Cook gently, pulling the edges in, until nearly set.
> Scatter the vegetables and cheese over one half, fold the omelette over and slide onto a plate. Cut in half to serve two.

# Banana Oat Porridge
Breakfast | International | 2 | 8 | 4 | Breakfast, Brunch
- 200 g | oats
- 800 ml | milk
- 2 | bananas
- 1 pinch | salt
- 2 tbsp | maple syrup
- 1 tsp | cinnamon
> Put the oats, milk and salt in a saucepan. Mash one banana and stir it in.
> Bring to a simmer and cook for 5 to 8 minutes, stirring often, until creamy.
> Stir in the cinnamon and maple syrup.
> Slice the second banana and spoon it over the bowls.

# Shakshuka
Breakfast | Tunisian | 10 | 20 | 4 | Breakfast, Brunch, Lunch, Dinner
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 1 | bell pepper, sliced
- 3 clove | garlic, crushed
- 1 tsp | cumin
- 1 tsp | paprika
- 800 g | canned tomatoes
- 6 | eggs
- | salt
- | black pepper
- 2 tbsp | parsley, chopped | opt
- 50 g | feta | opt
> Heat the oil in a large deep frying pan. Cook the onion and pepper for 8 minutes until soft.
> Add the garlic, cumin and paprika and cook for 1 minute.
> Pour in the tomatoes, season and simmer for 10 minutes until thickened.
> Make six wells in the sauce and crack an egg into each. Cover and cook on low for 6 to 8 minutes until the whites are set.
> Scatter with parsley and crumbled feta. Serve with bread.

# Huevos Rancheros
Breakfast | Mexican | 10 | 15 | 4 | Breakfast, Brunch, Lunch, Dinner
- 1 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 1 | chili, chopped
- 400 g | canned tomatoes
- 400 g | black beans, drained
- 4 | eggs
- 4 | tortillas
- 1 | avocado, sliced
- | salt
- 2 tbsp | cilantro | opt
> Heat the oil in a pan and soften the onion and chili for 5 minutes.
> Add the tomatoes and beans, season and simmer for 8 minutes.
> Meanwhile fry the eggs in a separate pan and warm the tortillas.
> Spoon the bean sauce onto the tortillas, top each with an egg and avocado slices, and scatter with cilantro.

# Breakfast Burritos

- 6 | eggs

- 1 tbsp | butter
- 1 | bell pepper, diced
- 80 g | cheddar, grated
- 4 tbsp | salsa
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Beat the eggs with salt and pepper.

> Warm the tortillas. Divide the eggs between them, top with cheese and salsa and roll up tightly.

# Tofu Scramble
Breakfast | International | 5 | 10 | 4 | Breakfast, Brunch, Lunch
- 400 g | firm tofu
- 1 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | onion, finely chopped
- 1 | bell pepper, diced
- 0.5 tsp | turmeric
- 0.5 tsp | cumin
- 100 g | spinach
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Pat the tofu dry and crumble it with your fingers.
> Heat the oil in a frying pan and cook the onion and pepper for 5 minutes.
> Add the tofu, turmeric and cumin and cook for 4 minutes, stirring.
> Stir in the spinach until wilted. Season well and serve with toast.
