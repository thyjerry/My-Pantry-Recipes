# Ginger Pork with Snow Peas
Main | Chinese | 15 | 12 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 400 g | pork tenderloin, thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp | cornstarch
- 200 g | snow peas
- 1 | red bell pepper, sliced
- 3 tbsp | soy sauce
- 1 tbsp | rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp | honey
- 2 tbsp | ginger, grated
- 300 g | long-grain rice, cooked
> Toss the pork with the cornstarch and a pinch of salt. Mix the sauce ingredients with 3 tbsp water.
> Heat a wok or large frying pan over high heat until smoking, add 1 tbsp oil and stir-fry the pork in a single layer for 3 to 4 minutes until browned and just cooked, then tip out.
> Add the rest of the oil and stir-fry the vegetables for 2 to 3 minutes until crisp-tender.
> Return the pork, pour in the sauce and toss for 1 minute until glossy.
> Serve straight away with hot rice.
