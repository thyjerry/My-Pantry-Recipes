# Garlic Roasted Potatoes
Side | International | 10 | 40 | 4 | Dinner, Side dish
- 1 kg | potatoes, in chunks
- 3 tbsp | olive oil
- 4 clove | garlic
- 1 tsp | rosemary
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Heat the oven to 220°C (425°F).
> Toss the potatoes with the oil, smashed garlic, rosemary, salt and pepper on a large tray.
> Roast for 35 to 40 minutes, turning once, until crisp and golden.

# Creamy Mashed Potatoes
Side | International | 10 | 20 | 4 | Dinner, Side dish
- 1 kg | potatoes
- 50 g | butter
- 150 ml | milk
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Peel and chop the potatoes and boil in salted water for 15 to 20 minutes until very tender.
> Drain well and let steam for 2 minutes.
> Mash with the butter and warm milk until smooth. Season to taste.

# Roasted Broccoli with Lemon
Side | International | 5 | 20 | 4 | Dinner, Side dish
- 1 | broccoli, in florets
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 2 clove | garlic
- 1 | lemon
- | salt
- 20 g | parmesan | opt
> Heat the oven to 220°C (425°F).
> Toss the broccoli with the oil, sliced garlic and salt and roast for 18 to 20 minutes until charred at the edges.
> Squeeze over lemon and grate over parmesan.

# Honey Glazed Carrots
Side | International | 5 | 25 | 4 | Dinner, Side dish
- 600 g | carrots
- 1 tbsp | butter
- 1 tbsp | honey
- 1 tsp | thyme
- | salt
> Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Halve the carrots lengthwise.
> Toss with melted butter, honey, thyme and salt and roast for 25 minutes until tender and glazed.

# Coconut Rice
Side | Thai | 5 | 20 | 4 | Dinner, Side dish
- 300 g | rice
- 400 ml | coconut milk
- 200 ml | water
- 0.5 tsp | salt
- 1 tsp | sugar
> Rinse the rice until the water runs clear.
> Put everything in a saucepan, bring to the boil, then cover and cook on the lowest heat for 15 minutes.
> Rest off the heat for 5 minutes and fluff with a fork.

# Spanish Tomato Rice
Side | Spanish | 5 | 25 | 4 | Dinner, Side dish
- 1 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | onion
- 2 clove | garlic
- 1 tsp | paprika
- 250 g | rice
- 200 g | canned tomatoes
- 500 ml | vegetable stock
- | salt
> Cook the chopped onion in the oil for 5 minutes. Add the garlic, paprika and rice and stir for 1 minute.
> Add the tomatoes and stock, cover and simmer gently for 18 minutes until the liquid is absorbed. Season.

# Garlic Bread
Side | Italian | 5 | 12 | 4 | Dinner, Side dish, Appetizer
- 1 | baguette
- 80 g | butter, soft
- 3 clove | garlic, crushed
- 2 tbsp | parsley
- | salt
> Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F).
> Mix the butter, garlic, parsley and a pinch of salt.
> Slice the baguette without cutting all the way through and spread the butter between the slices.
> Wrap in foil and bake for 10 minutes, then open the foil for 2 minutes to crisp.

# Classic Hummus
Snack | Levantine | 10 | 0 | 6 | Appetizer, Snack
- 400 g | chickpeas, drained
- 3 tbsp | tahini
- 1 | lemon
- 1 clove | garlic
- 3 tbsp | olive oil
- 0.5 tsp | cumin
- | salt
- 1 pinch | paprika | opt
> Blend the chickpeas, tahini, lemon juice, garlic, cumin and salt with 3 tablespoons cold water until very smooth.
> With the motor running, add 2 tablespoons oil.
> Spoon into a bowl, drizzle with the remaining oil and dust with paprika.

# Bruschetta
Snack | Italian | 10 | 5 | 4 | Appetizer, Snack
- 1 | baguette
- 4 | tomatoes, diced
- 1 clove | garlic
- 3 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 handful | basil
- | salt
> Mix the tomatoes with 2 tablespoons oil, torn basil and salt.
> Slice and toast the baguette. Rub each slice with the cut garlic clove and drizzle with the rest of the oil.
> Spoon the tomatoes on top and serve straight away.

# Deviled Eggs
Snack | American | 15 | 10 | 4 | Appetizer, Snack
- 6 | eggs
- 3 tbsp | mayonnaise
- 1 tsp | dijon mustard
- 1 pinch | paprika
- | salt
- 1 tbsp | chives | opt
> Boil the eggs for 10 minutes, then cool in cold water and peel.
> Halve and scoop the yolks into a bowl. Mash with the mayonnaise, mustard and salt.
> Spoon back into the whites and dust with paprika and chives.

# Crispy Roasted Chickpeas
Snack | International | 5 | 30 | 4 | Snack
- 800 g | chickpeas, drained
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 tsp | paprika
- 0.5 tsp | cumin
- | salt
> Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Pat the chickpeas very dry.
> Toss with the oil, spices and salt and spread on a tray.
> Roast for 25 to 30 minutes, shaking halfway, until crunchy. They crisp more as they cool.

# Loaded Nachos
Snack | Mexican | 10 | 10 | 4 | Appetizer, Snack
- 200 g | tortilla chips
- 400 g | black beans, drained
- 150 g | cheddar
- 4 tbsp | salsa
- 1 | avocado
- 4 tbsp | sour cream | opt
- 1 | chili | opt
> Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F).
> Spread the chips on a tray, scatter with beans and cheese.
> Bake for 8 to 10 minutes until melted.
> Top with salsa, diced avocado, sour cream and sliced chili.

# Spring Rolls in Lettuce Cups
Snack | Vietnamese | 15 | 5 | 4 | Appetizer, Snack
- 100 g | rice noodles
- 1 | lettuce
- 1 | carrot, in matchsticks
- 1 | cucumber, in matchsticks
- 1 handful | mint
- 1 handful | cilantro
- 2 tbsp | fish sauce
- 1 | lime
- 1 tsp | sugar
- 1 | chili | opt
> Soak or cook the noodles as the packet says, rinse in cold water and drain.
> Mix the fish sauce, lime juice, sugar, chili and 2 tablespoons water for a dipping sauce.
> Fill lettuce leaves with noodles, carrot, cucumber and herbs, roll up and dip in the sauce.
