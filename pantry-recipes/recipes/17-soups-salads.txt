# Split Pea Soup with Ham
Soup | American | 10 | 75 | 6 | Lunch, Dinner, Soup
- 450 g | split peas, rinsed
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 2 | carrots, diced
- 2 | celery stalks, diced
- 250 g | ham, diced
- 2 l | chicken stock
- 2 | bay leaves
- 1 tbsp | butter
> Soften the onion, carrots and celery in the butter for 6 minutes.
> Add the split peas, stock and bay leaves, bring to a boil and simmer, partly covered, for 60-70 minutes, stirring now and then, until the peas break down.
> Stir in the ham and simmer 5 minutes. Remove the bay leaves and season.
