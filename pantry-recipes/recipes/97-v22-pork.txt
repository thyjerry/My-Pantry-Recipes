# Thai Holy Basil Pork Tenderloin Stir-Fry
Main | Thai | 15 | 12 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 700 g | pork tenderloin, trimmed
- 2 tbsp | oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp | fish sauce
- 1 tsp | sugar
- 1 large handful | basil leaves (Thai holy basil if you can find it)
- 1 | onion, sliced
- 4 | garlic cloves, chopped
- 2 | red chilies, sliced
- 1 tsp | cornstarch
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
> Slice the pork tenderloin into thin strips (if not already), toss with the cornstarch and a pinch of salt and leave while you prep.
> For the sauce, stir together the oyster sauce, fish sauce, sugar and 3 tbsp water.
> Heat the oil in a wok or large frying pan over high heat until shimmering. Stir-fry the pork tenderloin in two batches, about 3 minutes per batch, until 63 °C (145 °F) in the centre; tip onto a plate.
> Stir-fry the onion with the garlic and chilies for 2 to 3 minutes.
> Return the pork tenderloin with any juices, pour in the sauce and toss for 1 minute until glossy and hot. Turn off the heat and stir in the basil until it wilts.
> Serve with jasmine rice.

# Thai Massaman Pork Shoulder Curry
Main | Thai | 20 | 147 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 1.2 kg | pork shoulder, boneless, cut into 5 cm pieces
- 3 tbsp | massaman curry paste
- 400 ml | coconut milk
- 2 | potatoes, cubed
- 1 | onion, cut into wedges
- 50 g | peanuts, roasted
- 1 tbsp | fish sauce
- 1 tbsp | brown sugar
- 1 | lime, juiced
- 4 | garlic cloves, chopped
- 2 | red chilies, sliced
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
> Pat the pork shoulder dry and season well with salt and the curry paste.
> Heat 2 tbsp oil in a heavy pot over medium-high heat and brown the pork shoulder in batches, about 12 minutes in total; set aside.
> Lower the heat and cook the garlic and chilies for 6 to 8 minutes until soft.
> Add the coconut milk, potatoes, onion and 300 ml water, scrape up the browned bits and return the meat. Cover and simmer gently for about 2 hours (or bake covered at 160 °C), until the pork is tender enough to pull apart.
> Stir in the fish sauce, sugar and lime juice and scatter with peanuts. Taste for salt.
> Serve with jasmine rice.

# British Honey Mustard Pork Chops Traybake
Main | British | 15 | 18 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 4 | pork chops, boneless, about 2 cm thick
- 2 tbsp | wholegrain mustard
- 2 tbsp | honey
- 600 g | new potatoes, halved
- 3 | parsnips, in batons
- 2 sprigs | thyme
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 2 | garlic cloves
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
> Mix the mustard, honey, thyme and oil with 2 tbsp oil and 1 tsp salt. Coat the pork chops and leave to marinate 30 minutes or up to 8 hours in the fridge.
> Heat a grill, barbecue or ridged grill pan to medium-high. Spread on a tray with the potatoes and parsnips (start the vegetables 15 minutes earlier).
> Grill about 5 minutes per side, turning once, until 63 °C (145 °F) in the centre.
> Rest 5 minutes. Spoon over the sticky juices.
> Serve with mashed potatoes and greens.

# British Peppercorn Pork Chops
Main | British | 10 | 16 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 4 | pork chops, boneless, about 2 cm thick
- 2 tsp | black pepper, coarsely crushed
- 150 ml | double cream
- 1 tsp | dijon mustard
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 2 | garlic cloves
- 1 tbsp | vegetable oil
> Pat the pork chops dry and season with salt and the crushed peppercorns.
> Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat. Cook 4 to 5 minutes per side, until 63 °C (145 °F) in the centre. Rest on a warm plate.
> Lower the heat and, in the same pan, cook the onion and garlic for 1 minute, then the cream and mustard; bubble 2 minutes.
> Return the pork chops with its resting juices and turn in the sauce. Spoon over and serve with chips or salad.
> Serve with mashed potatoes and greens.
