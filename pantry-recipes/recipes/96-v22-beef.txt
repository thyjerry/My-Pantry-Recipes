# Thai Holy Basil Beef Stir-Fry
Main | Thai | 15 | 10 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 500 g | beef sirloin, thinly sliced against the grain
- 2 tbsp | oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp | fish sauce
- 1 tsp | sugar
- 1 large handful | basil leaves (Thai holy basil if you can find it)
- 1 | onion, sliced
- 4 | garlic cloves, chopped
- 2 | red chilies, sliced
- 1 tsp | cornstarch
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
> Slice the beef into thin strips (if not already), toss with the cornstarch and a pinch of salt and leave while you prep.
> For the sauce, stir together the oyster sauce, fish sauce, sugar and 3 tbsp water.
> Heat the oil in a wok or large frying pan over high heat until shimmering. Stir-fry the beef in two batches, about 2 minutes per batch, until browned outside but still a little pink inside; tip onto a plate.
> Stir-fry the onion with the garlic and chilies for 2 to 3 minutes.
> Return the beef with any juices, pour in the sauce and toss for 1 minute until glossy and hot. Turn off the heat and stir in the basil until it wilts.
> Serve with jasmine rice.

# Thai Massaman Beef Curry
Main | Thai | 20 | 177 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 1.2 kg | beef chuck, cut into 4 cm pieces
- 3 tbsp | massaman curry paste
- 400 ml | coconut milk
- 2 | potatoes, cubed
- 1 | onion, cut into wedges
- 50 g | peanuts, roasted
- 1 tbsp | fish sauce
- 1 tbsp | brown sugar
- 1 | lime, juiced
- 4 | garlic cloves, chopped
- 2 | red chilies, sliced
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
> Pat the beef dry and season well with salt and the curry paste.
> Heat 2 tbsp oil in a heavy pot over medium-high heat and brown the beef in batches, about 12 minutes in total; set aside.
> Lower the heat and cook the garlic and chilies for 6 to 8 minutes until soft.
> Add the coconut milk, potatoes, onion and 300 ml water, scrape up the browned bits and return the meat. Cover and simmer gently for about 2½ hours (or bake covered at 160 °C), until the beef is very tender.
> Stir in the fish sauce, sugar and lime juice and scatter with peanuts. Taste for salt.
> Serve with jasmine rice.

- 1.2 kg | beef chuck, cut into 4 cm pieces
- 400 g | tomato sauce
- 2 | potatoes, in chunks
- 2 | carrots, in chunks
- 1 | red bell pepper, in chunks
- 2 tbsp | liver pate (or peanut butter)
- 50 g | green olives
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 6 | garlic cloves, crushed
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
> Pat the beef dry and season well with salt and black pepper.
> Heat 2 tbsp oil in a heavy pot over medium-high heat and brown the beef in batches, about 12 minutes in total; set aside.
> Lower the heat and cook the onion and garlic for 6 to 8 minutes until soft.
> Add the tomato sauce and 300 ml water, adding the potatoes and carrots for the last 30 minutes and the pepper for the last 10, scrape up the browned bits and return the meat. Cover and simmer gently for about 2½ hours (or bake covered at 160 °C), until the beef is very tender.
> Stir in the pate and olives. Taste for salt.
> Serve with steamed rice.

- 6 | garlic cloves, crushed
