# Dark Chocolate Mousse
Dessert | French | 20 | 5 | 6 | Dessert
- 200 g | dark chocolate
- 4 | eggs, separated
- 2 tbsp | sugar
- 200 ml | heavy cream
- 1 pinch | salt
> Melt the chocolate and cool slightly, then stir in the yolks.
> Whip the cream to soft peaks and fold in.
> Whisk the whites with the salt and sugar to soft peaks and fold in gently.
> Spoon into glasses and chill 3 hours; it contains raw egg.

# Classic Pavlova
Dessert | Australian | 20 | 90 | 8 | Dessert
- 4 | egg whites
- 220 g | sugar
- 1 tsp | white vinegar
- 2 tsp | cornstarch
- 300 ml | heavy cream
- 300 g | mixed berries
- 2 | passion fruits
> Heat the oven to 120 °C (250 °F).
> Whisk the whites to stiff peaks, then add the sugar a spoon at a time until glossy; fold in the vinegar and cornstarch.
> Spread into a 20 cm round on parchment and bake 1 1/4 to 1 1/2 hours; cool in the oven.
> Top with whipped cream and fruit just before serving.

# Key Lime Bars

- 2 tbsp | sugar
- 1 can | sweetened condensed milk
- 3 | egg yolks

> Heat the oven to 175 °C (350 °F).
