# Black Pepper Chicken Stir-Fry
Main | Chinese | 10 | 12 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 500 g | chicken breasts, thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 1 tsp | cornstarch | opt
- 2 tbsp | oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp | soy sauce
- 1.5 tsp | black pepper, coarsely ground
- 1 | onion, sliced
- 1 | green bell pepper, sliced
- 300 g | rice, to serve
> Start the rice. Mix the sauce ingredients (sauces, sweetener, citrus or nut butter) with 3 tbsp water.
> Heat the oil in a wok or large pan over high heat and stir-fry the chicken for 6 minutes until browned and just cooked; lift out.
> Stir-fry the vegetables, garlic, ginger or chili for 2 to 3 minutes until crisp-tender.
> Return the chicken, pour in the sauce and toss for a minute until glossy. Add any herbs and seeds and serve over rice.

# Lemon Garlic Chicken Skillet with Spinach
Main | Italian | 10 | 18 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 4 | chicken breasts, thin cut
- 3 | garlic cloves
- 1 | lemon
- 150 ml | chicken stock
- 150 g | spinach
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 30 g | butter
> Season and fry the chicken in the oil 5 to 6 minutes per side until 75 °C; rest.
> Fry the garlic for 30 seconds, add the stock, lemon juice and butter and bubble 2 minutes.
> Wilt the spinach in the sauce and spoon over the chicken.
