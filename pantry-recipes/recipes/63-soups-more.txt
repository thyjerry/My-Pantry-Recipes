# Cream of Mushroom Soup
Soup | French | 15 | 25 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Soup
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | onion
- 2 cloves | garlic
- 1 tsp | dried thyme
- 2 tbsp | flour
- 500 g | mushrooms, sliced
- 750 ml | chicken stock
- 150 ml | heavy cream
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Heat 2 tbsp oil (or the butter) in a large pot and cook the aromatics, chopped, over medium heat for 5 minutes until soft (stir in any flour or spices for the last minute).
> Add the vegetables and the liquid, bring to the boil, then simmer for about 15 minutes until everything is tender.
> Blend until smooth, then stir in 150 ml|heavy cream and heat gently without boiling.
> Season with salt and pepper and serve with chives.

# Avgolemono (Greek Lemon Chicken Soup)
Soup | Greek | 15 | 30 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Soup
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | onion
- 1 stalk | celery
- 100 g | long-grain rice
- 1.5 l | chicken stock
- 300 g | chicken, cooked and shredded
- 3 | eggs
- 2 | lemons
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Heat 2 tbsp oil (or the butter) in a large pot and cook the aromatics, chopped, over medium heat for 5 minutes until soft (stir in any flour or spices for the last minute).
> Add the vegetables and the liquid, bring to the boil, then simmer for about 20 minutes until everything is tender.
> Add the remaining ingredients (whisk any egg or egg-lemon mixture with a ladle of hot broth first, and stir in cornstarch mixed with a little water) and simmer until cooked through.
> Season with salt and pepper and serve with dill.
