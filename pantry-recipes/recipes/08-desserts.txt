# Banana Bread
Baking | American | 15 | 55 | 8 | Breakfast, Brunch, Snack, Baked goods
- 3 | bananas, very ripe
- 75 g | butter, melted
- 100 g | brown sugar
- 1 | egg
- 1 tsp | vanilla extract
- 1 tsp | baking soda
- 1 pinch | salt
- 190 g | flour
- 50 g | walnuts | opt
> Heat the oven to 175°C (350°F) and line a loaf tin.
> Mash the bananas, then stir in the butter, sugar, egg and vanilla.
> Fold in the baking soda, salt, flour and walnuts until just combined.
> Pour into the tin and bake for 50 to 60 minutes until a skewer comes out clean. Cool before slicing.

# Chocolate Chip Cookies
Baking | American | 15 | 12 | 8 | Snack, Dessert, Baked goods
- 115 g | butter, soft
- 100 g | brown sugar
- 50 g | sugar
- 1 | egg
- 1 tsp | vanilla extract
- 180 g | flour
- 0.5 tsp | baking soda
- 0.25 tsp | salt
- 150 g | chocolate, chopped
> Heat the oven to 180°C (350°F) and line two trays.
> Beat the butter and both sugars until creamy. Beat in the egg and vanilla.
> Stir in the flour, baking soda and salt, then the chocolate.
> Roll into 16 balls, space well apart and bake for 10 to 12 minutes until golden at the edges. Cool on the tray.

# Apple Crumble
Dessert | British | 15 | 35 | 6 | Dessert
- 6 | apples
- 2 tbsp | sugar
- 1 tsp | cinnamon
- 150 g | flour
- 100 g | butter, cold
- 75 g | brown sugar
- 50 g | oats
> Heat the oven to 190°C (375°F).
> Peel and slice the apples and toss with the sugar and cinnamon in a baking dish.
> Rub the butter into the flour until it looks like breadcrumbs, then stir in the brown sugar and oats.
> Scatter over the apples and bake for 35 minutes until golden and bubbling.

# Chocolate Mug Cake
Dessert | American | 3 | 2 | 1 | Dessert
- 4 tbsp | flour
- 3 tbsp | sugar
- 2 tbsp | cocoa powder
- 0.25 tsp | baking powder
- 3 tbsp | milk
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 1 tbsp | chocolate | opt
> Mix the flour, sugar, cocoa and baking powder in a large mug.
> Stir in the milk and oil until smooth, then push in the chocolate.
> Microwave on high for 60 to 90 seconds until risen but still slightly gooey. Cool for a minute.

# Vanilla Cupcakes
Baking | American | 15 | 20 | 6 | Dessert, Baked goods
- 125 g | butter, soft
- 125 g | sugar
- 2 | eggs
- 1 tsp | vanilla extract
- 125 g | self raising flour
- 2 tbsp | milk
- 150 g | powdered sugar
- 75 g | butter, for frosting
> Heat the oven to 180°C (350°F) and line a muffin tin with 12 cases.
> Beat the butter and sugar until pale. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the vanilla.
> Fold in the flour and milk. Divide between the cases and bake for 18 to 20 minutes. Cool.
> Beat the frosting butter with the powdered sugar and a splash of milk until fluffy and pipe or spread on top.

# Blueberry Muffins
Baking | American | 10 | 22 | 6 | Breakfast, Brunch, Snack, Baked goods
- 250 g | flour
- 2 tsp | baking powder
- 120 g | sugar
- 1 pinch | salt
- 1 | egg
- 200 ml | milk
- 80 ml | vegetable oil
- 150 g | blueberries
> Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F) and line a muffin tin.
> Mix the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt.
> Whisk the egg, milk and oil, then stir into the dry mix until just combined. Fold in the blueberries.
> Fill the cases and bake for 20 to 22 minutes until risen and golden.

# Baked Cinnamon Apples
Dessert | American | 10 | 30 | 4 | Dessert
- 4 | apples
- 2 tbsp | butter
- 2 tbsp | brown sugar
- 1 tsp | cinnamon
- 40 g | oats
- 2 tbsp | raisins | opt
> Heat the oven to 180°C (350°F). Core the apples and stand them in a small dish.
> Mix the butter, sugar, cinnamon, oats and raisins and stuff into the apples.
> Add a splash of water to the dish and bake for 30 minutes until tender.

# Simple Pancake Crepes
Dessert | French | 5 | 20 | 4 | Breakfast, Brunch, Dessert
- 125 g | flour
- 2 | eggs
- 300 ml | milk
- 1 pinch | salt
- 1 tbsp | butter
- 1 | lemon
- 2 tbsp | sugar
> Whisk the flour, eggs, milk and salt to a smooth thin batter. Rest for 10 minutes if you can.
> Heat a non-stick pan and wipe with butter. Pour in a thin layer of batter, swirling to coat.
> Cook for 1 minute until set, flip and cook 30 seconds.
> Serve sprinkled with sugar and a squeeze of lemon.
