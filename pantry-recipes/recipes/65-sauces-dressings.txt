# Honey Mustard Dressing
Sauce | American | 5 | 0 | 8 | Sauce/Condiment
- 4 tbsp | mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp | dijon mustard
- 2 tbsp | honey
- 1 tbsp | lemon juice
- | salt
> Whisk everything together until smooth.
> Thin with a little water if you want to drizzle it.

# Creamy Ranch Dressing
Sauce | American | 10 | 0 | 8 | Sauce/Condiment
- 120 ml | buttermilk
- 4 tbsp | mayonnaise
- 4 tbsp | sour cream
- 1 tbsp | dill
- 1 tbsp | chives
- 0.5 tsp | garlic powder
- 0.5 tsp | onion powder
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Whisk the buttermilk, mayonnaise and sour cream.
> Stir in the herbs, garlic and onion powder and season.
> Chill for 30 minutes so the flavours develop.

# Caesar Dressing
Sauce | American | 10 | 0 | 6 | Sauce/Condiment
- 1 | egg yolk
- 2 | anchovy fillets
- 1 clove | garlic
- 1 | lemon
- 1 tsp | dijon mustard
- 120 ml | olive oil
- 30 g | parmesan, grated
- | black pepper
> Mash the anchovies and garlic to a paste.
> Whisk in the yolk, lemon juice and mustard, then the oil in a thin stream.
> Stir in the parmesan and pepper; use within 2 days because of the raw yolk.

# Balsamic Glaze
Sauce | Italian | 2 | 15 | 8 | Sauce/Condiment
- 250 ml | balsamic vinegar
- 1 tbsp | brown sugar
- 1 pinch | salt
> Simmer the vinegar and sugar in a small pan for 12 to 15 minutes until reduced by about half.
> It thickens more as it cools; stop when it coats a spoon.
> Cool and store in a jar.

# Chimichurri Verde
Sauce | Argentine | 10 | 0 | 8 | Sauce/Condiment
- 1 large handful | parsley, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp | dried oregano
- 4 cloves | garlic, minced
- 0.5 tsp | red pepper flakes
- 3 tbsp | red wine vinegar
- 120 ml | olive oil
- | salt
> Mix the parsley, oregano, garlic and pepper flakes.
> Stir in the vinegar, oil and salt.
> Rest for 20 minutes before spooning over grilled meat or vegetables.

# Peanut Satay Sauce

- 4 tbsp | peanut butter
- 200 ml | coconut milk
- 1 tbsp | soy sauce
- 1 tbsp | brown sugar
- 1 | lime
- 1 tsp | red curry paste
> Warm everything in a small pan over low heat, whisking, until smooth.

# Tzatziki Sauce
Sauce | Greek | 10 | 0 | 8 | Sauce/Condiment
- 1 | cucumber, grated and squeezed
- 300 g | Greek yogurt
- 1 clove | garlic, grated
- 1 tbsp | dill
- 1 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | lemon
- | salt
> Squeeze the grated cucumber dry in a towel.

# Teriyaki Sauce
Sauce | Japanese | 2 | 5 | 8 | Sauce/Condiment
- 120 ml | soy sauce
- 60 ml | mirin
- 2 tbsp | sugar
- 1 tsp | ginger, grated
- 1 tbsp | cornstarch
> Simmer the soy sauce, mirin, sugar and ginger for 2 minutes.
> Stir in the cornstarch mixed with 2 tbsp water and simmer until glossy.
> Cool; keeps 2 weeks chilled.

# Enchilada Sauce
Sauce | Mexican | 5 | 15 | 8 | Sauce/Condiment
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 2 tbsp | flour
- 3 tbsp | chili powder
- 1 tsp | cumin
- 0.5 tsp | garlic powder
- 2 tbsp | tomato paste
- 500 ml | chicken stock
- | salt
> Cook the flour in the oil for 1 minute, then the spices for 30 seconds.
> Whisk in the tomato paste and stock.
> Simmer 10 minutes until slightly thick and season.

# Bechamel Sauce
Sauce | French | 2 | 10 | 6 | Sauce/Condiment
- 3 tbsp | butter
- 3 tbsp | flour
- 500 ml | milk
- 0.25 tsp | nutmeg
- | salt
> Melt the butter, stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute.
> Whisk in the milk gradually and simmer, whisking, for 5 minutes until thick.
> Season with nutmeg and salt.

# Chili Crisp Oil
Sauce | Chinese | 5 | 15 | 12 | Sauce/Condiment
- 200 ml | vegetable oil
- 4 tbsp | chili flakes
- 4 | shallots, thinly sliced
- 6 cloves | garlic, thinly sliced
- 1 tsp | sichuan peppercorns, ground
- 1 tbsp | sesame seeds
- 1 tsp | sugar
- 1 tsp | salt
> Fry the shallots in the oil over medium heat until golden, about 8 minutes, then the garlic for 2 minutes.
> Put the chili flakes, peppercorns, sesame, sugar and salt in a heatproof bowl.
> Pour the hot oil and crispy bits over, stir and cool; keep the jar refrigerated.

# Blender Hot Sauce
Sauce | International | 10 | 10 | 12 | Sauce/Condiment
- 10 | red chilies, stemmed
- 1 | carrot, chopped
- 3 cloves | garlic
- 200 ml | distilled vinegar
- 1 tsp | salt
- 1 tsp | sugar
> Simmer the chilies, carrot and garlic in the vinegar for 10 minutes.
> Blend until very smooth with the salt and sugar.
> Pour into a clean bottle and refrigerate; wear gloves when handling hot chilies.

- 1 tbsp | garlic powder
- 1 tbsp | onion powder
- 1 tbsp | salt
- 2 tsp | black pepper
