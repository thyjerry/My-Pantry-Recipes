# Roasted Cauliflower and Turmeric Soup
Soup | Indian | 10 | 35 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Soup
- 1 | cauliflower
- 1 | onion
- 1 tsp | turmeric
- 1 tsp | cumin
- 400 ml | coconut milk
- 500 ml | vegetable stock
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
> Roast the cauliflower and onion with the oil and spices at 210 °C for 25 minutes.
> Simmer with the stock and coconut milk 10 minutes and blend.

# Creamy Chicken and Wild Rice Soup
Soup | American | 15 | 45 | 6 | Lunch, Dinner, Soup
- 600 g | chicken thighs
- 150 g | wild rice
- 1 | onion
- 2 | carrots
- 2 | celery stalks
- 1.5 l | chicken stock
- 200 ml | heavy cream
- 30 g | butter
> Soften the vegetables in the butter.
> Add the stock, rice and chicken and simmer 40 minutes; shred the chicken.
> Stir in the cream.

# Roasted Carrot and Ginger Soup
Soup | International | 10 | 35 | 4 | Lunch, Soup
- 800 g | carrots
- 1 | onion
- 3 cm | ginger
- 1 l | vegetable stock
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 4 tbsp | plain yogurt
> Roast the carrots and onion in the oil at 210 °C for 25 minutes.
> Simmer with the ginger and stock 10 minutes and blend.
> Swirl with yogurt.

# Thai Pumpkin Coconut Soup
Soup | Thai | 10 | 25 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Soup
- 1 kg | pumpkin, cubed
- 2 tbsp | red curry paste
- 400 ml | coconut milk
- 500 ml | vegetable stock
- 1 | lime
- 1 handful | coriander
> Fry the paste in a splash of coconut milk.
> Add the pumpkin, the rest of the milk and stock and simmer 20 minutes.
> Blend and finish with lime and coriander.

# Greek Chicken Avgolemono Soup
Soup | Greek | 10 | 30 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Soup
- 1.5 l | chicken stock
- 100 g | orzo
- 300 g | chicken, cooked and shredded
- 3 | eggs
- 2 | lemons
- 1 handful | dill
> Simmer the orzo in the stock 10 minutes and add the chicken.
> Whisk the eggs with the lemon juice and slowly whisk in a ladle of hot stock.
> Stir the mixture into the pot off the heat until silky; add dill.

# Charred Corn and Tomato Salad
Salad | American | 10 | 8 | 4 | Side dish, Salad
- 4 | corn cobs
- 300 g | cherry tomatoes
- 1 | red onion
- 100 g | feta
- 1 handful | basil
- 1 | lime
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
> Char the corn in a hot pan or grill and cut off the kernels.
> Toss with halved tomatoes, sliced onion, lime and oil.
> Top with feta and basil.

# Crunchy Asian Slaw with Peanuts
Salad | Chinese | 15 | 0 | 4 | Side dish, Salad
- 300 g | red cabbage
- 2 | carrots
- 4 | spring onions
- 50 g | peanuts
- 2 tbsp | rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp | soy sauce
- 1 tbsp | sesame oil
- 1 tbsp | honey
> Shred the cabbage and carrots finely.
> Whisk the vinegar, soy, oil and honey.
> Toss and top with spring onions and peanuts.

# Greek Yogurt Honey Walnut Bowls
Dessert | Greek | 5 | 3 | 2 | Snack, Dessert
- 400 g | greek yogurt
- 3 tbsp | honey
- 60 g | walnuts
- 1 pinch | cinnamon
> Toast the walnuts.
> Spoon the yogurt into bowls and top with walnuts, honey and cinnamon.

# No-Bake Chocolate Peanut Butter Bars
Dessert | American | 15 | 0 | 16 | Snack, Dessert
- 200 g | digestive biscuits, crushed
- 100 g | butter, melted
- 200 g | peanut butter
- 100 g | powdered sugar
- 200 g | milk chocolate
> Mix the crumbs, butter, peanut butter and sugar and press into a lined 20 cm tin.
> Melt the chocolate and spread over.
> Chill 2 hours and cut into 16.

# Baked Rice Pudding with Nutmeg
Dessert | British | 5 | 120 | 6 | Dessert
- 100 g | arborio rice (or pudding rice)
- 1 l | milk
- 60 g | sugar
- 25 g | butter
- 0.5 tsp | nutmeg
> Put the rice, milk and sugar in a buttered dish and dot with butter.
> Grate over the nutmeg.
> Bake at 150 °C for 2 hours, stirring once after 30 minutes.
