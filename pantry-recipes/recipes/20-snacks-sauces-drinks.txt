# Baba Ganoush
Snack | Levantine | 10 | 40 | 6 | Appetizer, Snack
- 2 | eggplants
- 3 tbsp | tahini
- 1 | lemon
- 1 clove | garlic, grated
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 tbsp | parsley, chopped
> Prick the eggplants and roast at 220°C (200°C fan), or char over a flame, for 35-40 minutes until collapsed.
> Scoop out the flesh, drain for 10 minutes and mash with the tahini, lemon juice, garlic and salt.
> Drizzle with oil and sprinkle with parsley.
