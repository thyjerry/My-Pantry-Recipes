# Roman Egg Drop Soup (Stracciatella)
Soup | Italian | 5 | 10 | 4 | Lunch, Soup
- 1.2 l | chicken stock
- 3 | eggs
- 40 g | parmesan, grated
- 2 tbsp | semolina
- 1 pinch | nutmeg
- 1 handful | parsley
- 100 g | spinach
> Bring the stock to a simmer and add the spinach.
> Beat the eggs, parmesan, semolina and nutmeg.
> Pour into the simmering stock while stirring gently so it forms soft strands; cook 1 minute and add parsley.

# Scotch Broth
Soup | Scottish | 20 | 100 | 6 | Dinner, Soup
- 500 g | lamb neck, or shoulder
- 80 g | pearl barley
- 60 g | dried split peas, soaked
- 2 | carrots, diced
- 1 | onion, diced
- 1 | leek, sliced
- 150 g | rutabaga, diced
- 2 l | water
- 1 handful | parsley
- | salt
> Simmer the lamb, barley and peas in the water for 1 hour, skimming.
> Add the vegetables and simmer 40 minutes more.
> Lift out the lamb, shred the meat back in and season; scatter with parsley.

# Colombian Chicken and Potato Soup (Ajiaco)
Soup | Colombian | 20 | 60 | 6 | Dinner, Soup
- 1 kg | chicken pieces
- 1 kg | potatoes, half floury and sliced, half waxy and cubed
- 3 | corn cobs, in pieces
- 2 l | chicken stock
- 1 bunch | cilantro
- 120 ml | heavy cream
- 2 tbsp | capers
- 1 | avocado
- | salt
> Simmer the chicken in the stock with the cilantro stems for 30 minutes, then shred it.
> Add the potatoes and corn and simmer 30 minutes so some potatoes break down to thicken.
> Return the chicken.
> Serve with cream, capers and avocado.

# Ecuadorian Potato Cheese Soup (Locro de Papa)
Soup | Ecuadorian | 15 | 30 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Soup
- 1 kg | potatoes, cubed
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 2 cloves | garlic
- 1 tsp | cumin
- 1 tsp | achiote, or paprika
- 1 l | vegetable stock
- 250 ml | milk
- 150 g | queso fresco
- 1 | avocado
- 2 tbsp | butter
- 1 handful | cilantro
> Soften the onion and garlic in the butter with the cumin and achiote.
> Add the potatoes and stock and simmer 20 minutes, mashing some to thicken.
> Stir in the milk and most of the cheese and heat gently.
> Top with avocado, cilantro and more cheese.

# Peruvian Chicken Cilantro Soup (Aguadito de Pollo)
Soup | Peruvian | 15 | 45 | 6 | Dinner, Soup
- 800 g | chicken thighs
- 2 bunches | cilantro
- 1 | onion
- 3 cloves | garlic
- 2 tbsp | aji amarillo paste
- 150 g | long-grain rice
- 1 | red bell pepper, diced
- 150 g | frozen peas
- 2 | potatoes, diced
- 1.5 l | chicken stock
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 1 | lime
> Blend the cilantro with a little stock.
> Brown the chicken in the oil, add the onion, garlic and aji and cook 5 minutes.
> Add the stock, rice and potatoes and simmer 25 minutes.
> Add the cilantro puree, pepper and peas and cook 5 minutes; serve with lime.

# Greek White Bean Soup (Fasolada)
Soup | Greek | 15 | 90 | 6 | Lunch, Dinner, Soup
- 500 g | dried white beans, soaked overnight
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 2 | carrots, sliced
- 2 | celery stalks, sliced
- 2 tbsp | tomato paste
- 1 can | canned tomatoes
- 100 ml | olive oil
- 1 tsp | oregano
- 2 | bay leaves
- 1 | lemon
- | salt
> Simmer the drained beans in fresh water for 30 minutes and drain.
> Soften the vegetables in half the oil, add the tomato paste and tomatoes.
> Add the beans, bay, oregano and water to cover by 3 cm and simmer 1 hour until creamy.
> Stir in the rest of the oil and season; serve with lemon.

- 1 | potato, cubed
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 200 g | firm tofu, cubed
- 100 g | mushrooms
- 2 cloves | garlic

- 1 | green onion

- 1 tsp | black pepper, crushed
- 1 tsp | cumin seeds
- 3 cloves | garlic, crushed
- 0.25 tsp | turmeric
- 1 tsp | rasam powder, opt
- 1 tsp | mustard seeds
- 10 | curry leaves
- 1 tbsp | ghee
- 800 ml | water
- 1 handful | cilantro
- | salt

# Korean Rice Cake Soup (Tteokguk)
Soup | Korean | 15 | 25 | 4 | Dinner, Soup
- 500 g | korean rice cakes, sliced ovals
- 200 g | beef, thinly sliced
- 1.5 l | beef stock
- 2 cloves | garlic
- 2 tbsp | soy sauce
- 1 tbsp | sesame oil
- 2 | eggs, beaten
- 2 | green onions
- 1 sheet | nori, crumbled
> Soak the rice cakes in water for 20 minutes.
> Brown the beef in the sesame oil with the garlic, add the stock and soy sauce and simmer 15 minutes.

# Spanish Garlic Soup (Sopa de Ajo)
Soup | Spanish | 10 | 20 | 4 | Lunch, Soup
- 8 cloves | garlic, sliced
- 4 tbsp | olive oil
- 150 g | day-old bread, sliced
- 2 tsp | smoked paprika
- 1.2 l | chicken stock
- 4 | eggs
- 2 slices | serrano ham, chopped, opt
> Fry the garlic gently in the oil until golden, add the bread and toast it in the oil.
> Stir in the paprika and pour in the stock; simmer 10 minutes.
> Crack in the eggs and poach 3 to 4 minutes.
> Scatter with ham.

- 150 g | day-old bread
- 1 clove | garlic

- 1 tbsp | sherry vinegar
- 2 | eggs, hard-boiled
- 50 g | serrano ham
- | salt
> Blend the tomatoes, bread, garlic, vinegar and salt until very smooth.

# Matzo Ball Soup
Soup | Jewish | 20 | 60 | 6 | Dinner, Soup
- 100 g | matzo meal
- 3 | eggs
- 3 tbsp | vegetable oil, or schmaltz
- 3 tbsp | chicken stock
- 2 | carrots, sliced
- 2 | celery stalks, sliced
- 300 g | cooked chicken, shredded
- 1 handful | dill
- | salt
> Mix the matzo meal, eggs, fat, 3 tbsp stock and salt and chill 30 minutes.
> Roll into 12 balls with wet hands and simmer in salted water, covered, for 30 to 40 minutes.
> Simmer the carrots and celery in the stock for 15 minutes, add the chicken and matzo balls and finish with dill.

- 1 | onion, sliced

# Indonesian Chicken Soup (Soto Ayam)
Soup | Indonesian | 20 | 45 | 6 | Dinner, Soup
- 1 kg | chicken pieces
- 4 | shallots
- 4 cloves | garlic
- 1 tbsp | ginger
- 1 tsp | turmeric
- 4 | candlenuts, or macadamias
- 2 | lemongrass stalks
- 4 | kaffir lime leaves
- 2 l | water
- 200 g | rice vermicelli
- 100 g | bean sprouts
- 3 | eggs, hard-boiled
- 2 tbsp | fried shallots
- 1 | lime
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- | salt
> Blend the shallots, garlic, ginger, turmeric and nuts to a paste and fry in the oil for 5 minutes.
> Add the chicken, lemongrass, lime leaves, water and salt and simmer 40 minutes.
> Shred the chicken.
> Serve the broth over noodles, sprouts, chicken and halved eggs with fried shallots and lime.
