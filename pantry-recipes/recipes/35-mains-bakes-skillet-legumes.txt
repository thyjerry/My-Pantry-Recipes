# Chicken and Broccoli Rice Casserole
Main | American | 15 | 45 | 6 | Dinner, Main dish
- 250 g | rice
- 600 ml | chicken stock
- 500 g | chicken breast, diced
- 300 g | broccoli, in small florets
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 200 ml | milk
- 150 g | cheddar, grated
- 1 tsp | garlic powder
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Heat the oven to 190°C (375°F) and grease a 23 x 33 cm baking dish.
> Stir the rice, stock, milk, onion, garlic powder, 1 tsp salt and pepper together in the dish, then scatter over the chicken.
> Cover tightly with foil and bake 35 minutes.
> Stir in the broccoli and half the cheese, top with the rest and bake uncovered 10 to 15 minutes until the rice is tender and the top is golden.

# Beef Enchilada Casserole
Main | Tex-Mex | 15 | 30 | 6 | Dinner, Main dish
- 500 g | ground beef
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 1 can | black beans, drained
- 2 tbsp | taco seasoning
- 400 ml | enchilada sauce
- 8 | corn tortillas, torn
- 200 g | cheddar, grated
- 4 tbsp | sour cream
> Heat the oven to 190°C (375°F).
> Brown the beef and onion, then stir in the beans, taco seasoning and half the enchilada sauce.

# Lemon Asparagus Risotto
Main | Italian | 10 | 30 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish

- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 tbsp | butter
- 100 ml | white wine

- | salt
- | black pepper
- 250 g | asparagus, in short lengths

- 1 | onion, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 tbsp | butter
- 100 ml | white wine

- | salt
- | black pepper
- 1 pinch | saffron
