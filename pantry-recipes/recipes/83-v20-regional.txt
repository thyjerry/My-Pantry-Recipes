# Danish Rye Open Sandwich with Egg and Shrimp
Main | Danish | 15 | 10 | 2 | Lunch, Main dish
- 2 slices | rye bread
- 1 tbsp | butter
- 2 | eggs
- 100 g | cooked shrimp
- 2 tbsp | mayonnaise
- 1 tsp | lemon juice
- 1 handful | fresh dill
> Hard-boil the eggs for 9 minutes, cool in cold water, peel and slice.

> Mix the mayonnaise with the lemon juice. Lay the egg slices on the bread, pile the shrimp on top, add a spoon of the lemon mayonnaise and finish with dill.
