# Sheet Pan Pesto Chicken and Vegetables
Main | Italian | 15 | 32 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 4 | chicken breasts
- 1 | zucchini, in chunks
- 1 | red bell pepper, in chunks
- 1 | red onion, wedged
- 200 g | cherry tomatoes
- 4 tbsp | pesto
- 3 tbsp | olive oil
- | salt
- | black pepper
- 30 g | parmesan, grated
> Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan, 425°F) and line a large baking tray.
> Toss the vegetables with 2 tbsp oil, salt and pepper on the tray and roast for 10 minutes.
> Rub the chicken breasts with half the pesto and a little oil, then nestle it among the vegetables. Roast 22 minutes more, until it is cooked through and the vegetables are tender.
> To finish, spoon over the rest of the pesto and scatter with parmesan.

# Slow Cooker Beef Ragu with Rigatoni
Main | Italian | 25 | 480 | 6 | Dinner, Main dish
- 1 kg | beef chuck, in large pieces
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 2 | carrots, diced
- 3 cloves | garlic
- 2 can | canned tomatoes
- 2 tbsp | tomato paste
- 150 ml | red wine
- 1 tsp | dried oregano
- 400 g | rigatoni
- 40 g | parmesan, grated
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Season the beef and brown it all over in a hot pan with a little oil.
> Put the beef, onion, carrots, garlic, tomatoes, tomato paste, wine and oregano in the slow cooker, season with salt and pepper and stir.
> Cover and cook on LOW for 8 hours or on HIGH for 4 hours, until the meat is falling apart.
