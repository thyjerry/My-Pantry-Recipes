# Arnold Palmer (Half Tea Half Lemonade)
Drink | American | 10 | 5 | 4 | Drink
- 4 | black tea bags
- 500 ml | water, boiling
- 4 | lemons
- 80 g | sugar
- 8 | ice cubes
> Steep the tea in the boiling water for 5 minutes, remove and cool.
> Stir the lemon juice and sugar into the cold water until dissolved.
> Mix the tea and lemonade and serve over ice.

# Strawberry Basil Shrub
Drink | American | 10 | 5 | 8 | Drink
- 300 g | strawberries, sliced
- 150 g | sugar
- 150 ml | apple cider vinegar
- 1 handful | basil
- 1 l | sparkling water
> Crush the strawberries with the sugar and basil and leave 1 day in the fridge.
> Strain, pressing the fruit, and stir in the vinegar; keep chilled up to 2 weeks.
> Mix about 3 tbsp of shrub with sparkling water and ice per glass.

- 150 g | sugar
- 500 ml | water
- 2 | limes

> Simmer the ginger, sugar and water for 10 minutes and steep until cool.
> Strain and add the lime juice.
> Mix 1 part syrup with 3 parts sparkling water over ice.

# Jamaican Sorrel Drink
Drink | Jamaican | 10 | 10 | 8 | Drink
- 60 g | hibiscus flowers, dried
- 50 g | ginger, sliced
- 1 stick | cinnamon
- 4 | cloves
- 1.5 l | water
- 180 g | sugar
- 1 | orange, peel
> Boil the water with the ginger, cinnamon, cloves and orange peel, then add the hibiscus and turn off the heat.
> Steep at least 4 hours or overnight.
> Strain, stir in the sugar and chill; serve over ice.

# Classic Eggnog
Drink | American | 10 | 10 | 6 | Drink
- 500 ml | milk
- 250 ml | heavy cream
- 4 | egg yolks
- 100 g | sugar
- 0.5 tsp | nutmeg
- 1 tsp | vanilla extract
- 1 stick | cinnamon
> Heat the milk, cream, cinnamon and nutmeg until steaming.
> Whisk the yolks and sugar, pour on the hot milk while whisking and return to the pan.
> Stir over low heat until it coats a spoon, about 71 °C (160 °F), add the vanilla, strain and chill.

# Mexican Spiced Coffee (Cafe de Olla)
Drink | Mexican | 5 | 10 | 4 | Drink
- 1 l | water
- 60 g | dark brown sugar, or piloncillo
- 1 stick | cinnamon
- 1 | orange, peel strip
- 2 | cloves
- 40 g | ground coffee
> Simmer the water with the sugar, cinnamon, orange peel and cloves for 5 minutes.
> Stir in the coffee, turn off the heat and steep 5 minutes.
> Strain through a fine sieve or filter and serve hot.

# Brown Sugar Bubble Tea
Drink | Taiwanese | 10 | 20 | 2 | Drink
- 100 g | tapioca pearls
- 80 g | brown sugar
- 60 ml | water
- 2 | black tea bags
- 300 ml | milk
- 8 | ice cubes
> Cook the tapioca pearls following the packet, about 15 minutes, and drain.
> Simmer the brown sugar and 60 ml water to a syrup and stir in the pearls.
> Brew strong tea with the boiling water and cool.
> Spoon the pearls and syrup into glasses, add ice, tea and milk.
