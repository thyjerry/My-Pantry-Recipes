# Penne alla Vodka-Style Tomato Cream
Main | Italian | 5 | 20 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 400 g | penne
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves | garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp | tomato paste
- 400 g | canned tomatoes
- 150 ml | heavy cream
- 0.5 tsp | chili flakes
- 40 g | parmesan, grated
> Cook the penne in salted water until al dente. Save a mug of the cooking water before draining.
> Fry the onion in the oil for 6 minutes, add the garlic, chili flakes and tomato paste and cook 2 minutes until the paste darkens.
> Add the tomatoes and simmer 8 minutes, then stir in the cream.
> Toss in the pasta with the parmesan and a splash of pasta water until glossy.

# Cacio e Pepe
Main | Italian | 5 | 12 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 400 g | spaghetti
- 100 g | parmesan, very finely grated
- 2 tsp | black pepper, coarsely ground
- 30 g | butter
> Cook the spaghetti in a smaller amount of salted water than usual so the water gets starchy. Save 300 ml before draining.
> Toast the pepper in a large pan for 1 minute, add the butter and 150 ml pasta water and bring to a simmer.
> Add the pasta, take the pan off the heat and wait 30 seconds, then add the cheese a handful at a time, tossing hard and adding water until creamy.

- 30 g | parmesan, grated | opt

# Baked Ziti

- 1 | onion, chopped

- 250 g | ricotta
- 250 g | mozzarella, grated
- 40 g | parmesan, grated

> Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Cook the pasta for 2 minutes less than the packet says and drain.
