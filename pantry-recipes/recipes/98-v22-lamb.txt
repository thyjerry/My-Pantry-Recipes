# Thai Massaman Lamb Curry
Main | Thai | 20 | 147 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 1.2 kg | lamb shoulder, boneless, cut into 4 cm pieces
- 3 tbsp | massaman curry paste
- 400 ml | coconut milk
- 2 | potatoes, cubed
- 1 | onion, cut into wedges
- 50 g | peanuts, roasted
- 1 tbsp | fish sauce
- 1 tbsp | brown sugar
- 1 | lime, juiced
- 4 | garlic cloves, chopped
- 2 | red chilies, sliced
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
> Pat the lamb dry and season well with salt and the curry paste.
> Heat 2 tbsp oil in a heavy pot over medium-high heat and brown the lamb in batches, about 12 minutes in total; set aside.
> Lower the heat and cook the garlic and chilies for 6 to 8 minutes until soft.
> Add the coconut milk, potatoes, onion and 300 ml water, scrape up the browned bits and return the meat. Cover and simmer gently for about 2 hours (or bake covered at 160 °C), until the lamb is very tender.
> Stir in the fish sauce, sugar and lime juice and scatter with peanuts. Taste for salt.
> Serve with jasmine rice.

- 8 | lamb chops (loin or cutlets)
- 150 ml | dry white wine
- 2 | bay leaves
- 4 | eggs, fried
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 4 | garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 tbsp | vegetable oil
> Pat the lamb chops dry and season with salt and black pepper.
> Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat. Cook 3 to 4 minutes per side, until done to your liking (63 °C / 145 °F for medium). Rest on a warm plate.
> Lower the heat and, in the same pan, cook the onion and garlic for 1 minute, then the garlic and bay leaves, then the wine; bubble 2 minutes.
> Return the lamb chops with its resting juices and turn in the sauce. Serve each portion topped with a fried egg.

# Greek Stifado Lamb Stew
Main | Greek | 20 | 147 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 1.2 kg | lamb shoulder, boneless, cut into 4 cm pieces
- 500 g | shallots, peeled
- 400 g | chopped tomatoes
- 150 ml | red wine
- 2 tbsp | red wine vinegar
- 1 | cinnamon stick
- 4 | cloves
- 2 | bay leaves
- 1 | red onion, chopped
- 3 | garlic cloves, crushed
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
> Pat the lamb dry and season well with salt and the cinnamon and cloves.
> Heat 2 tbsp oil in a heavy pot over medium-high heat and brown the lamb in batches, about 12 minutes in total; set aside.
> Lower the heat and cook the onion and garlic for 6 to 8 minutes until soft.
> Add the wine, vinegar, tomatoes, bay leaves and whole shallots, scrape up the browned bits and return the meat. Cover and simmer gently for about 2 hours (or bake covered at 160 °C), until the lamb is very tender.
> Serve with rice or orzo. Taste for salt.

- 3 | garlic cloves, crushed

# Greek Souvlaki Lamb Chops Skewers
Main | Greek | 15 | 14 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 8 | lamb chops (loin or cutlets)
- 2 | lemons, juiced
- 2 tsp | dried oregano
- 200 g | Greek yogurt
- 1 | cucumber, grated
- 4 | pitta breads
- 1 | red onion, chopped
- 3 | garlic cloves, crushed
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
> Mix the lemon juice, oregano, garlic and oil with 2 tbsp oil and 1 tsp salt. Coat the lamb chops and leave to marinate 1 to 8 hours in the fridge.
> Heat a grill, barbecue or ridged grill pan to medium-high. Thread on skewers and cook on the grill; mix the yogurt and squeezed cucumber as tzatziki.
> Grill about 3 minutes per side, turning once, until done to your liking (63 °C / 145 °F for medium).
> Rest 5 minutes. Serve in warm pitta with the tzatziki.

# Greek Keftedes Lamb Meatballs
Main | Greek | 20 | 25 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish

- 1 | egg

- 1 handful | mint leaves, chopped
- 1 tsp | dried oregano
- 200 g | Greek yogurt
- 1 | lemon, cut into wedges
- 1 | red onion, chopped
- 3 | garlic cloves, crushed
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
> Mix the lamb with the egg, breadcrumbs, mint, oregano, onion and garlic and 1 tsp salt; with wet hands shape into 16 balls.
