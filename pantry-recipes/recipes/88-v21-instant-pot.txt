# Instant Pot Chicken Tinga
Main | Mexican | 10 | 40 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 800 g | chicken thighs, boneless
- 1 | onion, sliced
- 400 g | chopped tomatoes
- 2 tbsp | chipotle peppers in adobo, chopped
- 1 tsp | dried oregano
- 250 ml | chicken stock
> Select Sauté and heat the pot. Soften the onion in a little oil for 4 minutes.
> Pour in the stock and scrape the bottom of the inner pot clean, so nothing is stuck and the pot doesn't show a burn warning. An electric pressure cooker needs at least 1 cup (250 ml) of thin liquid to come up to pressure.
> Add the chicken, tomatoes, chipotle and oregano.
> Lock the lid and turn the valve to Sealing. Pressure cook on High for 10 minutes. The pot first takes about 10 to 15 minutes to come up to pressure; that is not part of the cook time.
> Let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes, then quick release the rest and open the lid carefully.
> Shred the chicken and simmer on Sauté for 5 minutes to thicken. Serve in tortillas.

# Instant Pot Dal Makhani
Main | Indian | 10 | 65 | 6 | Dinner, Main dish
- 250 g | brown lentils (or whole urad), soaked overnight
- 60 g | kidney beans, soaked overnight
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 2 tbsp | ginger garlic paste
- 200 g | passata
- 1 tsp | garam masala
- 40 g | butter
- 100 ml | heavy cream
- 750 ml | water
> Select Sauté and heat the pot. Melt the butter and fry the onion and ginger garlic paste for 4 minutes.
> Pour in the water and scrape the bottom of the inner pot clean, so nothing is stuck and the pot doesn't show a burn warning. An electric pressure cooker needs at least 1 cup (250 ml) of thin liquid to come up to pressure.
> Add the drained lentils, beans, passata and garam masala.
> Lock the lid and turn the valve to Sealing. Pressure cook on High for 30 minutes. The pot first takes about 10 to 15 minutes to come up to pressure; that is not part of the cook time.
> Let the pressure release naturally for about 15 minutes, until the float valve drops, then open the lid.
> Stir in the cream, mash a little and simmer 5 minutes.
