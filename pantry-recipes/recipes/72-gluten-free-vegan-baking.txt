# Gluten-Free Almond Cake
Baking | Spanish | 15 | 40 | 8 | Snack, Dessert, Baked goods
- 250 g | almond flour
- 200 g | sugar
- 4 | eggs
- 1 | lemon, zested
- 1 tsp | gluten-free baking powder
- 1 tbsp | powdered sugar
> Heat the oven to 175 °C (350 °F) and line a 20 cm (8-inch) tin.
> Whisk the eggs and sugar until pale and thick, then fold in the almond flour, zest and baking powder.
> Bake 35 to 40 minutes, cool and dust with powdered sugar.

# Vegan Banana Bread

- 120 g | brown sugar
- 80 ml | almond milk
- 1 tsp | vanilla extract
- 250 g | flour
- 1 tsp | baking soda
- 1 tsp | cinnamon
- 0.5 tsp | salt
- 60 g | walnuts, chopped
> Heat the oven to 175 °C (350 °F) and line a loaf tin.
