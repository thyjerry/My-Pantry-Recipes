# Loaded Baked Potatoes with Broccoli
Main | American | 5 | 60 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 4 | large potatoes
- 1 tbsp | olive oil
- 200 g | broccoli, in small florets
- 100 g | cheddar, grated
- 4 tbsp | sour cream
- 2 | green onions, sliced
- | salt
> Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Prick the potatoes, rub with oil and salt and bake for 55 to 60 minutes until the skins are crisp.
> Steam or boil the broccoli for 4 minutes.
> Split the potatoes, fluff the insides with a fork and fill with broccoli and cheese. Return to the oven for 5 minutes to melt.
> Top with sour cream and green onions.
