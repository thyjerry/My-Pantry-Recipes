# Chickpea Tikka Masala
Main | Indian | 10 | 30 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 2 can | chickpeas, drained
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 3 cloves | garlic
- 1 tbsp | ginger
- 2 tbsp | tikka masala paste
- 1 can | canned tomatoes
- 200 ml | coconut milk
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 1 handful | cilantro
- | salt
> Cook the onion in the oil for 8 minutes, then the garlic, ginger and paste for 1 minute.
> Add the tomatoes and simmer 10 minutes, then blend if you like it smooth.
> Add the chickpeas and coconut milk and simmer 10 minutes; finish with cilantro.

- 60 g | parmesan

- | salt
> Roast the vegetables with the oil and salt at 220 °C (425 °F) for 25 minutes and stir into the marinara.
