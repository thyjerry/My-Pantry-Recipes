# Slow Cooker Salsa Verde Chicken Thighs
Main | Mexican | 15 | 300 | 6 | Dinner, Main dish
- 1 kg | chicken thighs, boneless and skinless
- 1 tsp | salt
- 450 g | salsa verde
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 1 tsp | cumin
- 2 | garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 handful | coriander, chopped
- 1 | lime, juiced
> Put the chicken thighs in the slow cooker and season with the salt.
> Mix in the sauce ingredients, turning everything to coat.
> Cover and cook on Low for 5 hours (or High for about 2 hours) until tender.
> Shred or slice the chicken in the sauce. Taste for salt and finish with the coriander, lime.
