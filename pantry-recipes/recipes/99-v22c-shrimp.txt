# Thai Holy Basil Shrimp Stir-Fry
Main | Thai | 15 | 10 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 500 g | shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 2 tbsp | oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp | fish sauce
- 1 tsp | sugar
- 1 large handful | basil leaves (Thai holy basil if you can find it)
- 1 | onion, sliced
- 4 | garlic cloves, chopped
- 2 | red chilies, sliced
- 1 tsp | cornstarch
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
> Pat the shrimp dry and toss with the cornstarch and a pinch of salt.
> For the sauce, stir together the oyster sauce, fish sauce, sugar and 3 tbsp water.
> Heat the oil in a wok or large frying pan over high heat until shimmering. Stir-fry the shrimp in two batches, about 2 minutes per batch, until pink and opaque all the way through; tip onto a plate.
> Stir-fry the onion with the garlic and chilies for 2 to 3 minutes.
> Return the shrimp with any juices, pour in the sauce and toss for 1 minute until glossy and hot. Turn off the heat and stir in the basil until it wilts.
> Serve with jasmine rice.

# Thai Garlic Pepper Shrimp
Main | Thai | 10 | 12 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish

- 1 tsp | sugar
- 4 | garlic cloves, chopped
- 2 | red chilies, sliced
- 1 tbsp | vegetable oil
> Pat the shrimp dry and season with salt and the white pepper.
