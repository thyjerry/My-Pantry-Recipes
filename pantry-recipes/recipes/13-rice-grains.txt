# Chicken Biryani
Main | Indian | 20 | 45 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 300 g | basmati rice
- 600 g | chicken thighs, cut into chunks
- 200 g | yogurt
- 2 tbsp | ginger garlic paste
- 2 tsp | garam masala
- 1 tsp | chili powder
- 0.5 tsp | turmeric
- 2 | onions, thinly sliced
- 3 tbsp | ghee
- 1 pinch | saffron, soaked in 3 tbsp warm milk
- 3 tbsp | mint, chopped
> Mix the chicken with the yogurt, ginger garlic paste, spices and 1 teaspoon of salt. Leave for 20 minutes or longer.
> Fry the onions in the ghee for 12 minutes until deep golden. Remove half for the top.
> Parboil the rice in salted water for 5 minutes and drain.
> Add the chicken to the pot with the remaining onions and cook 10 minutes. Layer the rice over, drizzle with the saffron milk, scatter with mint and fried onion. Cover tightly and cook on the lowest heat for 20 minutes.

# Mujaddara
Main | Levantine | 10 | 45 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 200 g | lentils, brown or green
- 200 g | rice
- 3 | onions, thinly sliced
- 5 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 tsp | cumin
- 0.5 tsp | cinnamon
- 200 g | yogurt | opt
> Simmer the lentils in plenty of water for 15 minutes until just tender; drain.
> Fry the onions slowly in the oil for 25-30 minutes until deep brown and some are crisp. Lift out half.
> Add the rice, cumin and cinnamon to the pan and stir 1 minute, then add the lentils, 500 ml water and 1 teaspoon salt.
> Cover and cook on low for 18 minutes, then rest 5 minutes. Serve topped with the reserved onions and yogurt.

# Jollof Rice
Main | West African | 15 | 45 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 400 g | rice
- 400 g | canned tomatoes
- 2 | bell peppers
- 1 | habanero
- 2 | onions
- 3 tbsp | tomato paste
- 4 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 1 tsp | curry powder
- 1 tsp | dried thyme
- 2 | bay leaves
- 600 ml | chicken stock
> Blend the tomatoes, peppers, habanero and one onion until smooth.
> Slice the other onion and fry in the oil for 5 minutes, add the tomato paste and fry 3 minutes, then add the blended sauce, curry powder, thyme and bay leaves. Simmer 15 minutes until thick.
> Stir in the rinsed rice and stock, season with salt, cover tightly and cook on low for 25-30 minutes, without stirring, until the rice is tender.

# Arroz con Pollo
Main | Cuban | 15 | 40 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 700 g | chicken thighs
- 300 g | rice
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 1 | bell pepper, chopped
- 3 cloves | garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 tsp | cumin
- 1 tsp | paprika
- 1 pinch | saffron | opt
- 2 tbsp | tomato paste
- 650 ml | chicken stock
- 150 g | peas
> Season the chicken with salt, cumin and paprika and brown in the oil for 4 minutes per side. Set aside.
> Fry the onion, pepper and garlic for 5 minutes, then stir in the tomato paste, saffron and rice.
> Add the stock, nestle the chicken back in, cover and simmer on low for 25 minutes.
> Scatter over the peas, cover again for 5 minutes and serve.

# Pea and Parmesan Risotto

- 150 ml | white wine
- 1.1 l | vegetable stock, hot
- 250 g | peas
- 50 g | parmesan, grated
- 1 | lemon, zested
> Soften the onion in the oil and half the butter for 5 minutes. Add the rice and stir 1 minute.

# Paella with Chicken and Shrimp
Main | Spanish | 15 | 35 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 300 g | rice
- 400 g | chicken thighs, cut into chunks
- 250 g | shrimp
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 1 | bell pepper, sliced
- 3 cloves | garlic, minced
- 1 tsp | smoked paprika
- 1 pinch | saffron
- 400 g | canned tomatoes
- 900 ml | chicken stock
- 100 g | peas
- 3 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | lemon
> Brown the chicken in the oil in a wide pan. Add the onion and pepper for 5 minutes, then the garlic and paprika.
> Add the tomatoes and cook 5 minutes, then stir in the rice, saffron and hot stock. Spread evenly and do not stir again.

> Rest for 5 minutes under a towel and serve with lemon.
