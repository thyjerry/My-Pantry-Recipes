# Nigerian Jollof Rice
Main | Nigerian | 20 | 45 | 6 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 4 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 2 | onions
- 2 | red bell peppers
- 1 | scotch bonnet pepper
- 1 can | canned tomatoes
- 3 tbsp | tomato paste
- 2 tsp | curry powder
- 1 tsp | dried thyme
- 2 | bay leaves
- 400 g | long-grain rice, rinsed
- 750 ml | chicken stock
- | salt
> Blend one onion with the peppers, scotch bonnet and tomatoes until smooth.
> Fry the other onion, sliced, in the oil for 4 minutes, add the tomato paste and fry 3 minutes, then add the blended mixture and cook for 15 minutes until thick and the oil rises.
> Stir in the curry powder, thyme, bay, salt and stock, then the rice.
> Cover tightly with foil and the lid and cook on low for 25 to 30 minutes without stirring, until the rice is tender.
