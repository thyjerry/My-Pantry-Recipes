# Blueberry Buttermilk Pancakes
Breakfast | American | 10 | 15 | 4 | Breakfast, Brunch
- 250 g | flour
- 2 tsp | baking powder
- 0.5 tsp | baking soda
- 2 tbsp | sugar
- 1 pinch | salt
- 350 ml | buttermilk
- 2 | eggs
- 3 tbsp | butter, melted
- 150 g | blueberries
> Whisk the flour, baking powder, baking soda, sugar and salt in a bowl.
> Beat the buttermilk, eggs and melted butter together, pour into the dry mix and stir until just combined.
> Ladle about 3 tablespoons of batter into a lightly buttered pan over medium heat and scatter a few blueberries on top.
> Cook until bubbles form and the edges look set, about 2 minutes, then flip and cook 1 minute more.

# Classic Belgian-Style Waffles
Breakfast | Belgian | 10 | 20 | 4 | Breakfast, Brunch
- 250 g | flour
- 2 tsp | baking powder
- 2 tbsp | sugar
- 1 pinch | salt
- 400 ml | milk
- 2 | eggs
- 80 g | butter, melted
- 1 tsp | vanilla extract
> Heat the waffle iron and brush it lightly with butter.
> Whisk the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt. Beat the milk, eggs, melted butter and vanilla, then stir into the dry mix until smooth.

- 60 ml | milk
- 100 g | spinach, chopped
- 1 | bell pepper, finely diced
- 80 g | cheddar, grated
- 0.5 tsp | salt
- 1 pinch | black pepper
- | cooking spray
> Heat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan) and grease a 12-hole muffin tin with cooking spray.

- 300 g | sausages, skins removed
- 1 | onion, diced
- 1 | bell pepper, diced
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 1 tsp | paprika
- 4 | eggs
- 0.5 tsp | salt
> Boil the potato cubes in salted water for 5 minutes, then drain well.
> Brown the sausage meat in the oil in a large frying pan, breaking it up, for 5 minutes. Add the onion, pepper and potatoes.

- 150 ml | milk

# Tamagoyaki-Style Rolled Omelette
Breakfast | Japanese | 5 | 10 | 4 | Breakfast, Brunch, Lunch
- 8 | eggs
- 2 tbsp | mirin
- 2 tsp | soy sauce
- 2 tsp | sugar
- 1 tbsp | vegetable oil
> Beat the eggs with the mirin, soy sauce and sugar until even but not frothy.
> Wipe a non-stick frying pan with oil and set over medium-low heat. Pour in a thin layer of egg and let it nearly set.
> Roll the egg up from the far side towards you, push the roll back, oil the pan again and pour in another thin layer, lifting the roll so egg runs underneath.
> Roll again and repeat until the egg is used. Rest for 2 minutes, then slice.

# Poha with Peas and Peanuts

- 10 | curry leaves
- 1 | onion, finely chopped
- 1 | chili, chopped
- 0.5 tsp | turmeric
- 100 g | peas
- 50 g | peanuts
- 1 | lemon
- 1 tsp | sugar
> Rinse the poha in a sieve under cold water for 30 seconds, then leave to drain and soften.
