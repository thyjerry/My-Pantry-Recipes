# Broccoli Crunch Salad with Bacon
Salad | American | 15 | 5 | 6 | Lunch, Side dish, Salad
- 500 g | broccoli, small florets
- 6 slices | bacon, cooked and crumbled
- 40 g | dried cranberries
- 40 g | sunflower seeds
- 0.5 | red onion, finely diced
- 120 g | mayonnaise
- 1 tbsp | apple cider vinegar
- 1 tbsp | honey
> Whisk the mayonnaise, vinegar and honey.
> Toss with the broccoli, onion, cranberries and seeds.
> Chill 30 minutes and top with the bacon.

# Japanese Cucumber Sunomono
Salad | Japanese | 10 | 0 | 4 | Side dish, Salad
- 2 | cucumbers, very thinly sliced
- 1 tsp | salt
- 4 tbsp | rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp | sugar
- 1 tsp | soy sauce
- 1 tbsp | sesame seeds
- 1 tbsp | wakame, soaked, opt
> Toss the cucumber with the salt, leave 10 minutes and squeeze dry.
> Stir the vinegar, sugar and soy sauce until the sugar dissolves.
> Toss with the cucumber and wakame and sprinkle with sesame.

# Roasted Sweet Potato and Kale Salad
Salad | American | 15 | 25 | 4 | Lunch, Salad
- 2 | sweet potatoes, cubed
- 200 g | kale, chopped
- 1 can | chickpeas, drained
- 40 g | pumpkin seeds
- 3 tbsp | olive oil
- 2 tbsp | tahini
- 1 | lemon
- 1 tsp | maple syrup
- | salt
> Roast the sweet potato and chickpeas with 2 tbsp oil and salt at 210 °C (410 °F) for 25 minutes.
> Massage the kale with the rest of the oil and a pinch of salt.
> Whisk the tahini, lemon juice, maple syrup and water to a pourable dressing.
> Toss everything together and scatter with seeds.

- 0.5 | red onion, sliced
