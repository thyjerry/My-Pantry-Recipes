# Savory Oatmeal with Fried Egg
Breakfast | International | 5 | 10 | 2 | Breakfast
- 100 g | rolled oats
- 400 ml | water
- 2 | eggs
- 2 | green onions, sliced
- 1 tbsp | soy sauce
- 1 tsp | sesame oil
- 1 tbsp | vegetable oil
- | black pepper
> Simmer the oats in the salted water for 5 minutes until creamy.
> Fry the eggs in the oil until the edges crisp.
> Spoon the oats into bowls, top with the eggs and green onions and drizzle with soy sauce and sesame oil.

# Tofu Breakfast Tacos

# Chickpea Flour Omelette
Breakfast | Indian | 10 | 10 | 2 | Breakfast, Brunch
- 100 g | chickpea flour
- 180 ml | water
- 0.25 tsp | turmeric
- 0.5 tsp | baking powder
- 0.5 | onion, finely chopped
- 1 | tomato, diced
- 1 handful | cilantro
- 1 | green chili, opt
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- | salt
> Whisk the chickpea flour, water, turmeric, baking powder and salt to a smooth batter and rest 5 minutes.
> Stir in the onion, tomato, cilantro and chili.
> Pour half into a hot oiled frying pan, cook 3 minutes, flip and cook 2 minutes; repeat.

# Turkish Eggs with Garlic Yogurt (Cilbir)
Breakfast | Turkish | 10 | 10 | 2 | Breakfast, Brunch
- 250 g | greek yogurt
- 1 clove | garlic, grated
- 4 | eggs
- 40 g | butter
- 1 tsp | aleppo pepper
- 1 tbsp | white vinegar
- 1 handful | dill
- 2 slices | bread, toasted
- | salt
> Stir the garlic and salt into the yogurt and spread over two plates.
> Poach the eggs in simmering water with the vinegar for 3 to 4 minutes.
> Melt the butter with the aleppo pepper until foaming.
> Set the eggs on the yogurt, spoon over the butter, scatter with dill and serve with toast.

# Ham and Cheese Croissant Bake
Breakfast | French | 15 | 35 | 6 | Breakfast, Brunch
- 6 | croissants, torn
- 200 g | ham, diced
- 120 g | gruyere, grated
- 5 | eggs
- 400 ml | milk
- 1 tsp | dijon mustard
- | black pepper
> Butter a baking dish and scatter in the croissants, ham and most of the cheese.

# Cinnamon French Toast Casserole
Breakfast | American | 15 | 45 | 8 | Breakfast, Brunch
- 1 loaf | brioche, cubed
- 6 | eggs
- 500 ml | milk
- 80 g | brown sugar
- 2 tsp | cinnamon
- 1 tsp | vanilla extract
- 50 g | butter, melted
- | maple syrup
> Put the bread in a buttered dish.
> Whisk the eggs, milk, half the sugar, cinnamon and vanilla, pour over and chill at least 1 hour or overnight.
> Drizzle with the butter, sprinkle with the remaining sugar and bake at 175 °C (350 °F) for 40 to 45 minutes.
> Serve with maple syrup.

# Green Shakshuka
Breakfast | Israeli | 10 | 20 | 4 | Breakfast, Brunch, Dinner
- 1 | leek, sliced
- 200 g | spinach
- 200 g | kale, chopped
- 1 | zucchini, diced
- 2 cloves | garlic
- 1 tsp | cumin
- 6 | eggs
- 80 g | feta
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- | salt
> Soften the leek and zucchini in the oil over medium heat for 6 minutes.
> Add the garlic, cumin, kale and spinach and cook until wilted.
> Make 6 hollows, crack in the eggs, cover and cook on low for 6 to 8 minutes.
> Crumble over the feta.

# Tamago Kake Gohan (Egg over Rice)
Breakfast | Japanese | 2 | 0 | 1 | Breakfast
- 200 g | cooked rice, hot
- 1 | egg, very fresh
- 2 tsp | soy sauce
- 1 | green onion, sliced
- 1 pinch | furikake
> Put the steaming rice in a bowl and make a well.
> Crack in the egg, add the soy sauce and stir quickly so the heat of the rice lightly cooks the egg.
> Top with green onion and furikake; use only very fresh, high-quality eggs as the egg stays partly raw.

# Rava Upma
Breakfast | South Indian | 10 | 15 | 3 | Breakfast
- 180 g | semolina
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 1 tsp | mustard seeds
- 1 tbsp | urad dal, opt
- 10 | curry leaves
- 1 | green chili
- 1 tsp | ginger, grated
- 600 ml | water
- 2 tbsp | ghee
- 1 handful | cilantro
- | salt
> Toast the semolina in a dry pan until fragrant and set aside.
> Fry the mustard seeds, dal and curry leaves in the ghee until they crackle, then the onion, chili and ginger for 4 minutes.
> Add the water and salt and bring to a boil, then rain in the semolina while stirring and cook 2 to 3 minutes.
> Cover off the heat for 2 minutes and finish with cilantro.

# Onion Uttapam
Breakfast | South Indian | 10 | 20 | 4 | Breakfast
- 600 ml | dosa batter, store-bought or homemade
- 2 | onions, finely chopped
- 2 | tomatoes, finely chopped
- 1 | green chili, chopped
- 1 handful | cilantro
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
> Heat a lightly oiled frying pan over medium heat.
> Spread a ladle of batter into a thick 15 cm round.
> Scatter with onion, tomato, chili and cilantro and press in lightly.
> Drizzle with oil, cook 3 minutes, flip and cook 2 minutes; repeat.
