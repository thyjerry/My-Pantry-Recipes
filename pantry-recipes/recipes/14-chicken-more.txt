# Butter Chicken
Main | Indian | 15 | 30 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 700 g | chicken thighs, cut into chunks
- 150 g | yogurt
- 2 tbsp | ginger garlic paste
- 2 tsp | garam masala
- 1 tsp | kashmiri chili powder
- 40 g | butter
- 1 | onion, finely chopped
- 400 g | canned tomatoes, blended
- 150 ml | heavy cream
- 1 tsp | kasuri methi | opt
- 1 tsp | sugar
> Mix the chicken with the yogurt, half the ginger garlic paste, 1 teaspoon garam masala, the chili powder and salt. Leave 15 minutes.
> Grill or pan-fry the chicken over high heat until charred in places, about 8 minutes.
> Melt the butter, cook the onion 6 minutes, add the rest of the paste and garam masala, then the tomatoes and sugar. Simmer 12 minutes.
> Stir in the cream, kasuri methi and chicken and simmer 5 minutes.

# Chicken Tikka Masala
Main | British Indian | 15 | 30 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 700 g | chicken breast, cut into chunks
- 150 g | yogurt
- 2 tsp | garam masala
- 1 tsp | cumin
- 1 tsp | paprika
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 3 cloves | garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp | ginger, grated
- 400 g | canned tomatoes
- 200 ml | coconut cream
- 3 tbsp | cilantro, chopped
> Toss the chicken with the yogurt, half the spices and salt, then grill or sear in a hot pan until charred.
> Fry the onion in the oil for 8 minutes, add the garlic, ginger and the rest of the spices for 1 minute.
> Add the tomatoes and simmer 10 minutes, then blend smooth if you like.
> Stir in the coconut cream and chicken and simmer 8 minutes. Scatter with cilantro.

# Chicken Piccata
Main | Italian-American | 10 | 15 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 4 | chicken breasts, halved horizontally
- 50 g | flour
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 40 g | butter
- 2 cloves | garlic, minced
- 200 ml | chicken stock
- 1 | lemon, juiced
- 2 tbsp | capers
- 2 tbsp | parsley, chopped
> Season the chicken and dust with flour.
> Fry in the oil and half the butter for 3 minutes per side until golden and cooked through. Set aside.
> Add the garlic, stock, lemon juice and capers to the pan and boil 3 minutes until reduced by half.
> Whisk in the rest of the butter, return the chicken and spoon over the sauce. Sprinkle with parsley.
