# Instant Pot Chicken Noodle Soup
Soup | American | 15 | 38 | 6 | Lunch, Dinner, Soup
- 1 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 2 | carrots, sliced
- 2 stalks | celery, sliced
- 2 cloves | garlic, minced
- 700 g | chicken thighs, boneless
- 1.5 l | chicken stock
- 1 tsp | dried thyme
- 1 | bay leaf
- 150 g | egg noodles
- 2 tbsp | parsley, chopped
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Select Sauté and heat the pot. Add the oil, onion, carrot and celery and cook for 4 to 5 minutes until the onion softens. Stir in the garlic and thyme for 30 seconds.
> Pour in the stock and scrape the bottom of the inner pot clean, so nothing is stuck and the pot doesn't show a burn warning. An electric pressure cooker needs at least 1 cup (250 ml) of thin liquid to come up to pressure.
> Add the chicken thighs and bay leaf.
> Lock the lid and turn the valve to Sealing. Pressure cook on High for 8 minutes. The pot first takes about 10 to 15 minutes to come up to pressure; that is not part of the cook time.
> Let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes, then quick release the rest and open the lid carefully.
> Lift out the chicken, shred it with two forks and discard the bay leaf.
> Select Sauté, bring the soup to a boil, add the noodles and cook for 5 to 6 minutes until tender.
> Return the chicken, season with salt and pepper and stir in the parsley.

# Instant Pot Split Pea Soup
Soup | American | 15 | 50 | 6 | Lunch, Dinner, Soup
- 1 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 2 | carrots, diced
- 2 stalks | celery, diced
- 2 cloves | garlic, minced
- 450 g | dried split peas, rinsed
- 1.5 l | vegetable stock
- 1 | smoked ham hock, opt
- 1 tsp | dried thyme
- 1 | bay leaf
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Select Sauté and heat the pot. Add the oil, onion, carrot and celery and cook for 5 minutes, then stir in the garlic and thyme.
> Pour in the stock and scrape the bottom of the inner pot clean, so nothing is stuck and the pot doesn't show a burn warning. An electric pressure cooker needs at least 1 cup (250 ml) of thin liquid to come up to pressure.
> Add the rinsed split peas, bay leaf and ham hock if using; do not fill the pot above the half line because split peas foam.
> Lock the lid and turn the valve to Sealing. Pressure cook on High for 15 minutes. The pot first takes about 10 to 15 minutes to come up to pressure; that is not part of the cook time.
> Let the pressure release naturally for about 15 minutes, until the float valve drops, then open the lid.
> Remove the bay leaf and the ham hock (pull the meat off the bone and return it).
> Stir well; the peas break down as they cool. Thin with water if needed and season to taste.

# Instant Pot Borscht
Soup | Ukrainian | 15 | 38 | 6 | Lunch, Dinner, Soup
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 2 | carrots, grated
- 500 g | beets, peeled and grated
- 300 g | cabbage, shredded
- 2 | potatoes, diced
- 1 tbsp | tomato paste
- 1.5 l | beef stock
- 2 tbsp | red wine vinegar
- 1 | bay leaf
- 2 tbsp | dill
- 120 g | sour cream
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Select Sauté and heat the pot. Heat the oil and cook the onion and carrot for 4 minutes, then stir in the tomato paste.
> Pour in the stock and scrape the bottom of the inner pot clean, so nothing is stuck and the pot doesn't show a burn warning. An electric pressure cooker needs at least 1 cup (250 ml) of thin liquid to come up to pressure.
> Add the beets, cabbage, potatoes and bay leaf.
> Lock the lid and turn the valve to Sealing. Pressure cook on High for 8 minutes. The pot first takes about 10 to 15 minutes to come up to pressure; that is not part of the cook time.
> Let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes, then quick release the rest and open the lid carefully.
> Stir in the vinegar, season, and serve with sour cream and dill.
