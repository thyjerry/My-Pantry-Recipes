# Beef Tacos
Main | Mexican | 10 | 15 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 500 g | ground beef
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 2 clove | garlic
- 2 tsp | chili powder
- 1 tsp | cumin
- 1 tsp | paprika
- 2 tbsp | tomato paste
- 8 | tortillas
- 1 | lettuce, shredded
- 2 | tomatoes, diced
- 80 g | cheddar, grated
- 4 tbsp | sour cream | opt
> Brown the beef with the onion in a dry pan for 8 minutes.
> Add the garlic, spices and tomato paste with a splash of water. Simmer 5 minutes and season.
> Warm the tortillas.
> Fill with beef, lettuce, tomato, cheese and a spoon of sour cream.

# Chili con Carne
Main | Mexican | 15 | 45 | 6 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 1 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 1 | bell pepper, chopped
- 3 clove | garlic
- 500 g | ground beef
- 2 tsp | chili powder
- 2 tsp | cumin
- 1 tsp | paprika
- 800 g | canned tomatoes
- 2 tbsp | tomato paste
- 400 g | kidney beans, drained
- 300 ml | beef stock
- | salt
> Soften the onion and pepper in the oil for 6 minutes. Add the garlic.
> Add the beef and brown well. Stir in the spices for 1 minute.
> Add the tomatoes, tomato paste and stock. Simmer for 30 minutes, stirring now and then.
> Add the beans and cook 10 minutes more. Season and serve with rice.

# Classic Beef Burgers
Main | American | 15 | 10 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 600 g | ground beef
- 1 | onion, finely grated
- 1 tsp | salt
- 0.5 tsp | black pepper
- 4 | burger buns
- 4 slice | cheddar
- 1 | lettuce
- 2 | tomatoes, sliced
- 2 tbsp | ketchup | opt
> Mix the beef, onion, salt and pepper lightly and shape into four patties a little wider than the buns.
> Cook in a hot pan or grill for 4 to 5 minutes per side. Add the cheese for the last minute.
> Toast the buns and fill with lettuce, tomato, the burgers and ketchup.

# Beef and Broccoli
Main | Chinese | 15 | 10 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 500 g | steak, thinly sliced
- 1 | broccoli, in florets
- 3 tbsp | soy sauce
- 2 tbsp | oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp | brown sugar
- 1 tbsp | cornstarch
- 2 clove | garlic
- 1 tbsp | ginger
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
> Toss the beef with 1 tablespoon soy sauce and the cornstarch.
> Blanch the broccoli in boiling water for 2 minutes and drain.
> Stir-fry the beef in very hot oil for 2 minutes and remove.
> Add the garlic and ginger for 30 seconds, then the broccoli, beef, the rest of the soy sauce, oyster sauce, sugar and 4 tablespoons water. Toss until glossy and serve with rice.

# Shepherd's Pie
Main | British | 20 | 45 | 6 | Dinner, Main dish
- 1 kg | potatoes
- 50 g | butter
- 100 ml | milk
- 1 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 2 | carrots, diced
- 500 g | ground lamb
- 2 tbsp | tomato paste
- 1 tbsp | worcestershire sauce
- 300 ml | beef stock
- 150 g | peas
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Boil the potatoes until tender, then mash with the butter and milk and season.
> Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Cook the onion and carrot in the oil for 8 minutes, then brown the lamb.
> Stir in the tomato paste, worcestershire sauce and stock. Simmer for 15 minutes and add the peas.
> Spoon into a baking dish, top with the mash and rough it up with a fork.
> Bake for 25 minutes until golden.

# Swedish-Style Meatballs
Main | Swedish | 20 | 25 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 500 g | ground beef
- 1 | onion, grated
- 50 g | breadcrumbs
- 1 | egg
- 0.25 tsp | nutmeg
- 2 tbsp | butter
- 2 tbsp | flour
- 400 ml | beef stock
- 150 ml | heavy cream
- 1 tsp | soy sauce
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Mix the beef, onion, breadcrumbs, egg, nutmeg, salt and pepper. Roll into walnut-sized balls.
> Brown the meatballs in the butter in batches and set aside.
> Stir the flour into the pan for 1 minute, then whisk in the stock, cream and soy sauce.
> Return the meatballs and simmer for 10 minutes until cooked and the sauce has thickened. Serve with mashed potatoes.

# Garlic Butter Steak with Potatoes
Main | American | 10 | 25 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 4 | steaks
- 700 g | potatoes, diced small
- 3 tbsp | butter
- 4 clove | garlic
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 tsp | rosemary
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Boil the potatoes for 6 minutes and drain well.
> Fry them in the oil until golden and crisp, about 10 minutes. Season and keep warm.
> Season the steaks and sear in a very hot pan for 2 to 4 minutes per side.
> Add the butter, crushed garlic and rosemary and spoon over the steaks. Rest 5 minutes, then serve with the potatoes.

# Pork Chops with Apples
Main | American | 10 | 20 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 4 | pork chops
- 2 | apples, sliced
- 1 | onion, sliced
- 1 tbsp | butter
- 1 tbsp | olive oil
- 150 ml | chicken stock
- 1 tsp | dijon mustard
- 1 tsp | thyme
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Season the chops and brown in the oil for 4 minutes per side. Remove.
> Add the butter, onion and apples and cook for 6 minutes until golden.
> Stir in the stock, mustard and thyme, return the chops and simmer for 5 minutes until cooked through.

- 1 tbsp | olive oil

# Pulled Pork Sandwiches
Main | American | 15 | 240 | 8 | Dinner, Main dish
- 1.5 kg | pork shoulder
- 2 tsp | paprika
- 1 tbsp | brown sugar
- 1 tsp | garlic powder
- 1 | onion, sliced
- 200 ml | ketchup
- 3 tbsp | cider vinegar
- 1 tbsp | worcestershire sauce
- 8 | burger buns
- | salt
> Heat the oven to 150°C (300°F). Rub the pork with the paprika, sugar, garlic powder and salt.
> Put the onion in a casserole, sit the pork on top and add 150 ml water. Cover and cook for 4 hours until very tender.
> Shred the meat with two forks. Mix the ketchup, vinegar and worcestershire sauce with some of the cooking juices and stir through.
> Pile into the buns.

# Baked Salmon with Lemon and Dill
Main | International | 5 | 15 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 4 | salmon fillets
- 1 | lemon
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 2 tbsp | dill
- 1 clove | garlic
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F).
> Put the salmon on a lined tray. Mix the oil, crushed garlic, lemon zest and dill and spoon over.
> Season and bake for 12 to 15 minutes until just flaking. Serve with lemon wedges.

# Teriyaki Salmon
Main | Japanese | 10 | 12 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 4 | salmon fillets
- 4 tbsp | soy sauce
- 2 tbsp | honey
- 1 tbsp | rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp | ginger, grated
- 1 clove | garlic
- 1 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 1 tsp | sesame seeds | opt
> Mix the soy sauce, honey, vinegar, ginger and garlic.
> Sear the salmon skin side down in the oil for 4 minutes, then turn.
> Pour in the sauce and simmer for 3 to 4 minutes, spooning it over until glossy.
> Sprinkle with sesame seeds and serve with rice.

# Fish Tacos
Main | Mexican | 15 | 10 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 500 g | white fish
- 1 tsp | paprika
- 1 tsp | cumin
- 1 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 8 | tortillas
- 0.25 | cabbage, finely shredded
- 4 tbsp | mayonnaise
- 2 | limes
- 1 handful | cilantro
- | salt
> Rub the fish with the spices and salt.
> Fry in the oil for 2 to 3 minutes per side until it flakes.

# Garlic Butter Shrimp
Main | American | 5 | 8 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 500 g | shrimp, peeled
- 3 tbsp | butter
- 4 clove | garlic
- 1 | lemon
- 2 tbsp | parsley
- 0.25 tsp | chili flakes | opt
- | salt
> Melt the butter in a large pan and cook the garlic for 1 minute.
> Add the shrimp and chili and cook for 2 minutes per side until pink.
> Add the lemon juice and parsley, season and serve with rice or crusty bread.

# Fish Cakes
Main | British | 20 | 15 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 500 g | potatoes
- 400 g | white fish
- 2 | green onions
- 2 tbsp | parsley
- 1 | lemon
- 1 | egg
- 60 g | breadcrumbs
- 3 tbsp | vegetable oil
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Boil the potatoes until tender, drain and mash. Poach the fish in simmering water for 5 minutes and flake.
> Mix the potato, fish, green onions, parsley, lemon zest, salt and pepper. Shape into eight cakes.
> Dip in beaten egg, then breadcrumbs.
> Fry in the oil for 3 to 4 minutes per side until golden.
