# Moussaka
Main | Greek | 30 | 60 | 6 | Dinner, Main dish
- 2 | eggplants, sliced
- 4 tbsp | olive oil
- 500 g | ground lamb
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 2 cloves | garlic, minced
- 400 g | canned tomatoes
- 1 tsp | cinnamon
- 40 g | butter
- 40 g | flour
- 500 ml | milk
- 1 | egg
- 50 g | parmesan, grated
> Brush the eggplant with oil and roast at 200°C (180°C fan) for 20 minutes.
> Brown the lamb with the onion and garlic, add the tomatoes and cinnamon and simmer 20 minutes.
> Make a white sauce with the butter, flour and milk, then beat in the egg and half the cheese.
> Layer eggplant and lamb in a dish, top with the sauce and cheese and bake 35-40 minutes. Rest 15 minutes.

# Pastitsio-Style Baked Pasta

- 1 tsp | cinnamon

- 2 | eggs
- 60 g | parmesan, grated
> Cook the pasta 2 minutes under the packet time.
> Brown the beef and onion, add the tomatoes and cinnamon and simmer 15 minutes.
