# Thai Garlic Pepper Turkey Steaks
Main | Thai | 10 | 14 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 600 g | turkey breast, cut into 1 cm steaks
- 1 tsp | ground white pepper
- 2 tbsp | oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp | fish sauce
- 1 tsp | sugar
- 4 | garlic cloves, chopped
- 2 | red chilies, sliced
- 1 tbsp | vegetable oil
> Pat the turkey steaks dry and season with salt and the white pepper.
> Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat. Cook 3 to 4 minutes per side, until cooked through, with no pink in the middle (74 °C / 165 °F). Rest on a warm plate.
> Lower the heat and, in the same pan, cook the garlic and chilies for 1 minute, then the garlic for 1 minute, then add the oyster sauce, fish sauce, sugar and 4 tbsp water.
> Return the turkey steaks with its resting juices and turn in the sauce. Spoon the glossy garlic sauce over.
> Serve with jasmine rice.
