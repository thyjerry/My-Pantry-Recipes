# Chickpea and Spinach Curry
Main | Indian | 10 | 20 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 3 clove | garlic
- 1 tbsp | ginger
- 2 tbsp | curry powder
- 800 g | chickpeas, drained
- 400 g | canned tomatoes
- 400 ml | coconut milk
- 150 g | spinach
- | salt
- 1 | lime | opt
> Cook the onion in the oil for 8 minutes. Add the garlic, ginger and curry powder for 1 minute.
> Add the chickpeas, tomatoes and coconut milk and simmer for 12 minutes.
> Stir in the spinach until wilted, season and finish with lime juice. Serve with rice.

# Red Lentil Dal
Main | Indian | 10 | 25 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 300 g | red lentils, rinsed
- 1 l | water
- 1 tsp | turmeric
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 3 clove | garlic
- 1 tbsp | ginger
- 1 tsp | cumin
- 1 | chili | opt
- 200 g | canned tomatoes
- | salt
- 2 tbsp | cilantro | opt
> Simmer the lentils with the water and turmeric for 20 minutes until soft, stirring now and then.
> Meanwhile fry the onion in the oil for 8 minutes. Add the garlic, ginger, cumin and chili for 1 minute, then the tomatoes for 3 minutes.
> Stir the onion mixture into the lentils, season and simmer 5 minutes. Top with cilantro.

# Vegetable Fried Rice
Main | Chinese | 10 | 10 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 600 g | cooked rice, cold
- 3 | eggs
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 1 | carrot, diced
- 150 g | peas
- 4 | green onions, sliced
- 2 clove | garlic
- 3 tbsp | soy sauce
- 1 tsp | sesame oil
> Heat half the oil in a wok, scramble the eggs and remove.
> Add the rest of the oil and stir-fry the carrot for 2 minutes, then the peas, garlic and most of the green onions.
> Add the rice and stir-fry for 4 minutes until hot.
> Add the soy sauce, sesame oil and eggs and toss. Scatter with the remaining green onions.

# Black Bean Burgers
Main | American | 15 | 12 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 800 g | black beans, drained
- 60 g | breadcrumbs
- 1 | egg
- 1 tsp | cumin
- 1 tsp | paprika
- 2 | green onions
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 4 | burger buns
- 1 | avocado | opt
- 2 | tomatoes, sliced
- | salt
> Mash the beans roughly. Mix in the breadcrumbs, egg, spices, chopped green onions and salt.
> Shape into four patties and chill for 10 minutes if you have time.

> Serve in buns with tomato and avocado.

# Mushroom Risotto
Main | Italian | 10 | 30 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 1 tbsp | olive oil
- 30 g | butter
- 1 | onion, finely chopped
- 300 g | mushrooms, sliced
- 2 clove | garlic
- 300 g | arborio rice
- 1.2 l | vegetable stock, hot
- 50 g | parmesan
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Cook the onion in the oil and half the butter for 5 minutes. Add the mushrooms and garlic and cook until browned.
> Stir in the rice for 1 minute.
> Add the hot stock a ladle at a time, stirring, letting each addition absorb before the next. This takes about 20 minutes.
> When the rice is creamy and just tender, stir in the remaining butter and parmesan. Season and rest 2 minutes.

# Spinach and Feta Quiche
Main | French | 20 | 40 | 6 | Brunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 320 g | shortcrust pastry
- 200 g | spinach
- 150 g | feta
- 4 | eggs
- 250 ml | milk
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 1 tbsp | olive oil
- 0.25 tsp | nutmeg
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Heat the oven to 190°C (375°F). Line a 23 cm tart tin with the pastry and bake blind for 12 minutes.
> Soften the onion in the oil, add the spinach until wilted, then squeeze out the excess liquid.
> Spread the spinach and crumbled feta over the pastry.
> Whisk the eggs, milk, nutmeg, salt and pepper and pour over. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes until just set.

# Margherita Flatbread Pizza
Main | Italian | 10 | 12 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 4 | tortillas
- 6 tbsp | tomato paste
- 1 tsp | oregano
- 200 g | mozzarella
- 1 handful | basil
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- | salt
> Heat the oven to 220°C (425°F).
> Loosen the tomato paste with a little water, oregano and salt and spread over the tortillas.
> Top with torn mozzarella and drizzle with oil.

# Stuffed Bell Peppers
Main | International | 15 | 35 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 4 | bell peppers
- 200 g | cooked rice
- 400 g | black beans, drained
- 200 g | sweetcorn
- 200 g | canned tomatoes
- 1 tsp | cumin
- 100 g | cheddar, grated
- | salt
> Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Halve the peppers and remove the seeds.

# Vegetable Frittata
Main | Italian | 10 | 20 | 4 | Brunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 8 | eggs
- 1 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | onion, sliced
- 1 | zucchini, diced
- 1 | bell pepper, diced
- 100 g | spinach
- 60 g | cheddar
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Heat the grill (broiler). Cook the onion, zucchini and pepper in the oil in an ovenproof pan for 8 minutes. Add the spinach to wilt.
> Beat the eggs with salt, pepper and most of the cheese and pour over.
> Cook on low for 8 minutes until the base is set.
> Scatter over the rest of the cheese and grill for 3 to 4 minutes until puffed and golden.

- 2 clove | garlic
- 1 handful | parsley
- 2 tsp | cumin
- 1 tsp | coriander
- 3 tbsp | flour
- 4 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 4 | pita breads
- 1 | cucumber
- 2 | tomatoes

- 1 | lemon
- | salt

# Tofu Peanut Stir-Fry
Main | Thai | 15 | 15 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 400 g | firm tofu, cubed
- 2 tbsp | cornstarch
- 3 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 1 | bell pepper
- 1 | broccoli
- 3 tbsp | peanut butter
- 3 tbsp | soy sauce
- 1 tbsp | maple syrup
- 1 | lime
- 1 clove | garlic
- 2 tbsp | peanuts | opt
> Toss the tofu in the cornstarch and fry in the oil until crisp on all sides. Remove.
> Stir-fry the sliced pepper and broccoli for 4 minutes.
> Whisk the peanut butter, soy sauce, maple syrup, lime juice, garlic and 4 tablespoons hot water.
> Return the tofu, pour over the sauce and toss. Top with peanuts and serve with rice.

# Ratatouille
Main | French | 20 | 40 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 4 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | onion
- 1 | eggplant
- 2 | zucchini
- 2 | bell peppers
- 3 clove | garlic
- 400 g | canned tomatoes
- 1 tsp | thyme
- 1 handful | basil | opt
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Cut all the vegetables into bite-sized pieces.
> Cook the onion in the oil for 5 minutes. Add the eggplant and cook 5 minutes, then the zucchini, peppers and garlic for 5 minutes.
> Add the tomatoes and thyme, cover and simmer gently for 25 minutes.
> Season and stir in the basil. Good hot or at room temperature.

# Cauliflower Mac and Cheese
Main | American | 10 | 30 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 1 | cauliflower, in florets
- 30 g | butter
- 30 g | flour
- 500 ml | milk
- 150 g | cheddar
- 1 tsp | dijon mustard
- | salt
> Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Boil the cauliflower for 5 minutes and drain well.
> Melt the butter, stir in the flour, then whisk in the milk and simmer until thick.
> Stir in most of the cheese and the mustard and season.
> Mix with the cauliflower in a dish, top with the remaining cheese and bake for 20 minutes.
