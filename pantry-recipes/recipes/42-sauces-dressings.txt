# Lemon Tahini Dressing
Sauce | International | 5 | 0 | 6 | Sauce/Condiment
- 4 tbsp | tahini
- 1 | lemon, juiced
- 1 clove | garlic, grated
- 4 tbsp | water
- 1 tsp | maple syrup
- | salt
> Put everything in a jar or bowl.
> Shake or whisk until smooth and emulsified, adding a splash of water if it is too thick.
> Taste and adjust the salt, acid and sweetness. Keeps in the fridge for up to 5 days; good on grain bowls, roasted vegetables and falafel.

# Tomato Chutney
Sauce | Indian | 10 | 40 | 10 | Sauce/Condiment

- 2 cloves | garlic

- 150 g | brown sugar
- 150 ml | apple cider vinegar
- 1 tsp | mustard seeds
- 0.5 tsp | chili flakes
- | salt
> Put everything in a heavy pan and bring to a boil, stirring until the sugar dissolves.
> Simmer uncovered 35 to 40 minutes, stirring more often near the end, until thick and jammy.
> Spoon into clean jars and keep in the fridge for up to 3 weeks.
