# Cashew Chicken Stir-Fry
Main | Chinese-American | 15 | 15 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 500 g | chicken breast, cubed
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 2 cloves | garlic, chopped
- 1 tbsp | ginger, grated
- 1 | red bell pepper, diced
- 1 | onion, diced
- 80 g | cashews
- 3 tbsp | soy sauce
- 2 tbsp | hoisin sauce
- 1 tsp | rice vinegar
- 1 tsp | cornstarch
- 2 | green onions, sliced
- 300 g | rice
> Cook the rice. Whisk the soy sauce, hoisin and vinegar with 4 tbsp water and the cornstarch.
> Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wok or large frying pan over high heat. Stir-fry the chicken for 5 minutes until golden and cooked through, then tip onto a plate.
> Add the rest of the oil, then the pepper, onion and cashews. Stir-fry for 3 to 4 minutes until just tender-crisp. Add the garlic and ginger for 30 seconds.
> Return the chicken to the pan, pour in the sauce and toss for 1 to 2 minutes until glossy.
> Scatter with the green onions and serve with the rice.
