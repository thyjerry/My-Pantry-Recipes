# Iced Green Tea with Mint
Drink | Japanese | 5 | 5 | 4 | Drink
- 4 | green tea bags
- 1 l | water
- 1 handful | mint
- 2 tbsp | honey
- 1 | lemon
- 1 handful | ice cubes
> Steep the tea in 500 ml water at about 80 °C for 3 minutes, then remove the bags.
> Stir in the honey and mint and add the rest of the water, cold.
> Chill and serve over ice with lemon.

# Cold Brew Coffee
Drink | American | 5 | 0 | 6 | Drink
- 100 g | coffee, coarsely ground
- 1 l | water
- 250 ml | milk, to serve | opt
> Stir the coffee into the cold water in a jar.
> Cover and leave at room temperature for 12 to 18 hours.
> Strain through a paper filter; dilute 1:1 with water or milk and keep chilled up to a week.
