# Tomato Basil Soup
Soup | Italian | 10 | 25 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Soup
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 2 clove | garlic
- 800 g | canned tomatoes
- 500 ml | vegetable stock
- 1 tsp | sugar
- 1 handful | basil
- 4 tbsp | heavy cream | opt
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Cook the onion in the oil for 8 minutes, then add the garlic.
> Add the tomatoes, stock and sugar and simmer for 15 minutes.
> Add the basil and blend until smooth. Season and swirl in the cream.

# Minestrone
Soup | Italian | 15 | 30 | 6 | Lunch, Dinner, Soup
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | onion
- 2 | carrots
- 2 stalk | celery
- 2 clove | garlic
- 400 g | canned tomatoes
- 1.5 l | vegetable stock
- 400 g | white beans, drained
- 100 g | pasta
- 100 g | kale
- 40 g | parmesan | opt
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Dice the onion, carrot and celery and cook in the oil for 10 minutes. Add the garlic.
> Add the tomatoes and stock and simmer for 10 minutes.
> Add the beans and pasta and cook for 10 minutes, adding the shredded kale for the last 3.
> Season and serve with grated parmesan.

# Creamy Potato Leek Soup
Soup | French | 10 | 25 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Soup
- 30 g | butter
- 3 | leeks, sliced
- 600 g | potatoes, diced
- 1 l | vegetable stock
- 100 ml | heavy cream
- | salt
- | black pepper
- 1 tbsp | chives | opt
> Melt the butter and cook the leeks gently for 8 minutes without browning.
> Add the potatoes and stock and simmer for 15 minutes until soft.
> Blend until smooth, stir in the cream and season. Top with chives.

# French Onion Soup
Soup | French | 10 | 60 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Appetizer, Soup
- 50 g | butter
- 1 kg | onions, thinly sliced
- 1 tsp | sugar
- 2 tbsp | flour
- 1.2 l | beef stock
- 1 tsp | thyme
- 4 slice | bread
- 100 g | gruyere, grated
- | salt
> Melt the butter and cook the onions with the sugar very slowly for 40 minutes, stirring, until deep golden.
> Stir in the flour, then add the stock and thyme and simmer for 20 minutes. Season.
> Toast the bread, top with cheese and grill until bubbling.
> Ladle the soup into bowls and float the cheese toasts on top.

# Miso Noodle Soup
Soup | Japanese | 10 | 10 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Soup
- 1.2 l | vegetable stock
- 3 tbsp | miso
- 200 g | noodles
- 200 g | firm tofu, cubed
- 150 g | mushrooms, sliced
- 100 g | spinach
- 3 | green onions
- 1 tbsp | soy sauce
> Bring the stock to a simmer with the mushrooms and cook for 3 minutes.
> Add the noodles and cook as the packet says.
> Add the tofu and spinach for 1 minute. Take off the heat and stir in the miso and soy sauce.
> Ladle into bowls and top with sliced green onions.

# Quinoa Salad with Roasted Vegetables
Salad | International | 15 | 25 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish, Salad
- 200 g | quinoa
- 1 | zucchini
- 1 | bell pepper
- 1 | red onion
- 3 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | lemon
- 100 g | feta | opt
- 1 handful | parsley
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Heat the oven to 220°C (425°F). Chop the vegetables, toss with 1 tablespoon oil and roast for 25 minutes.
> Rinse the quinoa and simmer in 400 ml water for 15 minutes. Drain and cool slightly.
> Mix the quinoa with the vegetables, the rest of the oil, lemon juice, parsley, salt and pepper. Crumble over the feta.

# Tuna Nicoise Salad

- 300 g | tuna

- 250 g | cherry tomatoes
- 80 g | olives
- 4 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 tbsp | red wine vinegar
- 1 tsp | dijon mustard
- | salt
