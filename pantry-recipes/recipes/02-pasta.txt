# Spaghetti Aglio e Olio
Main | Italian | 5 | 12 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 400 g | spaghetti
- 6 tbsp | olive oil
- 5 clove | garlic, thinly sliced
- 0.5 tsp | chili flakes
- 3 tbsp | parsley, chopped
- | salt
- 40 g | parmesan | opt
> Cook the spaghetti in plenty of salted boiling water until al dente. Save a cup of the cooking water.
> Meanwhile warm the oil in a large pan over low heat. Add the garlic and chili flakes and cook gently until the garlic is pale gold.
> Add the drained spaghetti and a splash of pasta water and toss until glossy.
> Stir in the parsley, season and serve with parmesan if you like.

# Spaghetti Bolognese
Main | Italian | 15 | 45 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 1 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | onion, finely chopped
- 1 | carrot, finely chopped
- 1 stalk | celery, finely chopped
- 2 clove | garlic, crushed
- 500 g | ground beef
- 2 tbsp | tomato paste
- 800 g | canned tomatoes
- 1 tsp | oregano
- 400 g | spaghetti
- | salt
- | black pepper
- 40 g | parmesan | opt
> Heat the oil and cook the onion, carrot and celery for 8 minutes until soft. Add the garlic for 1 minute.
> Turn up the heat, add the beef and brown it, breaking it up with a spoon.
> Stir in the tomato paste, tomatoes and oregano. Season, cover partly and simmer for 30 minutes.
> Cook the spaghetti in salted boiling water and drain.
> Toss the pasta with the sauce and serve with grated parmesan.

# Creamy Tomato Penne
Main | Italian | 5 | 20 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 400 g | penne
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 2 clove | garlic, crushed
- 400 g | canned tomatoes
- 150 ml | heavy cream
- 1 tsp | sugar
- 1 handful | basil | opt
- 40 g | parmesan
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Cook the penne in salted boiling water.
> Meanwhile soften the onion in the oil for 6 minutes, then add the garlic for 1 minute.
> Add the tomatoes and sugar and simmer for 10 minutes. Stir in the cream and season.
> Toss with the drained pasta, parmesan and torn basil.

# Pesto Pasta with Peas
Main | Italian | 5 | 12 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 400 g | pasta
- 200 g | peas
- 6 tbsp | pesto
- 40 g | parmesan
- | salt
- 1 | lemon | opt
> Cook the pasta in salted boiling water. Add the peas for the last 3 minutes.
> Drain, keeping a little cooking water.
> Toss with the pesto, a splash of the water and most of the parmesan.
> Serve topped with the remaining cheese and a squeeze of lemon.

# Baked Macaroni and Cheese
Main | American | 15 | 30 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 350 g | macaroni
- 50 g | butter
- 50 g | flour
- 700 ml | milk
- 250 g | cheddar, grated
- 1 tsp | dijon mustard
- 4 tbsp | breadcrumbs
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Cook the macaroni for 2 minutes less than the packet says and drain.
> Melt the butter in a saucepan, stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute.
> Gradually whisk in the milk and simmer, stirring, until thick. Take off the heat and stir in most of the cheese and the mustard. Season.
> Mix the sauce with the macaroni and pour into a baking dish.
> Top with the rest of the cheese and the breadcrumbs. Bake for 20 minutes until golden and bubbling.

# Pasta Carbonara
Main | Italian | 10 | 15 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 400 g | spaghetti
- 150 g | bacon, diced
- 3 | eggs
- 60 g | parmesan, finely grated
- 2 clove | garlic | opt
- | black pepper
- | salt
> Cook the spaghetti in salted boiling water. Keep a mug of the cooking water.
> Fry the bacon (with the garlic, if using) until crisp. Remove the garlic.
> Beat the eggs with the parmesan and plenty of black pepper.
> Take the pan off the heat, add the drained pasta to the bacon, then pour in the egg mixture with a splash of pasta water, tossing quickly so it turns creamy rather than scrambled.
> Serve straight away with more pepper and cheese.

# One-Pot Vegetable Pasta
Main | Italian | 10 | 15 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 350 g | pasta
- 1 | onion, sliced
- 1 | zucchini, diced
- 1 | bell pepper, sliced
- 250 g | cherry tomatoes
- 2 clove | garlic, sliced
- 900 ml | vegetable stock
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 handful | basil | opt
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Put everything except the basil into a large wide pan.
> Bring to the boil, then simmer for 12 to 15 minutes, stirring often, until the pasta is cooked and most of the liquid is absorbed.
> Season, tear in the basil and serve.

# Tuna Pasta Bake
Main | British | 10 | 30 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 350 g | penne
- 300 g | tuna, drained
- 200 g | sweetcorn
- 400 g | canned tomatoes
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 1 tbsp | olive oil
- 120 g | cheddar, grated
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Cook the penne for 2 minutes less than the packet says.

# Lemon Garlic Shrimp Linguine
Main | Italian | 10 | 15 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 400 g | linguine
- 400 g | shrimp, peeled
- 3 tbsp | butter
- 4 clove | garlic, crushed
- 1 | lemon
- 0.5 tsp | chili flakes
- 3 tbsp | parsley, chopped
- | salt
> Cook the linguine in salted boiling water.
> Melt the butter in a large pan and cook the garlic and chili for 1 minute.
> Add the shrimp and cook for 2 to 3 minutes until pink.
> Add the drained pasta, lemon zest and juice, parsley and a splash of pasta water. Toss and serve.

# Mushroom Stroganoff Pasta
Main | Russian | 10 | 20 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 350 g | pasta
- 1 tbsp | butter
- 1 | onion, sliced
- 400 g | mushrooms, sliced
- 2 clove | garlic
- 1 tsp | paprika
- 200 ml | sour cream
- 150 ml | vegetable stock
- 2 tbsp | parsley | opt
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Cook the pasta in salted boiling water.
> Melt the butter and cook the onion for 5 minutes. Add the mushrooms and cook on high until browned.
> Add the garlic and paprika for 1 minute, then the stock. Simmer 3 minutes.
> Stir in the sour cream over low heat, season and toss with the pasta. Scatter with parsley.

# Vegetable Lasagne
Main | Italian | 25 | 45 | 6 | Dinner, Main dish
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 2 | zucchini, diced
- 1 | eggplant, diced
- 2 clove | garlic
- 800 g | canned tomatoes
- 250 g | lasagne sheets
- 500 g | ricotta
- 150 g | mozzarella
- 40 g | parmesan
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Heat the oven to 190°C (375°F).
> Cook the onion, zucchini and eggplant in the oil for 10 minutes. Add the garlic and tomatoes, season and simmer for 10 minutes.
> Spread a little sauce in a baking dish, then layer pasta sheets, sauce and spoonfuls of ricotta. Repeat, finishing with sauce.
> Top with torn mozzarella and parmesan and bake for 40 to 45 minutes. Rest 10 minutes before cutting.
