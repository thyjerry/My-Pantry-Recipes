# Lemon Poppy Seed Loaf
Baking | American | 15 | 50 | 10 | Breakfast, Snack, Baked goods
- 220 g | flour
- 2 tsp | baking powder
- 0.25 tsp | salt
- 150 g | sugar
- 2 | eggs
- 120 ml | milk
- 100 ml | vegetable oil
- 2 | lemons, zest and juice
- 1 tbsp | poppy seeds
> Heat the oven to 180°C (350°F) and line a 900 g (2 lb) loaf tin.
> Whisk the flour, baking powder, salt and spices in a bowl.
> In another bowl whisk the sugar, eggs, milk, oil and lemons, then stir into the dry ingredients until just combined.
> Scrape into the tin.
> Bake 50 to 60 minutes until a skewer comes out clean. Cool 10 minutes in the tin, then on a rack.

# Classic Cream Scones
Baking | British | 15 | 15 | 8 | Breakfast, Brunch, Snack, Baked goods
- 300 g | flour
- 1 tbsp | baking powder
- 0.5 tsp | salt
- 50 g | sugar
- 80 g | butter, cold, diced
- 180 ml | milk
- 1 | egg
> Heat the oven to 220°C (425°F) and line a tray.
> Mix the flour, baking powder, salt and sugar, then rub in the butter until it looks like breadcrumbs.
> Stir in the milk beaten with the egg (keep 1 tbsp for brushing) to make a soft dough; do not overwork it.
> Pat out 3 cm thick, cut into 8 wedges or rounds, brush with the reserved egg and bake 12 to 15 minutes until risen and golden.
> Serve warm with jam and clotted or whipped cream.

# Millionaire's Shortbread
Baking | Scottish | 30 | 25 | 16 | Dessert, Baked goods
- 250 g | flour
- 80 g | sugar
- 180 g | butter, divided
- 1 can | sweetened condensed milk
- 3 tbsp | golden syrup
- 200 g | dark chocolate
> Heat the oven to 180°C (350°F). Rub the flour, sugar and 120 g butter into a dough, press into a lined 20 cm tin and bake 20 to 25 minutes until pale golden.
