# Strawberry Lemonade
Drink | American | 10 | 0 | 6 | Drink
- 300 g | strawberries
- 100 g | sugar
- 4 | lemons, juiced
- 1 l | water
- | ice | opt
> Blend the strawberries with 500 ml cold water and the sugar until smooth.
> Strain through a sieve into a jug, pressing on the pulp (skip this if you like it pulpy).
> Stir in the lime or lemon juice and 500 ml more cold water. Taste and adjust the sweetness.
> Chill and serve over plenty of ice.
