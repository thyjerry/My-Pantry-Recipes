# Pasta alla Norma
Main | Italian | 10 | 20 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 400 g | rigatoni
- 2 | eggplants, cubed
- 4 tbsp | olive oil
- 3 cloves | garlic
- 1 can | canned tomatoes
- 1 handful | basil
- 60 g | ricotta salata
- | salt
> Bring a large pot of well-salted water to the boil and cook the pasta until al dente, keeping a cup of the cooking water before draining.
> Fry the eggplant in the oil until golden, add the garlic, then the tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes.
> Toss the drained pasta with the sauce and finish with basil and grated ricotta salata.

# Fettuccine Alfredo
Main | Italian | 10 | 20 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 400 g | fettuccine
- 4 tbsp | butter
- 240 ml | heavy cream
- 80 g | parmesan, grated
- 0.25 tsp | nutmeg
- | salt
> Bring a large pot of well-salted water to the boil and cook the pasta until al dente, keeping a cup of the cooking water before draining.

# Spinach and Ricotta Stuffed Shells
Main | Italian | 10 | 20 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 250 g | jumbo pasta shells
- 500 g | ricotta
- 200 g | spinach, cooked and squeezed
- 1 | egg
- 60 g | parmesan
- 700 g | marinara sauce
- 150 g | mozzarella
- | salt
> Heat the oven to 190 °C (375 °F). Cook the pasta in well-salted boiling water for 2 minutes less than the packet says and drain.
> Mix the ricotta, spinach, egg and half the parmesan, fill the cooked shells and set them on marinara in a dish, then top with mozzarella and bake at 190 °C for 25 minutes.
> Finish with the remaining parmesan and rest 5 minutes before serving.

# Singapore Noodles
Main | Singaporean | 10 | 20 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 250 g | rice vermicelli
- 200 g | shrimp, peeled
- 150 g | char siu pork, sliced
- 2 | eggs
- 1 | red bell pepper, sliced
- 100 g | bean sprouts
- 2 tsp | curry powder
- 2 tbsp | soy sauce
- | salt
> Cook or soak the noodles according to the packet, then drain, rinse briefly and toss with a few drops of oil so they don't stick.
> Scramble the eggs and set aside; stir-fry the shrimp and pork, add the pepper and curry powder, then toss in the soaked noodles, soy and sprouts.
> Toss well over the heat (or in the bowls) and finish with green onions.

# Dan Dan Noodles
Main | Chinese | 10 | 20 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 350 g | noodles, wheat
- 250 g | ground pork
- 2 tbsp | chili oil
- 2 tbsp | sesame paste
- 2 tbsp | soy sauce
- 1 tbsp | black vinegar
- 1 tsp | sichuan peppercorns, ground
- 2 | green onions
- 150 g | bok choy
- | salt
> Cook or soak the noodles according to the packet, then drain, rinse briefly and toss with a few drops of oil so they don't stick.
> Fry the pork until crisp with 1 tbsp soy; whisk the sesame paste, chili oil, rest of the soy, vinegar and pepper in bowls with a little noodle water.
> Toss well over the heat (or in the bowls) and finish with the noodles, blanched bok choy, pork and green onions.

# Garlic Butter Shrimp Linguine
Main | Italian | 10 | 20 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 400 g | linguine
- 400 g | shrimp, peeled
- 4 tbsp | butter
- 5 cloves | garlic
- 120 ml | dry white wine
- 1 | lemon
- 0.25 tsp | red pepper flakes
- 1 handful | parsley
- | salt
> Bring a large pot of well-salted water to the boil and cook the pasta until al dente, keeping a cup of the cooking water before draining.

# One-Pot Tomato Basil Pasta
Main | Italian | 10 | 20 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 400 g | linguine
- 1 can | canned tomatoes
- 1 | onion, thinly sliced
- 4 cloves | garlic, sliced
- 1 handful | basil
- 3 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 l | water
- 40 g | parmesan
- | salt
> Put the raw pasta, tomatoes, onion, garlic, half the basil, oil, water and 2 tsp salt in one wide pot and boil, stirring often, for 9 to 10 minutes until the pasta is tender and the liquid has become a sauce.
> Finish with the rest of the basil and parmesan and serve straight from the pot.

# Gluten-Free Pasta Primavera
Main | Italian | 10 | 20 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 350 g | gluten-free pasta
- 3 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | zucchini, sliced
- 150 g | asparagus, cut
- 150 g | peas, frozen
- 150 g | cherry tomatoes, halved
- 2 cloves | garlic
- 1 | lemon
- 40 g | parmesan
- | salt
> Bring a large pot of well-salted water to the boil and cook the pasta until al dente, keeping a cup of the cooking water before draining.
> Saute the vegetables in the oil for 4 minutes with the garlic. Cook gluten-free pasta for the shortest time on the pack and rinse briefly in hot water so it doesn't stick.
> Toss the drained pasta with the sauce and finish with lemon zest and parmesan.
