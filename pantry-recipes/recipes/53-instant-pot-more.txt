# Instant Pot Sweet Potato Black Bean Chili
Main | American | 15 | 38 | 6 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 3 cloves | garlic
- 2 tbsp | chili powder
- 1 tsp | cumin
- 1 tsp | smoked paprika
- 500 ml | vegetable stock
- 2 | sweet potatoes, cubed
- 2 can | black beans, drained
- 1 can | canned tomatoes
- 1 | lime
- 1 handful | cilantro
- | salt
> Select Sauté and heat the pot. Heat the oil and cook the onion for 4 minutes, then the garlic and spices.
> Pour in the stock and scrape the bottom of the inner pot clean, so nothing is stuck and the pot doesn't show a burn warning. An electric pressure cooker needs at least 1 cup (250 ml) of thin liquid to come up to pressure.
> Add the sweet potatoes and beans, then the tomatoes on top.
> Lock the lid and turn the valve to Sealing. Pressure cook on High for 8 minutes. The pot first takes about 10 to 15 minutes to come up to pressure; that is not part of the cook time.
> Let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes, then quick release the rest and open the lid carefully.
> Season with lime and salt, and add cilantro.

# Instant Pot Aloo Gobi
Side | Indian | 15 | 23 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Side dish
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 1 tsp | cumin seeds
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 2 cloves | garlic
- 1 tbsp | ginger
- 1 tsp | turmeric
- 1 tsp | garam masala
- 0.5 tsp | chili powder
- 250 ml | water
- 2 | potatoes, cubed
- 1 | cauliflower, in florets
- 1 | tomato, chopped
- 1 handful | cilantro
- | salt
> Select Sauté and heat the pot. Heat the oil and fry the cumin seeds until they crackle, then cook the onion for 3 minutes and the garlic, ginger and spices for 30 seconds.
> Pour in the water and scrape the bottom of the inner pot clean, so nothing is stuck and the pot doesn't show a burn warning. An electric pressure cooker needs at least 1 cup (250 ml) of thin liquid to come up to pressure.
> Add the potatoes, cauliflower and tomato with salt.
> Lock the lid and turn the valve to Sealing. Pressure cook on High for 2 minutes. The pot first takes about 10 to 15 minutes to come up to pressure; that is not part of the cook time.
> Quick release the pressure by turning the valve to Venting, keeping your hands and face away from the steam, then open the lid when the float valve drops.
> Stir gently, cook on Sauté for 2 minutes to dry slightly, and add cilantro.

# Instant Pot Hummus
Snack | Levantine | 15 | 75 | 8 | Appetizer, Snack
- 200 g | dried chickpeas, rinsed
- 1 l | water
- 1 tsp | baking soda
- 120 g | tahini
- 2 | lemons
- 2 cloves | garlic
- 3 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 tsp | cumin
- | salt
> Pour in the water and scrape the bottom of the inner pot clean, so nothing is stuck and the pot doesn't show a burn warning. An electric pressure cooker needs at least 1 cup (250 ml) of thin liquid to come up to pressure.
