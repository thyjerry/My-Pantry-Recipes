# Air Fryer Lemon Pepper Wings
Main | American | 10 | 25 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish, Appetizer, Snack
- 1 kg | chicken wings, split
- 1 tsp | baking powder
- 1 tsp | salt
- 1 tsp | black pepper, coarsely ground
- 30 g | butter, melted
- 1 | lemon, zested
> Pat the wings very dry and toss with the baking powder and salt.
> Air fry at 190 °C for 22 to 25 minutes, shaking the basket every 8 minutes, until crisp, golden and 75 °C inside.
> Toss the hot wings in the melted butter, lemon zest and lemon pepper. Serve straight away.
