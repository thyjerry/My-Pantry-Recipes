# Banana Walnut Muffins
Baking | American | 15 | 20 | 12 | Breakfast, Brunch, Snack, Baked goods
- 3 | ripe bananas, mashed
- 250 g | flour
- 2 tsp | baking powder
- 0.5 tsp | baking soda
- 1 tsp | cinnamon
- 1 pinch | salt
- 100 g | brown sugar
- 2 | eggs
- 120 ml | milk
- 80 ml | vegetable oil
- 80 g | walnuts, chopped
> Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan, 400°F) and line a 12-hole muffin tin with paper cases.
> Whisk the flour, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon and salt in a large bowl.
> In a jug, whisk the mashed bananas, brown sugar, eggs, milk and oil.
> Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and fold with a spatula until just combined; a few lumps are fine. Fold in most of the walnuts.
> Spoon the batter into the cases. Scatter over the last walnuts, then bake for 20 minutes, until risen and a skewer pushed into the middle comes out clean.
> Cool in the tin for 5 minutes, then lift onto a rack. They keep for 2 days in a tin or 2 months in the freezer.

# Potato and Onion Frittata
Breakfast | Spanish | 10 | 25 | 4 | Breakfast, Brunch, Lunch, Dinner
- 8 | eggs
- 4 tbsp | milk
- | salt
- | black pepper
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 2 | potatoes, peeled and thinly sliced
- 1 | onion, thinly sliced
- | parsley, chopped | opt
> Heat the oven to 190°C (170°C fan, 375°F). Beat the eggs with the milk and a good pinch of salt and pepper.

- 40 g | almonds, sliced
- 0.5 tsp | almond extract
> Heat the oven to 190°C (170°C fan, 375°F) and butter a 20 x 30 cm baking dish.
> Mix the oats, baking powder and salt. Whisk the milk, eggs, maple syrup, butter and vanilla and stir into the oats.
> Pour into the dish. Stir in the almond extract, lay the peaches on top and scatter with almonds.
> Bake for 35 minutes until set and golden. Serve warm, cut into squares, with a splash of milk or yogurt. Reheats well.
