# Spanish Seafood Fideua
Main | Spanish | 15 | 25 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 300 g | fideo noodles, or broken thin spaghetti
- 300 g | shrimp
- 300 g | squid, in rings
- 1 | onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves | garlic
- 1 tsp | smoked paprika
- 1 pinch | saffron
- 200 g | canned tomatoes
- 900 ml | fish stock
- 4 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | lemon
- 3 tbsp | garlic aioli
> Toast the noodles in half the oil until golden and set aside.
> Cook the onion, garlic and paprika in the rest of the oil, add the tomatoes, then the squid.
> Add the hot stock, saffron and noodles, spread evenly and simmer 10 minutes.
> Push in the shrimp and cook 5 minutes until the liquid is absorbed; serve with lemon and aioli.

- 200 g | pork belly

- 1 | onion, halved
- 2 cloves | garlic
- | salt
> Cover the beans and meats with cold water, add the onion and garlic and bring slowly to a simmer, skimming.

# Portuguese Salt Cod with Potatoes (Bacalhau a Bras)
Main | Portuguese | 20 | 20 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 400 g | salt cod, soaked 24 hours
- 400 g | potatoes, in fine matchsticks
- 2 | onions, thinly sliced
- 2 cloves | garlic
- 6 | eggs
- 60 g | black olives
- 1 handful | parsley
- 6 tbsp | olive oil
> Poach the cod 5 minutes, then flake, removing skin and bones.
> Fry the potato sticks in half the oil until crisp and set aside.
> Soften the onions and garlic in the rest, add the cod and potatoes.
> Pour in the beaten eggs and stir gently until just creamy; finish with olives and parsley.

# Polish Cabbage Rolls (Golabki)

- 500 g | ground pork, or beef
- 150 g | cooked rice
- 1 | onion, finely chopped
- 1 | egg
- 700 ml | tomato sauce
- 1 tsp | paprika
- | salt
> Core the cabbage and blanch whole, peeling off 12 leaves as they soften.
> Mix the meat, rice, onion, egg, paprika and salt.
