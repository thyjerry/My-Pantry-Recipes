# Seven-Layer Bean Dip
Snack | Tex-Mex | 20 | 0 | 8 | Appetizer, Snack
- 1 can | refried beans
- 1 tbsp | taco seasoning
- 200 g | sour cream
- 250 g | guacamole
- 200 g | salsa
- 100 g | cheddar, grated
- 2 | tomatoes, diced
- 3 | green onions
> Mix the beans with the taco seasoning and spread in a dish.
> Layer on the sour cream, guacamole, salsa, cheese, tomatoes and green onions.
> Chill and serve with tortilla chips.

# French Onion Dip
Snack | American | 5 | 30 | 8 | Appetizer, Snack
- 2 | onions, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp | butter
- 250 g | sour cream
- 100 g | cream cheese
- 1 tsp | worcestershire sauce
- | salt
> Cook the onions in the butter over low heat for 25 to 30 minutes until deep golden.
> Cool, then mix with the sour cream, cream cheese and worcestershire.
> Season and chill.

# Baked Brie with Honey and Walnuts
Snack | French | 5 | 15 | 6 | Appetizer, Snack
- 1 wheel | brie, about 250 g
- 2 tbsp | honey
- 40 g | walnuts
- 1 sprig | thyme
> Heat the oven to 180 °C (350 °F).
> Bake the brie on a lined tray for 12 to 15 minutes until soft.
> Top with honey, walnuts and thyme and serve with crackers.

# Spiced Candied Nuts
Snack | American | 5 | 20 | 8 | Snack
- 300 g | mixed nuts
- 1 | egg white
- 60 g | brown sugar
- 1 tsp | cinnamon
- 0.25 tsp | cayenne
- 0.5 tsp | salt
> Heat the oven to 150 °C (300 °F).
> Whisk the egg white until frothy and toss with the nuts, then the sugar and spices.
> Spread on a lined tray and bake for 20 minutes, stirring once; cool to crisp.

# Stovetop Popcorn with Parmesan
Snack | American | 2 | 5 | 4 | Snack
- 80 g | popcorn kernels
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 2 tbsp | butter, melted
- 30 g | parmesan, finely grated
- | salt
> Heat the oil with 3 kernels in a large covered pot until they pop.
> Add the rest, cover and shake over medium heat until popping slows.
> Toss with butter, parmesan and salt.

# Jalapeno Poppers
Snack | Tex-Mex | 15 | 20 | 6 | Appetizer, Snack
- 12 | jalapenos, halved and seeded
- 200 g | cream cheese
- 80 g | cheddar, grated
- 6 slices | bacon, halved
> Heat the oven to 200 °C (400 °F).
> Mix the cream cheese and cheddar and fill the jalapeno halves.
> Wrap each with bacon and bake for 20 minutes until the bacon is crisp.

# Cheese Straws
Snack | British | 15 | 15 | 8 | Appetizer, Snack
- 1 sheet | puff pastry
- 80 g | cheddar, finely grated
- 1 | egg, beaten
- 0.25 tsp | cayenne
> Heat the oven to 200 °C (400 °F).
> Brush the pastry with egg, scatter with cheese and cayenne, fold and roll lightly.
> Cut into strips, twist and bake for 12 to 15 minutes.

# Sweet Potato Fries (Oven)
Snack | American | 10 | 30 | 4 | Side dish, Snack
- 3 | sweet potatoes, in wedges
- 1 tbsp | cornstarch
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 tsp | smoked paprika
- | salt
> Heat the oven to 220 °C (425 °F).
> Toss the sweet potatoes with the cornstarch, then the oil and paprika.
> Spread out well and roast for 25 to 30 minutes, turning once; salt while hot.
