# Air Fryer Paprika Chicken Thighs
Main | American | 5 | 22 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 8 | chicken thighs, boneless
- 2 tsp | smoked paprika
- 1 tsp | garlic powder
- 1 tsp | brown sugar
- 1 tbsp | olive oil
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Mix the paprika, garlic powder, sugar, 1 tsp salt and some pepper.
> Rub the chicken with the oil, then the spice mix.
> Air fry at 190°C (375°F) for 18 to 22 minutes, turning halfway, until the juices run clear (75°C/165°F inside).
> Rest 5 minutes before serving.

# Air Fryer Coconut Shrimp
Main | American | 15 | 10 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 400 g | shrimp, peeled, tails on
- 40 g | flour
- 2 | eggs
- 60 g | shredded coconut
- 40 g | panko
- | salt
- | cooking spray
- 4 tbsp | sweet chili sauce
> Set out three bowls: flour seasoned with salt, beaten eggs, and the coconut mixed with the panko.
> Dip each shrimp in flour, egg, then the coconut mix, pressing it on.
> Spray the basket and the shrimp, then air fry at 200°C (400°F) for 8 to 10 minutes, turning once, until golden.
> Serve with sweet chili sauce for dipping.

# Air Fryer Crispy Fish Fingers
Main | British | 15 | 10 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 500 g | white fish fillets, cut into fingers
- 40 g | flour
- 2 | eggs
- 80 g | panko
- 0.5 tsp | paprika
- 1 | lemon
- | salt
- | cooking spray
> Pat the fish dry and season with salt.
> Coat each finger in flour, then beaten egg, then panko mixed with the paprika.
> Spray with oil and air fry at 200°C (400°F) for 8 to 10 minutes, turning halfway, until crisp and the fish flakes.
> Serve with lemon wedges, peas and tartar sauce or ketchup.
