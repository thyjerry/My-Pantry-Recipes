# Butter-Basted Ribeye
Main | American | 5 | 12 | 2 | Dinner, Main dish
- 2 | ribeye steaks, about 2.5 cm thick
- 2 tbsp | butter
- 1 tsp | salt
> Pat the steaks dry and salt them on both sides 30 minutes ahead if you can.
> Heat a heavy pan until very hot. Sear the steaks 3-4 minutes per side.
> Add the butter, tilt the pan and spoon the foaming butter over the steaks for a minute. Rest 5 minutes before slicing.

# Soft Scrambled Eggs in Butter
Breakfast | French | 2 | 6 | 2 | Breakfast, Brunch
- 6 | eggs
- 2 tbsp | butter
- 2 tbsp | heavy cream
- 1 pinch | salt
> Whisk the eggs with the cream and salt.
> Melt the butter in a pan over low heat, add the eggs and stir slowly with a spatula until just set and creamy.
> Take off the heat while still slightly glossy; they finish cooking on the plate.

# Pan-Seared Salmon with Brown Butter
Main | International | 5 | 10 | 2 | Dinner, Main dish
- 2 | salmon fillets, skin on
- 3 tbsp | butter
- 0.5 tsp | salt
> Dry the salmon well and salt both sides.
> Cook skin side down in a hot pan with a little of the butter for 5-6 minutes until the skin is crisp, then turn for 1-2 minutes.
> Remove the fish, add the rest of the butter and cook until it smells nutty and turns golden. Pour over the salmon.

# Slow-Roasted Pork Belly
Main | British | 10 | 150 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 1 kg | pork belly, skin scored
- 2 tsp | salt
- 1 tbsp | ghee, melted
> Dry the skin well, rub the salt into the scores and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight if you have time. Brush the meat side (not the skin) with the ghee.
> Roast at 150 °C for 2 hours, then turn the oven up to 230 °C for 20-30 minutes until the crackling puffs.
> Rest 10 minutes, then cut into thick slices.

# Simple Beef Bone Broth
Soup | International | 10 | 480 | 8 | Lunch, Soup
- 2 kg | beef bones
- 3 l | water
- 1 tsp | salt
> Roast the bones at 220 °C for 40 minutes until browned.
> Put them in a large pot with the water, bring to a bare simmer and skim. Simmer gently, partly covered, for 6-8 hours, topping up with water.
> Strain, season with salt and cool. Lift off the fat once cold. Keeps 4 days in the fridge or 3 months frozen.

# Crispy Chicken Thighs
Main | International | 5 | 35 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 8 | chicken thighs, bone-in, skin on
- 1 tsp | salt
- 1 tbsp | butter
> Dry the chicken and salt the skin.
> Start skin side down in a cold heavy pan, then cook over medium heat for 20-25 minutes until the fat has rendered and the skin is deep golden.
> Turn, add the butter and baste; cook 8-10 minutes more, until 75 °C inside.

> Salt the chops and sear in a hot pan 3 minutes per side for pink, holding them fat edge down for a minute to crisp the fat.

# Cuban Black Beans (Frijoles Negros)
Side | Cuban | 10 | 30 | 4 | Dinner, Side dish
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 1 | green bell pepper, chopped
- 4 cloves | garlic, minced
- 1 tsp | cumin
- 1 tsp | dried oregano
- 1 | bay leaf
- 800 g | canned black beans
- 1 tbsp | red wine vinegar
- 1 tsp | sugar
> Soften the onion and pepper in the oil for 8 minutes, add the garlic, cumin and oregano for a minute.
> Add the beans with their liquid and the bay leaf. Simmer 15-20 minutes, mashing a few beans to thicken.
> Stir in the vinegar and sugar, season and serve with rice.

# Dominican Mangú with Pickled Onions
Breakfast | Dominican | 10 | 20 | 4 | Breakfast, Brunch
- 4 | green plantains, peeled and cut in chunks
- 4 tbsp | butter
- 1 | red onion, thinly sliced
- 3 tbsp | apple cider vinegar
- 1 tsp | salt
> Boil the plantains in salted water for 15-20 minutes until very tender.
> Meanwhile soften the onion in a little oil, add the vinegar and a pinch of salt and cook 2 minutes.
> Mash the plantains with the butter and some of the cooking water until smooth. Top with the onions; serve with eggs or fried cheese.

# Trinidadian Curry Chickpeas (Channa)
Main | Trinidadian & Tobagonian | 10 | 25 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 3 cloves | garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp | curry powder
- 1 tsp | ground cumin
- 800 g | canned chickpeas, drained
- 300 ml | water
- 2 tbsp | green seasoning | opt
- 1 | potato, diced
> Fry the onion and garlic in the oil until soft, add the curry powder and cumin with a splash of water and cook 2 minutes.
> Add the chickpeas, potato, water and green seasoning. Simmer 15-20 minutes until the potato is soft and the sauce thick; crush some chickpeas.
> Season and serve with roti or rice.

# Bajan Cou-Cou with Okra
Side | Bajan | 10 | 20 | 4 | Dinner, Side dish
- 8 | okra, thinly sliced
- 750 ml | water
- 200 g | cornmeal
- 2 tbsp | butter
- 1 tsp | salt
> Simmer the okra in the water with the salt for 8 minutes.
> Mix the cornmeal with a little cold water to a paste, then whisk into the pot over low heat.
> Stir with a wooden spoon for 8-10 minutes until thick and pulling from the sides. Beat in the butter, shape in a buttered bowl and turn out. Serve with flying fish or any stewed fish.

# Bahamian Conch-Style Fritters with Shrimp
Snack | Bahamian | 15 | 15 | 4 | Appetizer, Snack
- 250 g | shrimp, chopped
- 150 g | flour
- 1 tsp | baking powder
- 1 | egg
- 120 ml | milk
- 0.5 | onion, finely chopped
- 0.5 | green bell pepper, finely chopped
- 1 | celery stalk, finely chopped
- 0.5 tsp | salt
- 500 ml | vegetable oil, for frying
> Fritters in the Bahamas use conch; shrimp is an easy stand-in.
> Mix the flour, baking powder and salt, then the egg and milk to a thick batter. Fold in the shrimp and vegetables.
> Fry spoonfuls in oil at 175 °C for 3-4 minutes until golden. Drain and serve with a lime and hot sauce dip.

# Guyanese Pepperpot-Style Beef Stew
Main | Guyanese | 15 | 150 | 6 | Dinner, Main dish
- 1 kg | beef chuck, cubed
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 4 cloves | garlic, minced
- 1 | cinnamon stick
- 4 | cloves
- 1 | scotch bonnet pepper, whole
- 2 tbsp | dark brown sugar
- 3 tbsp | cassareep, or molasses
- 2 sprigs | thyme
> Traditional pepperpot uses cassareep (cassava extract); molasses gives a similar dark sweetness.
> Brown the beef in the oil in batches. Add the onion and garlic and cook 5 minutes.
> Add the spices, pepper, sugar, cassareep or molasses, thyme and water to just cover. Simmer covered for 2-2½ hours until very tender and the sauce is dark and glossy. Remove the whole pepper. Best the next day.

# Grenadian Oil Down
Main | Grenadian | 20 | 50 | 6 | Dinner, Main dish
- 1 | breadfruit, peeled and cut in chunks
- 400 ml | coconut milk
- 300 g | salt cod, soaked overnight and flaked
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 3 cloves | garlic, minced
- 2 sprigs | thyme
- 1 tsp | turmeric
- 200 g | callaloo, or spinach
- 1 | carrot, sliced
> Layer the onion, garlic, thyme, fish, breadfruit and carrot in a heavy pot. Add the turmeric to the coconut milk with 250 ml water and pour over.
> Cover and simmer gently 40 minutes without stirring, until the breadfruit is tender and has soaked up most of the liquid.
> Lay the callaloo on top for the last 10 minutes. Serve from the pot.
