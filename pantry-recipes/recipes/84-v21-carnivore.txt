# Tallow-Grilled Ribeye
Main | American | 5 | 15 | 2 | Dinner, Main dish
- 2 | ribeye steaks, about 2.5 cm thick
- 1 tsp | salt
- 1 tbsp | beef tallow, melted
- 0.5 tsp | black pepper
> Heat a grill or griddle pan until very hot. Brush the steak with the melted tallow and salt it well.
> Grill 5 minutes on each side for medium-rare, turning once; a thermometer should read 54 °C in the thickest part.
> Rest 5 minutes on a warm plate, then slice across the grain and season with the pepper.

# Air Fryer Ribeye
Main | American | 5 | 12 | 2 | Dinner, Main dish
- 2 | ribeye steaks, about 2.5 cm thick
- 1 tsp | salt
- 2 tsp | butter, melted
> Preheat the air fryer to 200 °C for 5 minutes. Pat the steak dry, brush with the butter and salt both sides.
> Air fry for 5 minutes, turn and air fry 5 minutes more for medium-rare (54 °C inside). Thinner cuts need a minute or two less.
> Rest 5 minutes before slicing across the grain.

# Butter-Basted Sirloin
Main | American | 5 | 12 | 2 | Dinner, Main dish
- 2 | sirloin steaks, about 2.5 cm thick
- 1 tsp | salt
- 40 g | butter
- 0.5 tsp | black pepper
> Pat the steak dry and salt it on both sides 30 minutes ahead if you have time (or just before cooking).
> Heat a heavy frying pan over high heat until smoking. Cook the steak 3 minutes on the first side without moving it.
> Turn it, add the butter and, as it foams, tilt the pan and spoon the butter over the steak for 3 minutes for medium-rare (54 °C inside), longer if you like it more done.
> Rest the steak 5 minutes, slice across the grain and pour over the pan butter. Finish with the pepper.

# Slow-Roasted Pork Shoulder with Crackling
Main | American | 15 | 300 | 8 | Dinner, Main dish
- 2.5 kg | pork shoulder, skin on and scored
- 1 tbsp | salt
- 250 ml | water
> Dry the skin well and rub the salt into the scores. Leave uncovered in the fridge overnight if you can.
> Roast at 160 °C with the water in the tin for 4½ hours, topping up the water if it dries.
> Turn the oven to 230 °C for 20 to 30 minutes until the skin crackles all over.
> Rest 20 minutes, then pull or slice.

# Instant Pot Pork Belly Bites
Main | American | 10 | 60 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish

- 1.5 tsp | salt
- 250 ml | water
