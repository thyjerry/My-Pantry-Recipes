# Slow Cooker Beef Stew
Main | American | 20 | 480 | 6 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 900 g | beef chuck, cubed
- 2 tbsp | flour
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 3 | carrots, chunked
- 500 g | potatoes, cubed
- 2 stalks | celery, sliced
- 3 cloves | garlic
- 2 tbsp | tomato paste
- 500 ml | beef stock
- 1 tbsp | worcestershire sauce
- 1 tsp | dried thyme
- 2 | bay leaves
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Toss the beef in the flour, salt and pepper. Put everything in the slow cooker and stir.
> Cover and cook on Low for 8 hours or on High for 5 hours, until tender. Slow cookers vary, so check toward the end.
> Discard the bay leaves and season.
