# Honey Roasted Carrots with Thyme
Side | American | 10 | 35 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Side dish
- 700 g | carrots, halved
- 2 tbsp | honey
- 1 tsp | dried thyme
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Heat the oven to 220 °C (425 °F).
> Toss the vegetables with the oil, salt, pepper and any dry spices or herbs and spread in a single layer on a large tray.
> Roast for about 30 minutes, turning once, until tender and browned at the edges.
> Toss with the remaining flavourings and serve.

# Crispy Smashed Potatoes
Side | American | 10 | 35 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Side dish
- 800 g | baby potatoes, boiled until tender
- 3 cloves | garlic
- 1 tbsp | rosemary
- 2 tbsp | parmesan
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Heat the oven to 230 °C (450 °F).
> Lay the boiled potatoes on an oiled tray and press each one flat with the bottom of a glass.
> Brush with oil, scatter with garlic, rosemary and salt and roast for 30 minutes until very crisp.
> Sprinkle with parmesan and serve.

# Lemon Herb Rice Pilaf
Side | International | 10 | 25 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Side dish
- 300 g | long-grain rice
- 1 | onion, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp | butter
- 600 ml | chicken stock
- 1 | lemon
- 1 handful | parsley
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Melt the butter and cook the onion for 4 minutes.
> Stir in the rice for 1 minute, add the stock and salt, bring to the boil, cover and cook on low for 18 minutes.
> Rest 5 minutes, then fluff with lemon zest, juice and parsley.
