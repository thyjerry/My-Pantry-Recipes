# Creamy Cauliflower Soup
Soup | French | 15 | 30 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Soup
- 2 tbsp | butter
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 1 | cauliflower, chopped
- 1 | potato, diced
- 2 cloves | garlic
- 0.25 tsp | nutmeg
- 1 l | vegetable stock
- | salt
- | black pepper
- 100 ml | milk
- 1 tbsp | chives, snipped
> Melt or heat the butter in a large pot and soften the onion gently for 5 minutes.
> Add the vegetables and the garlic and nutmeg and stir for 2 minutes.
> Pour in the vegetable stock, bring to a boil and simmer for 20 minutes until everything is very tender.
> Blend until smooth with a stick blender (or in batches in a blender, venting the lid). Season to taste.
> Reheat gently and stir in the milk, and scatter with chives.
