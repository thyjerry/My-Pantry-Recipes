# Durban Bunny Chow
Main | South African | 20 | 50 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 1 kg | lamb shoulder, cubed
- 2 | onions, chopped
- 3 cloves | garlic
- 1 tbsp | ginger, grated
- 3 tbsp | curry powder
- 1 tsp | garam masala
- 2 | tomatoes, chopped
- 2 | potatoes, cubed
- 10 | curry leaves
- 1 | bread loaf, white, unsliced
- 3 tbsp | vegetable oil
- | salt
> Brown the lamb in the oil and set aside; soften the onions 8 minutes, then add the garlic, ginger, curry leaves and spices.

- | salt

- | salt
