# Thai Massaman Cod Curry
Main | Thai | 15 | 35 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 4 | cod fillets, about 150 g each
- 3 tbsp | massaman curry paste
- 400 ml | coconut milk
- 2 | potatoes, cubed
- 1 | onion, cut into wedges
- 50 g | peanuts, roasted
- 1 tbsp | fish sauce
- 1 tbsp | brown sugar
- 1 | lime, juiced
- 4 | garlic cloves, chopped
- 2 | red chilies, sliced
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
> Cook the garlic and chilies in 2 tbsp oil over medium heat for 5 minutes until soft, then stir in the curry paste for 1 minute.
> Add the coconut milk, potatoes, onion and 300 ml water and simmer 15 minutes so the flavours come together.
> Season the cod with salt, nestle it into the sauce, cover and poach gently 8 to 10 minutes until the fish flakes easily (63 °C / 145 °F).
> Stir in the fish sauce, sugar and lime juice and scatter with peanuts. Taste for salt.
> Serve with jasmine rice.

# Thai Lemongrass Salmon Traybake
Main | Thai | 15 | 17 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 4 | salmon fillets, about 150 g each
- 2 | lemongrass stalks, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp | fish sauce
- 1 tbsp | brown sugar
- 1 tbsp | soy sauce
- 1 handful | coriander
- 4 | garlic cloves, chopped
- 2 | red chilies, sliced
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
> Mix the lemongrass, garlic, fish sauce, sugar and soy sauce with 2 tbsp oil and 1 tsp salt. Coat the salmon and leave to marinate 15 minutes (no longer, or the acid starts to cook it) in the fridge.
> Heat the oven to 200 °C (180 °C fan). Spread on a lined tray in one layer.
> Roast for about 12 minutes, until the fish flakes easily (63 °C / 145 °F).
> Rest 5 minutes. Scatter with coriander and serve with lime wedges.
> Serve with jasmine rice.

# Thai Garlic Pepper Salmon
Main | Thai | 10 | 16 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 4 | salmon fillets, about 150 g each
- 1 tsp | ground white pepper
- 2 tbsp | oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp | fish sauce
- 1 tsp | sugar
- 4 | garlic cloves, chopped
- 2 | red chilies, sliced
- 1 tbsp | vegetable oil
> Pat the salmon dry and season with salt and the white pepper.
> Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat. Cook skin side down 4 minutes, then 2 to 3 minutes on the other side, until the fish flakes easily (63 °C / 145 °F). Rest on a warm plate.
> Lower the heat and, in the same pan, cook the garlic and chilies for 1 minute, then the garlic for 1 minute, then add the oyster sauce, fish sauce, sugar and 4 tbsp water.
> Return the salmon with its resting juices and turn in the sauce. Spoon the glossy garlic sauce over.
> Serve with jasmine rice.

# Chinese Red-Braised Cod Hong Shao
Main | Chinese | 15 | 35 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 4 | cod fillets, about 150 g each
- 4 tbsp | soy sauce (1 tbsp dark if you have it)
- 3 tbsp | Shaoxing wine (or dry sherry)
- 30 g | brown sugar (or rock sugar)
- 2 | star anise
- 1 | cinnamon stick
- 3 | garlic cloves, chopped
- 3 cm | ginger, chopped
- 4 | spring onions, sliced
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
> Cook the garlic, ginger and white parts of the spring onions in 2 tbsp oil over medium heat for 5 minutes until soft, then stir in the star anise and cinnamon for 1 minute.
> Add the soy sauces, wine, sugar and water to just cover and simmer 15 minutes so the flavours come together.
> Season the cod with salt, nestle it into the sauce, cover and poach gently 8 to 10 minutes until the fish flakes easily (63 °C / 145 °F).
> Uncover for the last 15 minutes and boil until the sauce is sticky. Taste for salt.
> Serve with steamed rice.
