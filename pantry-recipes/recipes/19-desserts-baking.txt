# Brownies
Dessert | American | 15 | 25 | 16 | Dessert, Baked goods
- 200 g | chocolate, dark, chopped
- 150 g | butter
- 250 g | sugar
- 3 | eggs
- 1 tsp | vanilla extract
- 100 g | flour
- 30 g | cocoa powder
- 1 pinch | salt
> Heat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan) and line a 20 cm square tin.
> Melt the chocolate and butter together, then whisk in the sugar, eggs and vanilla.
> Fold in the flour, cocoa and salt.
> Bake for 22-25 minutes until just set with a slightly gooey center. Cool before cutting.

# Cinnamon Rolls
Baking | American | 30 | 25 | 12 | Breakfast, Brunch, Baked goods
- 500 g | flour
- 7 g | yeast
- 50 g | sugar
- 250 ml | milk, warm
- 1 | egg
- 120 g | butter, half melted for the dough, half softened for the filling
- 100 g | brown sugar
- 2 tbsp | cinnamon
- 150 g | powdered sugar
> Mix the flour, yeast, sugar, a pinch of salt, milk, egg and the melted half of the butter and knead for 8 minutes. Leave to rise for 1 hour.
> Roll out to 30 x 45 cm, spread with the softened butter and sprinkle with brown sugar and cinnamon. Roll up and cut into 12.
> Arrange in a lined tin, rise 30 minutes, then bake at 180°C (160°C fan) for 22-25 minutes.
> Mix the powdered sugar with 2 tablespoons water and drizzle over.
