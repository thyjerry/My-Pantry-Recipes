# Baja Fish Tacos
Main | Mexican | 20 | 20 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 500 g | white fish fillets
- 1 tsp | chili powder
- 1 tsp | cumin
- 200 g | cabbage, shredded
- 4 tbsp | sour cream
- 1 tbsp | sriracha
- 1 | lime
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 12 | corn tortillas
- | salt
> Season the fish and pan-fry in 2 tbsp oil for 3 minutes per side, then flake.
> Warm the tortillas in a dry pan for 30 seconds per side and keep them wrapped in a towel.
> Fill the tortillas with the hot filling and add the toppings.
> Serve straight away with extra lime or salsa.

# Sheet-Pan Lemon Herb Chicken and Potatoes
Main | International | 15 | 45 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 8 | chicken thighs, bone-in, skin-on
- 800 g | baby potatoes, halved
- 1 | red onion, wedged
- 1 | lemon
- 3 cloves | garlic
- 2 tsp | dried oregano
- 3 tbsp | olive oil
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Heat the oven to 220 °C (425 °F) and line a large sheet pan with parchment.
> Toss the vegetables with half the oil, salt and pepper and spread them out in one layer; roast for 15 minutes first if they are potatoes or root vegetables.
> Toss the protein with the rest of the oil and the seasoning, add it to the pan and roast for about 30 minutes, turning halfway, until cooked through (chicken to 74 °C / 165 °F).
> Finish with any lemon, sauce, cheese or herbs from the list and serve straight from the pan.

# Sheet-Pan Eggs with Peppers and Potatoes

- | salt
- | black pepper
