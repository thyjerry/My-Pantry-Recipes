# Carrot Spice Muffins (by Weight)
Baking | American | 15 | 22 | 12 | Breakfast, Snack, Baked goods
- 250 g | flour
- 10 g | baking powder
- 3 g | salt
- 130 g | sugar
- 2 | eggs
- 220 g | milk
- 90 g | vegetable oil
- 150 g | carrots, grated
- 1 tsp | mixed spice
> Heat the oven to 200 °C (180 °C fan) and line a 12-hole muffin tin. Set a bowl on the scale and weigh as you go.
> Whisk the flour, baking powder, salt and sugar (and any ground almonds, oats or spice).
> Weigh the milk and oil into a jug, then beat in the eggs. Pour onto the dry mix with the fruit, veg, nuts or chips and fold just until no dry flour shows.
> Divide between the cases (about 75 g each) and bake 20 to 22 minutes until a skewer comes out clean.

# Same-Day Crusty White Loaf
Baking | British | 20 | 35 | 12 | Baked goods
- 500 g | bread flour
- 350 g | water, warm
- 7 g | instant yeast
- 10 g | salt
> Mix to a shaggy dough, rest 20 minutes, then knead 8 minutes.
> Rise 1½ hours until doubled. Shape into a round and rise 45 minutes on a floured tray.
> Slash and bake at 230 °C for 30 to 35 minutes, until it sounds hollow underneath.

# Rosemary Focaccia (by Weight)
Baking | Italian | 20 | 25 | 12 | Snack, Baked goods
- 500 g | bread flour
- 400 g | water
- 7 g | instant yeast
- 10 g | salt
- 50 g | olive oil
- 2 sprigs | rosemary
> Mix the flour, water, yeast and salt into a sticky dough. Fold it over itself 4 times, 15 minutes apart.
> Rise 1 hour, then tip into an oiled tin, dimple with oily fingers and rise 45 minutes.
> Top with rosemary and a pinch more salt and bake at 220 °C for 22 to 25 minutes.

# Milk Bread Rolls (Tangzhong)
Baking | Japanese | 20 | 20 | 12 | Baked goods
- 375 g | bread flour
- 200 g | milk
- 1 | egg
- 40 g | sugar
- 30 g | butter, soft
- 5 g | instant yeast
- 5 g | salt
> Cook 25 g of the flour with 125 g of the milk, whisking, until a thick paste; cool.
> Knead it with everything else for 10 minutes until smooth.
> Rise 1 hour, divide into 12 balls (about 55 g each), rise 40 minutes and bake at 180 °C for 18 to 20 minutes.

# Soft Pretzels (by Weight)

- 500 g | bread flour
- 300 g | water, warm
- 7 g | instant yeast
- 10 g | salt
- 30 g | butter, soft
- 60 g | baking soda
- 1 | egg
> Knead the flour, water, yeast, salt and butter 8 minutes and rise 1 hour.
