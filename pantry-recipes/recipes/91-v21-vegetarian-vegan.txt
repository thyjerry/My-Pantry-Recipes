# Thai Yellow Sweet Potato and Spinach Coconut Curry
Main | Thai | 15 | 30 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 2 | sweet potatoes, cubed
- 150 g | spinach
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 3 | garlic cloves
- 2 cm | ginger, grated
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 400 ml | coconut milk
- 250 ml | vegetable stock
- 3 tbsp | yellow curry paste
- 1 tbsp | soy sauce
- 1 | lime
> Heat the oil and fry any seeds, leaves or curry paste for 1 minute, then add the onion and cook 6 minutes until soft.
> Add the garlic, ginger and spices and cook 1 minute.
> Add the firmer vegetables (and lentils), the coconut milk and stock with 1 tsp salt. Simmer 15 to 20 minutes until tender.
> Add the leafy greens, peas or beans for the last 5 minutes. Finish with lime if using and serve with rice.
