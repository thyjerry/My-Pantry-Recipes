# Lemon Herb Roast Chicken Thighs
Main | International | 10 | 40 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 8 | chicken thighs
- 800 g | potatoes, cut into chunks
- 1 | lemon
- 4 clove | garlic
- 3 tbsp | olive oil
- 2 tsp | thyme
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Heat the oven to 210°C (410°F).
> Toss the potatoes, chicken, smashed garlic, oil, thyme, salt and pepper in a roasting tin. Turn the chicken skin side up.
> Squeeze over the lemon and tuck the squeezed halves into the tin.
> Roast for 40 minutes until the chicken is golden and cooked through and the potatoes are crisp.

# Chicken Stir-Fry
Main | Chinese | 15 | 10 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 500 g | chicken breast, sliced
- 2 tbsp | soy sauce
- 1 tbsp | cornstarch
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 1 | bell pepper, sliced
- 1 | broccoli, in small florets
- 1 | carrot, thinly sliced
- 2 clove | garlic
- 1 tbsp | ginger, grated
- 2 tbsp | oyster sauce
- 1 tsp | sesame oil
> Toss the chicken with 1 tablespoon soy sauce and the cornstarch.
> Heat the oil in a wok until very hot. Stir-fry the chicken for 4 minutes and remove.
> Stir-fry the vegetables for 3 minutes, adding a splash of water if needed. Add the garlic and ginger for 30 seconds.
> Return the chicken with the remaining soy sauce, oyster sauce and sesame oil. Toss for 1 minute and serve with rice.

# Chicken Curry
Main | Indian | 15 | 35 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 2 | onions, chopped
- 3 clove | garlic
- 1 tbsp | ginger, grated
- 2 tbsp | curry powder
- 1 tsp | turmeric
- 600 g | chicken thighs, diced
- 400 g | canned tomatoes
- 200 ml | coconut milk
- 1 tsp | garam masala
- | salt
- 2 tbsp | cilantro | opt
> Heat the oil and cook the onions for 10 minutes until golden.
> Add the garlic, ginger, curry powder and turmeric and cook for 1 minute.
> Add the chicken and stir to coat, then the tomatoes. Simmer for 15 minutes.
> Stir in the coconut milk and garam masala and simmer 5 minutes more. Season and top with cilantro. Serve with rice or naan.

# Honey Garlic Chicken
Main | Chinese-American | 10 | 20 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 600 g | chicken thighs
- 4 tbsp | honey
- 3 tbsp | soy sauce
- 4 clove | garlic, crushed
- 1 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 1 tsp | sesame seeds | opt
- 2 | green onions | opt
> Mix the honey, soy sauce and garlic.
> Brown the chicken in the oil for 5 minutes per side.
> Pour in the sauce and simmer for 8 to 10 minutes, turning, until sticky and cooked through.
> Sprinkle with sesame seeds and green onions. Serve with rice and greens.

# Chicken Fajitas
Main | Mexican | 15 | 12 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 500 g | chicken breast, in strips
- 2 | bell peppers, sliced
- 1 | onion, sliced
- 2 tsp | paprika
- 1 tsp | cumin
- 1 tsp | chili powder
- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 1 | lime
- 8 | tortillas
- 100 ml | sour cream | opt
- 1 | avocado | opt
> Toss the chicken with the spices, half the oil and a pinch of salt.
> Cook in a very hot pan for 5 to 6 minutes until cooked, then remove.
> Add the rest of the oil and cook the peppers and onion for 5 minutes until charred at the edges.
> Return the chicken, squeeze over the lime and serve in warm tortillas with sour cream and avocado.

# Chicken Noodle Soup
Soup | American | 15 | 30 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Soup
- 1 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 2 | carrots, sliced
- 2 stalk | celery, sliced
- 2 clove | garlic
- 1.5 l | chicken stock
- 400 g | chicken breast
- 120 g | egg noodles
- 1 tsp | thyme
- 2 tbsp | parsley | opt
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Soften the onion, carrot and celery in the oil for 8 minutes. Add the garlic.
> Pour in the stock, add the thyme and the whole chicken breasts. Simmer for 15 minutes until cooked.
> Lift out the chicken and shred it with two forks.
> Add the noodles and cook for 5 minutes. Return the chicken, season and add parsley.

# Crispy Baked Chicken Tenders
Main | American | 15 | 20 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish
- 600 g | chicken breast, in strips
- 2 | eggs
- 100 g | breadcrumbs
- 40 g | parmesan, grated
- 1 tsp | paprika
- 1 tsp | garlic powder
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- | salt
> Heat the oven to 220°C (425°F) and line a tray with baking paper.
> Beat the eggs. Mix the breadcrumbs, parmesan, paprika, garlic powder and salt.
> Dip the chicken in egg, then the crumbs. Lay on the tray and drizzle with oil.
> Bake for 18 to 20 minutes, turning once, until golden and cooked through.

# Chicken and Rice Skillet
Main | International | 10 | 30 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish
- 500 g | chicken thighs, diced
- 1 tbsp | olive oil
- 1 | onion, chopped
- 1 | bell pepper, diced
- 2 clove | garlic
- 1 tsp | paprika
- 300 g | rice
- 700 ml | chicken stock
- 150 g | peas
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Brown the chicken in the oil and set aside.
> Cook the onion and pepper for 5 minutes. Add the garlic and paprika, then stir in the rice.
> Pour in the stock, return the chicken, cover and simmer gently for 18 minutes.
> Scatter over the peas, cover again for 5 minutes, fluff with a fork and season.

# Chicken Caesar Salad
Salad | American | 15 | 12 | 4 | Lunch, Dinner, Main dish, Salad
- 2 | chicken breasts
- 1 | lettuce
- 2 slice | bread, cubed
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 40 g | parmesan
- 4 tbsp | mayonnaise
- 1 | lemon
- 1 tsp | dijon mustard
- 1 clove | garlic, crushed
- | salt
- | black pepper
> Season the chicken and cook in a little oil for 5 to 6 minutes per side. Rest, then slice.
> Fry the bread cubes in the remaining oil until crisp.
> Whisk the mayonnaise, lemon juice, mustard, garlic and half the parmesan, loosening with water.
> Toss the lettuce with the dressing, then top with chicken, croutons and the rest of the parmesan.

# Chicken Tikka Skewers
Main | Indian | 15 | 15 | 4 | Dinner, Main dish, Appetizer
- 600 g | chicken breast, cubed
- 200 g | yogurt
- 2 tsp | garam masala
- 1 tsp | turmeric
- 1 tsp | chili powder
- 2 clove | garlic
- 1 tbsp | ginger
- 1 | lemon
- | salt
> Mix the yogurt, spices, crushed garlic, grated ginger, lemon juice and salt. Stir in the chicken and marinate for at least 30 minutes.
> Thread onto skewers.
> Grill or cook in a hot griddle pan for 12 to 15 minutes, turning, until charred and cooked through.
