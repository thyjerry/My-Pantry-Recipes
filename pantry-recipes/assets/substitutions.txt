# Pantry Recipes ingredient substitutions. Written for this app (MIT License).
# Format: "## Category", "* Ingredient | alias, alias", "= what to use instead | needs | ratio | effect",
# where needs are ingredient names joined by "+" (all needed); "a / b" means either one.
# ratio is "swap:original" by volume or count (fractions allowed, e.g. 3/4:1), or "to taste".
# effect says how the result changes. "~ note" adds a tip. Diet and allergen flags are worked out
# by the app from the needed ingredients, the same way recipe tags are.

## Dairy & eggs
* Buttermilk
= 1 cup milk + 1 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar; stir and wait 5 minutes | milk + lemon / white vinegar / cider vinegar | 1:1 | Same tang and rise; slightly thinner
= ¾ cup plain yogurt + ¼ cup milk, whisked | yogurt + milk | 1:1 | Thicker and tangier; very tender crumb
= 1 cup kefir | kefir | 1:1 | Almost identical; a little more sour
= 1 cup plant milk + 1 tbsp lemon juice (dairy-free) | soy milk / oat milk / almond milk + lemon / cider vinegar | 1:1 | Dairy-free; soy milk curdles best, others stay thinner
* Heavy cream | whipping cream, double cream
= For cooking: ¾ cup milk + ¼ cup melted butter per cup (won't whip) | milk + butter | 1:1 | Rich enough for sauces; will not whip
= 1 cup half and half for sauces and soups (won't whip) | half and half | 1:1 | Lighter; may split if boiled hard
= 1 cup full-fat coconut cream (dairy-free) | coconut cream | 1:1 | Dairy-free; adds coconut flavour; chilled it whips
= 1 cup evaporated milk in sauces and baking | evaporated milk | 1:1 | Lighter, slightly caramel taste; will not whip
~ None of these whip like real cream except chilled coconut cream.
* Half and half
= ½ cup milk + ½ cup heavy cream per cup | milk + heavy cream | 1:1 | Same result
= ¾ cup milk + 2 tbsp melted butter per cup | milk + butter | 1:1 | Close in sauces and coffee; less body
* Sour cream
= Same amount of plain or Greek yogurt | yogurt | 1:1 | Tangier and lighter; can split if boiled
= Same amount of crème fraîche | creme fraiche | 1:1 | Richer and milder; safe to boil
= Blend 1 cup cottage cheese with 1 tbsp lemon juice | cottage cheese + lemon | 1:1 | Lower fat; good in dips
* Yogurt | greek yogurt, natural yogurt
= Same amount of sour cream | sour cream | 1:1 | Richer, less tart
= Same amount of kefir (thinner) | kefir | 1:1 | Thinner; use in marinades and batters, not as a topping
= Same amount of coconut yogurt (dairy-free) | coconut yogurt | 1:1 | Dairy-free; slight coconut taste
* Creme fraiche
= Same amount of sour cream | sour cream | 1:1 | Tangier; may split if boiled
= Same amount of Greek yogurt (don't boil it) | yogurt | 1:1 | Lighter and sharper; add off the heat
* Milk
= ½ cup evaporated milk + ½ cup water per cup | evaporated milk | 1:1 | Same in baking and sauces
= Same amount of soy, oat or almond milk | soy milk / oat milk / almond milk | 1:1 | Dairy-free; soy is closest in baking, oat in sauces
= ¼ cup half and half + ¾ cup water per cup | half and half | 1:1 | Same result
* Butter
= For baking: ¾ cup oil per 1 cup butter | vegetable oil / coconut oil | 3/4:1 | Moister, denser crumb; no butter flavour; not for creaming
= Same amount of vegan butter or margarine | vegan butter | 1:1 | Dairy-free; bakes almost the same
= Same amount of ghee | ghee | 1:1 | Nuttier; less water, so cookies spread a little more
= For sautéing: olive oil, about ¾ the amount | olive oil | 3/4:1 | Savoury cooking only; fruity flavour
~ Baked goods made with oil come out a little denser and moister.
* Ghee | clarified butter
= Same amount of butter (watch the heat, it burns sooner) | butter | 1:1 | Lower smoke point; milkier taste
= Same amount of vegetable or coconut oil | vegetable oil / coconut oil | 1:1 | Dairy-free; loses the nutty flavour
* Cream cheese
= Same amount of mascarpone | mascarpone | 1:1 | Richer and sweeter; softer set in cheesecake
= Same amount of ricotta blended smooth | ricotta | 1:1 | Lighter, grainier; fine in dips and fillings
= Thick Greek yogurt strained for an hour | yogurt | 1:1 | Tangier and lower fat; softer
= Same amount of vegan cream cheese (dairy-free) | vegan cream cheese | 1:1 | Dairy-free; softer when baked
* Ricotta
= Same amount of cottage cheese, blended or drained | cottage cheese | 1:1 | Saltier and wetter unless drained
= Same amount of mascarpone (richer) | mascarpone | 1:1 | Much richer, smoother
* Mascarpone
= Same amount of cream cheese beaten with a splash of cream | cream cheese + heavy cream | 1:1 | Tangier, firmer
= Same amount of ricotta, blended smooth | ricotta | 1:1 | Lighter, less rich
* Evaporated milk
= 1 cup milk simmered down to about ⅔ cup | milk | 3/2:1 | Same once reduced; takes about 15 minutes
= Same amount of half and half | half and half | 1:1 | Slightly richer, no cooked taste
* Condensed milk | sweetened condensed milk
= 1 cup evaporated milk + ¾ cup sugar, simmered until thick | evaporated milk + sugar | 1:1 | Close; a little thinner
= 1 cup milk + ⅔ cup sugar + 2 tbsp butter, simmered until reduced by half | milk + sugar + butter | 2:1 | Close after long reduction
= Coconut condensed milk or 1 can coconut milk + ½ cup sugar, reduced (dairy-free) | coconut milk + sugar | 1:1 | Dairy-free; coconut flavour
* Egg
= 1 tbsp ground flaxseed + 3 tbsp water, rested 5 minutes (per egg) | flaxseed | 1:1 | Egg-free binder; denser, slightly nutty
= 1 tbsp chia seeds + 3 tbsp water, rested 10 minutes (per egg) | chia seeds | 1:1 | Egg-free binder; visible specks
= ¼ cup applesauce per egg (sweet bakes) | applesauce | 1:1 | Moist and dense; adds sweetness
= ½ ripe banana, mashed, per egg (sweet bakes) | banana | 1:1 | Moist; banana flavour
= 3 tbsp aquafaba per egg | aquafaba / chickpeas | 1:1 | Egg-free; best for light bakes and meringue
= ¼ cup plain yogurt per egg | yogurt | 1:1 | Moist, tender; less lift
~ Egg swaps work best when eggs are a binder, not the star (not for omelettes or meringues).
~ Egg whites: 3 tbsp aquafaba per white whips like meringue. Yolks in custard: 1 whole egg per 2 yolks (firmer, less rich).
* Parmesan | pecorino, grana padano, parmesan cheese
~ Pecorino and grana padano work 1:1 (pecorino is saltier).
= Nutritional yeast, about half the amount (dairy-free) | nutritional yeast | 1/2:1 | Dairy-free; cheesy-savoury but no melt
= Same amount of aged manchego or gouda, finely grated | manchego / gouda | 1:1 | Milder, slightly sweeter
* Mozzarella
= Same amount of provolone | provolone | 1:1 | Sharper; melts well
= Same amount of Monterey Jack | monterey jack | 1:1 | Creamier melt, mild
= Same amount of burrata (torn, add after cooking) | burrata | 1:1 | Creamier; do not bake
= Same amount of vegan cheese (dairy-free) | vegan cheese | 1:1 | Dairy-free; melts less evenly
* Cheddar
= Same amount of Monterey Jack, Colby or Gouda | monterey jack / colby jack / gouda | 1:1 | Milder; melts smoothly
= Same amount of Swiss cheese or Gruyère | swiss cheese / gruyere | 1:1 | Nuttier; stretchier melt
* Feta
= Same amount of goat cheese | goat cheese | 1:1 | Creamier, less salty
= Same amount of queso fresco or cotija | queso fresco / cotija | 1:1 | Milder (queso fresco) or saltier (cotija)
= Halloumi, cubed (firmer, saltier) | halloumi | 1:1 | Squeaky and firm; grill it
* Gruyere
= Same amount of Swiss cheese | swiss cheese | 1:1 | Milder
= Same amount of aged gouda or cheddar | gouda / cheddar | 1:1 | Sharper; less nutty
* Goat cheese
= Same amount of feta | feta | 1:1 | Saltier, crumblier
= Same amount of cream cheese + pinch of salt | cream cheese | 1:1 | Milder, smoother
* Paneer
= Same amount of halloumi | halloumi | 1:1 | Saltier; soak 10 minutes to soften
= Same amount of extra-firm tofu, pressed (vegan) | tofu | 1:1 | Vegan; softer, absorbs sauce
* Queso fresco
= Same amount of feta, rinsed for less salt | feta | 1:1 | Saltier and tangier
= Same amount of ricotta salata or mild crumbly goat cheese | ricotta salata / goat cheese | 1:1 | Similar crumble
* Blue cheese | gorgonzola, stilton, roquefort
= Same amount of feta + a little black pepper | feta | 1:1 | Saltier and milder; no blue tang
* Swiss cheese | emmental
= Same amount of gruyere | gruyere | 1:1 | Nuttier and stronger
= Same amount of jarlsberg or provolone | provolone | 1:1 | Similar melt
* Monterey jack | pepper jack
= Same amount of mild cheddar | cheddar | 1:1 | Sharper; slightly oilier melt
= Same amount of mozzarella | mozzarella | 1:1 | Stretchier, milder
* Provolone
= Same amount of mozzarella | mozzarella | 1:1 | Milder
= Same amount of swiss cheese | swiss cheese | 1:1 | Nuttier
* Halloumi
= Same amount of paneer, fried | paneer | 1:1 | Milder, less salty; also holds shape
= Thick slices of feta, fried briefly (very soft) | feta | 1:1 | Softer and saltier; handle gently
* Burrata
= Same amount of fresh mozzarella with a spoon of cream | mozzarella + heavy cream | 1:1 | Similar; less oozy
* Cottage cheese
= Same amount of ricotta | ricotta | 1:1 | Smoother, sweeter
= Same amount of farmer cheese | farmer cheese | 1:1 | Drier; good in pancakes and fillings
* Kefir
= Same amount of buttermilk | buttermilk | 1:1 | Same tang
= Same amount of yogurt thinned with milk | yogurt + milk | 1:1 | Same in smoothies and bakes
* Labneh
= Greek yogurt, strained overnight | yogurt | 3/2:1 | Same once strained; start with 1½ times the amount
= Same amount of cream cheese + a squeeze of lemon | cream cheese + lemon | 1:1 | Richer, less tangy
* Vegan butter
= Same amount of coconut oil (solid for pastry, melted for cakes) | coconut oil | 1:1 | Dairy-free; can taste of coconut
= ¾ the amount of vegetable oil in cakes and muffins | vegetable oil | 3/4:1 | Moist; not for creaming or pastry

## Baking
* Baking powder
= 1 tsp = ¼ tsp baking soda + ½ tsp cream of tartar | baking soda + cream of tartar | 1:1 | Same lift; mix just before using
= 1 tsp = ¼ tsp baking soda + ½ cup buttermilk or yogurt (reduce other liquid by ½ cup) | baking soda + buttermilk / yogurt | 1:1 | Same lift; tangier, moister
* Baking soda | bicarbonate of soda
= 3 tsp baking powder per 1 tsp baking soda (and leave out salt) | baking powder | 3:1 | Weaker browning; slightly different taste
~ Use soda swaps only in recipes with an acid like buttermilk, yogurt or lemon.
* Self raising flour | self-rising flour
= 1 cup flour + 1½ tsp baking powder + ¼ tsp salt | flour + baking powder | 1:1 | Same result
* Flour | all-purpose flour, plain flour
= Whole wheat flour for up to half the flour (denser) | whole wheat flour | 1:1 | Denser and nuttier; add a spoon more liquid
= 1 cup gluten-free blend or rice flour (cakes, not bread) | gluten free flour / rice flour | 1:1 | Gluten-free; more crumbly; add ¼ tsp xanthan gum per cup if the blend has none
= ¾ cup almond flour per cup in cookies and quick breads | almond flour | 3/4:1 | Gluten-free; moist, flat and nutty; not for yeast bread
= Same amount of oat flour in muffins and pancakes | oat flour | 1:1 | Softer, nuttier; gluten-free only with certified oats
~ Bread flour: all-purpose works 1:1 with a little less chew (add 1 tsp vital wheat gluten per cup if you have it).
* Cake flour
= ¾ cup + 2 tbsp flour + 2 tbsp cornstarch per cup, sifted | flour + cornstarch | 1:1 | Same tender crumb
* Gluten free flour | gluten free flour blend
= Mix 2 parts rice flour + 2/3 part potato starch + 1/3 part tapioca flour | rice flour + potato starch / cornstarch + tapioca flour | 1:1 | Gluten-free blend; add ½ tsp xanthan gum per cup for cakes
= Plain flour (not gluten-free) | flour | 1:1 | Contains gluten; usually needs a little less liquid
* Almond flour | ground almonds, almond meal
= Same weight of ground hazelnuts or cashews | hazelnuts / cashews | 1:1 | Different nut flavour; same moist crumb
= Sunflower seed flour (nut-free; may turn green with baking soda) | sunflower seeds | 1:1 | Nut-free; add a little lemon juice to stop greening
= ½ the amount of plain flour in cookies | flour | 1/2:1 | Not gluten-free; drier, less nutty
* Oat flour
= Rolled oats blended fine | oats | 5/4:1 | Same; 1¼ cups oats make about 1 cup flour
= Same amount of plain flour | flour | 1:1 | Lighter, less nutty; contains gluten
* Xanthan gum
= 2 tsp psyllium husk powder per 1 tsp | psyllium husk | 2:1 | Similar binding; slightly darker crumb
= 1 tbsp chia or flaxseed, ground, per 1 tsp | chia seeds / flaxseed | 3:1 | Binds well; visible specks
* Cornstarch | cornflour
= 2 tbsp flour per 1 tbsp cornstarch for thickening | flour | 2:1 | Cloudier sauce; cook 2 minutes longer; contains gluten
= Same amount of rice flour, potato starch or arrowroot | rice flour / potato starch / arrowroot | 1:1 | Gluten-free; arrowroot stays glossy, add at the end
* Potato starch
= Same amount of cornstarch | cornstarch | 1:1 | Slightly less crisp coating
= Same amount of tapioca flour | tapioca flour | 1:1 | Chewier; good for crisp frying
* Tapioca flour | tapioca starch
= Same amount of cornstarch or potato starch | cornstarch / potato starch | 1:1 | Less chewy (cheese bread will be flatter)
* Semolina
= Same amount of fine polenta | cornmeal | 1:1 | Gluten-free; grainier and more yellow
= Same amount of plain flour in pasta dough | flour | 1:1 | Softer pasta
* Cornmeal | polenta
= Same amount of fine semolina | semolina | 1:1 | Contains gluten; smoother
= Same amount of masa harina in breads | masa harina | 1:1 | Stronger corn flavour; finer
* Masa harina
= Same amount of fine cornmeal + a little extra water | cornmeal | 1:1 | Grittier; tortillas crack more easily
* Masarepa | precooked cornmeal
= Same amount of masa harina (arepas a little denser) | masa harina | 1:1 | Denser, nixtamal flavour
* Brown sugar
= 1 cup sugar + 1 tbsp molasses | sugar + molasses | 1:1 | Same; use 2 tbsp molasses for dark brown
= 1 cup sugar + 2 tbsp maple syrup (slightly lighter) | sugar + maple syrup | 1:1 | Lighter caramel note
= Same amount of coconut sugar | coconut sugar | 1:1 | Less moist; deeper toffee flavour
* Sugar | white sugar, caster sugar
= Same amount of brown sugar (adds caramel notes) | brown sugar | 1:1 | Moister, chewier, darker
= ¾ cup honey per cup; cut liquid by 3 tbsp and bake 25°F/15°C cooler | honey | 3/4:1 | Moist and browns faster; not vegan
= ¾ cup maple syrup per cup; cut liquid by 3 tbsp | maple syrup | 3/4:1 | Moist, maple taste
= Same amount of coconut sugar | coconut sugar | 1:1 | Darker, less sweet
* Powdered sugar | icing sugar, confectioners sugar
= Blend 1 cup sugar + 1 tbsp cornstarch until fine | sugar + cornstarch | 1:1 | Same once very fine
* Honey
= Same amount of maple syrup | maple syrup | 1:1 | Vegan; thinner, maple flavour
= Same amount of agave syrup | agave syrup | 1:1 | Vegan; sweeter, neutral
= Same amount of golden syrup | golden syrup | 1:1 | Vegan; buttery taste
= 1¼ cups sugar + ¼ cup water per cup | sugar | 5/4:1 | Less flavour; crisper bakes
* Maple syrup
= Same amount of honey | honey | 1:1 | Thicker, floral; not vegan
= Same amount of agave syrup | agave syrup | 1:1 | Neutral sweetness
= 1 cup brown sugar + ¼ cup water, simmered (per cup) | brown sugar | 1:1 | Similar on pancakes; no maple flavour
* Agave syrup | agave
= Same amount of maple syrup | maple syrup | 1:1 | Maple flavour; slightly less sweet
= Same amount of honey | honey | 1:1 | Thicker; not vegan
* Molasses
= Same amount of dark corn syrup, honey or maple syrup | honey / maple syrup / corn syrup | 1:1 | Less bitter and dark
= ¾ cup brown sugar + ¼ cup water per cup | brown sugar | 1:1 | Milder
* Corn syrup
= Same amount of golden syrup | golden syrup | 1:1 | Same texture; buttery
= Same amount of honey or agave (browns faster) | honey / agave syrup | 1:1 | Browns faster; adds flavour
* Golden syrup
= Same amount of honey | honey | 1:1 | Floral; not vegan
= Same amount of light corn syrup | corn syrup | 1:1 | Less flavour
* Vanilla extract
= 1 vanilla bean's seeds per 1 tbsp extract | vanilla bean | 1:1 | Stronger, specks
= Same amount of maple syrup (for flavour) | maple syrup | 1:1 | Maple note instead of vanilla
= Half the amount of almond extract (stronger) | almond extract | 1/2:1 | Almond flavour; contains nut flavouring
* Chocolate | dark chocolate
= 1 oz (30 g) = 3 tbsp cocoa powder + 1 tbsp butter or oil | cocoa powder + butter / vegetable oil / coconut oil | 1:1 | Same in bakes; not for melting or dipping
= Same amount of raisins, dried cranberries or chopped nuts (in cookies) | raisins / dried cranberries / walnuts | 1:1 | No chocolate; chewier or crunchier
* White chocolate
= Same amount of milk chocolate | milk chocolate | 1:1 | More chocolatey, less sweet
* Cocoa powder
= 1 oz (30 g) unsweetened chocolate per 3 tbsp cocoa; reduce fat by 1 tbsp | chocolate | 1:1 | Fudgier
* Cream of tartar
= For egg whites: ½ tsp lemon juice or vinegar per ¼ tsp | lemon / white vinegar | 2:1 | Same stability
* Yeast | dried yeast, instant yeast
~ No real swap for bread. For quick breads use 2 tsp baking powder per cup of flour instead.
= Quick bread instead: 2 tsp baking powder per cup flour | baking powder | 1:1 | Becomes a soda-bread style loaf; no chew
* Gelatin
= Agar powder, about ⅓ the amount, boiled 2 minutes to set (vegan) | agar | 1/3:1 | Vegan; firmer, sets at room temperature
~ Agar must boil; it won't set with raw pineapple or kiwi either way.
* Breadcrumbs | panko
= Same amount of crushed crackers | cracker | 1:1 | Saltier, finer
= Same amount of rolled oats, pulsed | oats | 1:1 | Chewier; good binder in meatballs

= Ground almonds (gluten-free) | almond flour / almonds | 1:1 | Gluten-free; browns faster
* Graham cracker | digestive biscuit
= Same amount of crushed cookies or vanilla wafers | cookie / vanilla wafer | 1:1 | Sweeter crust
= Same amount of gingersnaps | gingersnap cookie | 1:1 | Spiced crust
* Ladyfinger | ladyfingers, savoiardi
= Slices of sponge cake, lightly toasted | sponge cake | 1:1 | Softer; soak more briefly
* Pie crust | shortcrust pastry
= Press-in crust: 1½ cups crushed cookies + 5 tbsp melted butter | cookie / graham cracker + butter | 1:1 | Crunchy and sweet; no blind baking needed
= Puff pastry (flakier) | puff pastry | 1:1 | Flakier and puffier
* Puff pastry
= Phyllo, brushed with melted butter, 4-5 layers | phyllo dough + butter | 1:1 | Crisper, shatters; less rise
= Pie crust (less flaky) | pie crust | 1:1 | Denser, short texture
* Phyllo dough | filo
= Puff pastry, rolled thin | puff pastry | 1:1 | Puffier and richer
* Shredded coconut | desiccated coconut
= Fresh coconut, grated and dried in a low oven | coconut | 1:1 | Same once dried
= Same amount of finely chopped almonds | almonds | 1:1 | No coconut flavour; adds tree nuts
* Marshmallows | marshmallow
= Same amount of marshmallow fluff | marshmallow fluff | 1:1 | Softer, sticks better
* Sprinkles | chocolate sprinkles
= Finely grated chocolate | chocolate | 1:1 | Less colourful; melts faster
* Jam | raspberry jam, strawberry jam, preserves
= Same amount of any fruit jam or marmalade | marmalade | 1:1 | Different fruit flavour
= Quick chia jam: 1 cup mashed berries + 2 tbsp chia + 1 tbsp maple syrup | chia seeds + berries | 1:1 | Less sweet, softer set
* Chocolate hazelnut spread | hazelnut spread
= Melted chocolate + a spoon of hazelnut or peanut butter | chocolate + peanut butter | 1:1 | Close; firms when cold

## Sauces & seasonings
